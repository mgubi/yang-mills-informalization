\section{The continuum limit}\label{sec:continuum}

We first remove the lattice spacing at each fixed volume, then let the volume increase along one refinement. Throughout, the Wilson family and insertions are those of Section~\ref{sec:renormalization}; they are fixed before any volume sequence is prescribed. Constants may depend on the fixed group, its realization, the penalty and the construction choices of Appendix~\ref{sec:constants}. Additional dependence is stated below.

For positions in~$(\R^4)^n$, set
\begin{align*}
\Conf{n}&=\{x:x_i\ne x_j\text{ for }i\ne j\},&
\Gen{n}&=\{x:x_i^a\ne x_j^a\text{ for }i\ne j,\ a=1,\ldots,4\},\\
\NoHit{L}{n}&=\{x:x_i-x_j\notin L\Z^4\text{ for }i\ne j\}\,.
\end{align*}
The last domain excludes collisions on a torus of physical side~$L$. A separated test is a smooth function on~$(\R^4)^n$ with compact closed support in~$\Conf{n}$. It is $p$-admissible if its support lies in~$((-L_p/2,L_p/2)^4)^n$. Write $\sepinf(x)=\min_{i\ne j}|x_i-x_j|_\infty$, with value~$+\infty$ for $n\le1$.

For a finite list~$\boldsymbol l$ of slot--axis pairs, define
\begin{equation*}
\Delta_{\boldsymbol l}=\prod_{(i,a)\in\boldsymbol l}a_N^{-1}(\tau_{i,a}-1),\qquad
\partial_{\boldsymbol l}=\prod_{(i,a)\in\boldsymbol l}\partial_{x_i^a}\,.
\end{equation*}
Here $\tau_{i,a}$ shifts slot~$i$ forward by one lattice link. These differences measure derivatives in physical units. Torus separation is the minimum, over distinct slots, of $\min_{z\in L_p\Z^4}|\hat y_i-\hat y_j+z|_\infty$.

The local estimates give bounds independent of the volume. They control compactness for every fixed list of fields and derivatives.
\begin{proposition}[Uniform difference bounds]\label{prop:UB}
For every arity~$n$, token list~$v$, finite difference list~$\boldsymbol l$ and $r>0$, there are $B(n,v,\boldsymbol l,r)\ge0$ and $N_0(n,v,\boldsymbol l,r)$ such that, for every $N\ge N_0$, every~$p$ and every lattice tuple of torus separation at least~$r$,
\begin{equation*}
|\Delta_{\boldsymbol l}\latmoment_{N,p}(v;y)|\le B\,.
\end{equation*}
\end{proposition}
The proof is in Section~\ref{sec:partII-E}, II.E.2.2--E.2.4. Marked transfers control insertion coefficients and remainders; the centered chessboard estimate then bounds correlations. An intermediate scale below the prescribed separation also permits finite differences. The chessboard constant in II.E.2.3 precedes that scale, although its cutoff threshold may depend on it. UB's bound and cutoff threshold are independent of~$p$.

Analytic tuning to the common endpoint gives convergence along the full ultraviolet sequence. At a fixed volume, the limiting kernel is smooth even where two positions share some coordinates.
\begin{theorem}[Fixed-volume limit]\label{prop:UV}\label{thm:UV}
For every $p,n,v$, there is $f_{p,v}\in C^\infty(\NoHit{L_p}{n})$ such that, for every finite~$\boldsymbol l$ and every $p$-admissible separated test~$f$,
\begin{equation*}
\lim_{N\to\infty}a_N^{4n}\sum_y f(\hat y)\Delta_{\boldsymbol l}\latmoment_{N,p}(v;y)
=\int f(x)\partial_{\boldsymbol l}f_{p,v}(x)\,dx\,.
\end{equation*}
\end{theorem}
The proof is in Section~\ref{sec:partII-E}, II.E.3.1--E.3.2. At a fixed intermediate level, the normalized correlations are Cauchy as the cutoff increases. On common lattice configurations, comparison with the original correlations has an error tending to zero exponentially in~$B_m$. First choose~$m$, then the cutoffs. UB extends the resulting convergence from a dense set and identifies its derivatives. The proof also gives convergence at moving interpolated points on the periodic domain and integrability of the displayed integrand. Its thresholds may depend on~$p$.

The following definition keeps the order of limits explicit. Write $C_p^\infty(v;f)$ for a fixed-volume smeared limit.
\begin{definition}[Ordered convergence]\label{def:D09}
A Wilson family converges to~$K$ along~$\mathsf v$ if, for every $n,v$ and separated test~$f$, the test is eventually $\mathsf v(k)$-admissible and there are numbers $C_p^\infty(v;f)$ with
\begin{align*}
\latsmear_{N,p}(v;f)&\longrightarrow C_p^\infty(v;f)\quad(N\to\infty)
&&\text{at every admissible }p,\\
C_{\mathsf v(k)}^\infty(v;f)&\longrightarrow\int f(x)K_n(v;x)\,dx\,.
\end{align*}
Values at inadmissible volumes are unrestricted.
\end{definition}

We specify the Euclidean conditions for the multilinear table~$S$ to be constructed. Units, conjugation and the signed permutation action are inherited from the polynomial labels.
\begin{definition}[E1--E3. Euclidean symmetries]\label{def:E1-E3}
E1 is simultaneous permutation symmetry, $S_0=1$ and deletion of a unit slot. E2 is
\begin{equation*}
S_n(hc;hx)=S_n(c;x)\quad(h\in\hypercubic),\qquad
S_n(\bar c;x)=\overline{S_n(c;x)}\,.
\end{equation*}
E3 is translation invariance.
\end{definition}

A word is a finite list of labels at separated positions; it is positive-side for axis~$a$ when all its positions have positive $a$th coordinate. The product of words concatenates their lists, so reflection positivity tests a correlation with one reflected group of insertions.
\begin{definition}[E4--E5. Reflection positivity]\label{def:E4-E5}
E4 requires, for every axis~$a$, positive-side separated words~$w_i$ and complex coefficients~$z_i$,
\begin{equation*}
\sum_{i,j}\bar z_i z_jS(\theta_aw_i^*w_j)\ge0\,.
\end{equation*}
The star reverses the word and applies conjugation to its labels; $\theta_a$ reflects positions and acts on labels. E5 requires the same positivity for finite sums of words smeared against joint smooth compactly supported tests on positive-side separated configurations, and absolute integrability of every mixed pairing.
\end{definition}

For a fixed label list, $D_x^k$ denotes the $k$th Fr\'echet derivative in the positions, with the product Euclidean norm. The next bound controls approach to collision sets uniformly over translations and over configurations with large diameter.
\begin{definition}[E6--E7. Euclidean regularity]\label{def:E6-E7}
E6 is smoothness on~$\Conf{n}$. E7 requires, for each $n,d_i,c_i,k$, constants $C(n,d,c,k)\ge0$ and an integer $A(n,d,c,k)\ge0$ such that
\begin{equation*}
\|D_x^kS_n(c;x)\|\le Cr^{-A}
\qquad(0<r\le1,\ \sepinf(x)\ge r)\,.
\end{equation*}
\end{definition}

One volume refinement will serve every field and all subsequent constructions.
\nopagebreak
\begin{theorem}[Infinite-volume limit]\label{prop:IV}\label{thm:IV}
For every strictly increasing $\mathsf p:\N\to\N$, there are a strictly increasing~$\mathsf q$ and a multilinear table~$S$ such that the Wilson family converges to~$S$ along $\mathsf v=\mathsf p\circ\mathsf q$ in the sense of Definition~\ref{def:D09}:
\begin{equation*}
\lim_{k\to\infty}\lim_{N\to\infty}\latsmear_{N,\mathsf v(k)}(v;f)
=\int f(x)S_n(v;x)\,dx\,.
\end{equation*}
The table satisfies E1--E7.
\end{theorem}
The proof is in Section~\ref{sec:partII-F}, II.F.1--F.3. The extraction first gives volume limits on~$\Gen{n}$; the difference bounds extend them smoothly to~$\Conf{n}$. Uniqueness identifies each extracted fixed-volume kernel with~$f_{p,v}$, retaining UV's full inner limit at every admissible volume. Reflection positivity passes from Wilson expectations to finite sums and then to joint smeared words. Fix this refinement and its table~$S$ from now on.

Write $v(x)=(v_1(x_1),\ldots,v_n(x_n))$ for a spectator word. Two polynomial labels represent the same observable when no separated correlation distinguishes them.
\begin{definition}[Observable fields]\label{def:D10}
Set
\begin{align*}
\nullspace{d}(S)&=\bigl\{c\in V_d:S_{n+1}(c(x_0),v(x))=0\\
&\hspace{7em}\text{for every }n,v\text{ and }x\in\Conf{n+1}\bigr\},\\
F_d&=V_d/\nullspace{d}(S),\qquad \overline{[c]}=[\bar c],\\
\iota_d[c](v;x)&=S_{n+1}(c(x_0),v(x)),\qquad
\mathcal L=\operatorname{span}\bigcup_d\iota_dF_d\,.
\end{align*}
The maps~$\iota_d$ give the responses to all spectators. Choose conjugation-invariant quotient norms.
\end{definition}
Permutation symmetry makes correlations independent of representatives in every slot. The unit field is $\unitfield{d}=[a_{\reflevel{d}}^d\mathbf1]$, with the normalization of Definition~\ref{def:D08}.

To state the dimension of~$F_d$, introduce the free comparison space.
\begin{definition}[Free Maxwell comparison]\label{def:D11}
For $\dimG$ independent colours, take
\begin{equation*}
\mathbb E A_a^\alpha(x)A_b^\beta(y)
=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta_{ab}\Gamma(x-y),\qquad
\Gamma(x)=\frac1{4\pi^2|x|^2},\qquad F_{ab}=\partial_aA_b-\partial_bA_a\,.
\end{equation*}
Let $\maxwellspace{d}$ consist of $\operatorname{Ad}(G)$-invariant Wick polynomials in curvature jets of weight at most~$d$, modulo antisymmetry, the linear Bianchi identities and $\sum_a\partial_aF_{ab}=0$. Their separated Wick correlations are~$\maxwellcorr$.
\end{definition}

The quotient fields admit inclusions and differentiation that preserve their correlations, and have the Maxwell dimensions.
\begin{proposition}[Field calculus]\label{prop:FC}
For all $d\le e$, there are injective compatible inclusions $\jmath_{d,e}:F_d\to F_e$ and linear maps $\partial_a:F_d\to F_{d+1}$, with
\begin{gather*}
\dim F_d=\dim\maxwellspace{d},\qquad \mathcal L=\bigcup_d\iota_dF_d,\\
\partial_{x_i^a}S_n(A;x)=S_n(A_1,\ldots,\partial_aA_i,\ldots,A_n;x)\,.
\end{gather*}
\end{proposition}
The proof is in Section~\ref{sec:partII-F}, II.F.2. It constructs the corrected coefficient maps on the refinement already chosen for IV, preserves units and correlations, and proves compatibility with conjugation and the discrete symmetries. Every quotient field has a token representative.
