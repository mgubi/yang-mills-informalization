\section{Constants and order of choice}\label{sec:constants}

Table~\ref{tab:constant-choices} gives the choices for the construction. At each fixed stage the requirements in Sections~\ref{sec:partII-B}--\ref{sec:partII-E}, \ref{sec:partII-G}.2, \ref{sec:partII-I}.1 and~\ref{sec:partII-J} impose finitely many lower bounds, depending on earlier data. Costs determining a ratio precede that ratio; subsequent entry levels can be increased. The displayed inequalities are properties of these choices.

1 & \raggedright Gauge normalization;\newline $\blockpenalty>0$ & Fix~$G$, its realization, probability Haar measure and metric, then~$\dimG,\casimir,b_0,\slope$ as in Definition~\ref{def:D01}; $\slope>0$. All later choices use these data.\\[3pt]
2 & \raggedright Frame and predictor cutoffs; axial factor & Depend on~$G$. Choose the cutoffs small enough and~$a_{\rm ax}\ge16$ so that the branches are valid on every patch (Section~\ref{sec:partII-B}.1).\\[3pt]
3 & \raggedright Homogeneous bases, norms, sections, stencils & Depend on~$G,d$. Impose Taylor matching, reality and~$\hypercubic$ covariance (Section~\ref{sec:partII-A}.1 and Definitions~\ref{def:D07}--\ref{def:D08}).\\[3pt]
4 & \raggedright Stage parameters & Depend on rows 1--3 and~$j$. Choose $\mathfrak q_j\ge6$, $\varpi_j\ge2$, $\sigma_j=100$, $w_j^{\rm path},A_j^{\rm path},R_j^{\rm rel}>0$, $C_j\ge1$, $\omega_j^{\rm RG}\ge0$, and clipping constants. Require $R_j^{\rm rel}w_j^{\rm path}\ge12$; the drift exceeds the one-loop residual. Orders and powers tend to infinity and, along levels, are nondecreasing, as are support exponents. All ratio costs are now fixed.\\[3pt]
5 & \raggedright Odd ratios $\ell_j^{\rm st}$ & Depend on rows 1--4; choose $\ell_j^{\rm st}\ge3$ nondecreasing and above every ratio threshold. At $j=J_{m+1}$ and $\ell_m=\ell_j^{\rm st}$ require
\begin{gather*}
\frac{\omega_j^{\rm RG}}{\log\ell_m}\le\min(\slope/4,j^{-2})\,,\\
\frac{\log(1+A_j^{\rm path}+(w_j^{\rm path})^{-1}+C_j+R_j^{\rm rel}+\sigma_j)}{\log\ell_m}\le j^{-2}\,.
\end{gather*}
These bounds control the drift and logarithmic stage costs relative to~$\log\ell_m$.\\[3pt]
6 & \raggedright Exponents and coupling entry bounds & Depend on rows 1--5. Choose positive antitone~$\theta_j$ and bounds~$\beta_j^{\rm ent}$ meeting the finite maximum of the remaining stage requirements, including those of Sections~\ref{sec:partII-G}.2, \ref{sec:partII-I}.1 and~\ref{sec:partII-J}. Entry bounds may be raised.\\[3pt]
7 & \raggedright $\bar\beta$, entry levels $m_j^{\rm ent}$, minimum periods $n_j^{\min}$ & Depend on rows 1--6. Require~$\bar\beta>1$, coupling and level entry conditions, and~$t_m\le1$. Delaying entry preserves earlier requirements.\\[3pt]
8 & \raggedright $J_m,\ell_m,D_m$, $a_m,L_p$, $\reflevel{d}$ & Depend on rows 1--7. Set $D_0=1$, $D_{m+1}=\ell_mD_m$, $a_m=D_m^{-1}$, $s_m=\log D_m$, with $\ell_m=\ell^{\rm st}_{J_{m+1}}$ and $4\le J_m\le J_{m+1}\le J_m+1$, $J_m\to\infty$. Require $0<\theta_{J_m}\le J_m^{-1}$, $\log\ell_m\to\infty$, $\log\ell_m/s_m\to0$. Choose strictly increasing~$L_p$ with~$4\mid L_p$ and every positive integer dividing infinitely many~$L_p$. Require $L_pD_m\ge100(B_m+1)$ and $L_pD_{m+1}\ge100((J_{m+1}+3)\ell_m+1)$. Choose $J_{\reflevel{d}}\ge d+3$, with $\mathfrak q_{J_m}\ge d+1$, $\varpi_{J_m}\ge d$ for $m\ge\reflevel{d}$.\\[3pt]
9 & \raggedright Derived level scales & No new choices. Set
\begin{gather*}
\refbeta_m=\bar\beta+\slope s_m>1,\qquad
\cutoffsize_m=\exp((\log\log\refbeta_m)^2)\,,\\
t_m=\refbeta_m^{-1/2}\cutoffsize_m,\qquad
B_m=\lceil(\log\refbeta_m)^2\rceil\,,\\
\epsilon_m^{\rm reg}=\refbeta_m^{-11/10},\qquad
\epsilon_m^{\rm for}=e^{-(\cutoffsize_m)^{1/5}}\,,\\
\Lambda_m^{\rm loop}=2(\cutoffsize_m)^{\theta_{J_m}},\qquad
\mathfrak B_m=[\refbeta_m/2,2\refbeta_m]\,.
\end{gather*} These fix the activity bounds.\\[3pt]
10 & \raggedright Actions, classes, recursion, trajectories, couplings; width~$w$ & Depend on rows 1--9. Choose the path actions and admissible subsets, the specified recursion and its trajectories for every finite~$N,p$, all with the common level-zero endpoint. The width~$w>0$ is independent of~$N,p$ (Theorem~\ref{prop:MT}, Section~\ref{sec:partII-E}.1).\\[3pt]
11 & \raggedright Marked transfers, terminal maps, insertions & Depend on rows 1--10; impose reference normalization (Definition~\ref{def:D08}). Fix them before any prescribed volume sequence.\\[3pt]
12 & \raggedright $\mathsf p$, then $\mathsf q,S,F_d,\partial_a,\mathcal L$ & Prescribe arbitrary increasing~$\mathsf p$ first. Theorem~\ref{prop:IV} and Proposition~\ref{prop:FC} give the refinement and continuum fields. Theorem~\ref{prop:UV} retains the full inner limit.\\[3pt]
13 & \raggedright Comparison radii, frames, $J$, charges, $c_\star,R,T$ & Depend on row 12 and the fixed lattice family. Use Proposition~\ref{prop:MC}, then Theorem~\ref{prop:ST} and Proposition~\ref{prop:SC}. Refine only comparison radii.\\[3pt]
14 & \raggedright Slab operators, $\phases,\nu,S^\xi$, then~$\xi_*$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:PH} selects~$\xi_*$ solely for a one-dimensional OS ground space.\\[3pt]
15 & \raggedright Every-phase theories and continuations; $\Theta,\tracespectrum$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:RC} reconstructs the theories; Theorem~\ref{prop:TR} centers the trace in the already selected phase and supplies its spectral measure.\\[3pt]
16 & \raggedright OPE prescription; contact projections, distributions, bases, scales & Depend on rows 12--14 and the fixed family. Theorems~\ref{prop:OP} and~\ref{prop:CT} use one underlying prescription, with separate contact functionals and clearance.\\[3pt]
17 & \raggedright Estimate constants and thresholds & Depend only on the fixed data preceding them in each statement. They have no later position, volume or state dependence wherever uniformity is asserted.\\
