\section{Constants and order of choice}\label{sec:constants}

Table~\ref{tab:constant-choices} gives the choices for the construction. At each fixed stage the requirements in Sections~\ref{sec:partII-B}--\ref{sec:partII-E}, \ref{sec:partII-G.2}, \ref{sec:partII-I.1} and~\ref{sec:partII-J} impose finitely many lower bounds on a ratio or an entry level, depending only on earlier data. Costs determining a ratio precede that ratio; increasing an entry level preserves earlier requirements. The displayed inequalities are properties of these choices. They do not define a class of constructions for which the conclusions are asserted.

1 & \raggedright Gauge normalization;\newline $\blockpenalty>0$ & Fix~$G$, its realization, probability Haar measure and metric, then~$\dimG,\casimir,b_0,\slope$ as in Definition~\ref{def:D01}; $\slope>0$. All later choices use these data.\\[3pt]
2 & \raggedright Frame and predictor cutoffs; axial factor & Depend on~$G$. The frame and predictor cutoffs select the unique-minimizer branches of the block kernel. On a radius-$R$ patch, the total link chord in an eligible axial frame is at most $a_{\rm ax}(2R+1)$ times the total plaquette chord, with $a_{\rm ax}\ge16$. Choose the cutoffs small enough for branch validity on every patch (Lemma~\ref{lem:B1}).\\[3pt]
3 & \raggedright Homogeneous bases, norms, sections, stencils & Depend on~$G,d$. Impose Taylor matching, reality and~$\hypercubic$ covariance (Lemma~\ref{lem:A1} and Definitions~\ref{def:D07}--\ref{def:D08}).\\[3pt]
4 & \raggedright Stage parameters & Depend on rows 1--3 and~$j$. Choose derivative orders~$\mathfrak q_j\ge6$, spatial powers~$\varpi_j\ge2$ and support exponents~$\sigma_j=100$. In Section~\ref{sec:partII-C}, \ref{lem:C1}, $w_j^{\rm path}>0$ multiplies path length in the exponential weight, and~$A_j^{\rm path}>0$ bounds the weighted row, cubic and quartic sums. The constant~$C_j\ge1$ bounds marked coefficient transport (\ref{lem:E2.2}). Let $\gamma_j>0$ be half a uniform positive lower bound for the free effective plaquette forms in Lemma~\ref{lem:B2}, chosen for the top form and both possible incoming stages. Choose clipping coefficients with $0<16c_{{\rm lin},j}<c_{{\rm dir},j}\le\min(\gamma_j,1)$; the remaining free form then has the positive row factor used in Definition~\ref{def:D23}. Its health cutoff relaxes with distance through $e^{\operatorname{dist}/R_j^{\rm rel}}$, where $R_j^{\rm rel}>0$ and $R_j^{\rm rel}w_j^{\rm path}\ge12$. Let $d_j\ge0$ be the block-ratio-independent one-loop bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:C7}. Fix the reset half-width $r_{\rm res}=1$, then choose $\omega_j^{\rm RG}\ge d_j+1$ for Lemma~\ref{lem:C8}. Orders and powers tend to infinity and, along levels, are nondecreasing, as are support exponents. All ratio costs are now fixed.\\[3pt]
5 & \raggedright Odd ratios $\ell_j^{\rm st}$ & Depend on rows 1--4; choose $\ell_j^{\rm st}\ge3$ nondecreasing and above every ratio threshold. At $j=J_{m+1}$ and $\ell_m=\ell_j^{\rm st}$ require
\begin{gather*}
\frac{\omega_j^{\rm RG}}{\log\ell_m}\le\min(\slope/4,j^{-2})\,,\\
\frac{\log(1+A_j^{\rm path}+(w_j^{\rm path})^{-1}+C_j+R_j^{\rm rel}+\sigma_j)}{\log\ell_m}\le j^{-2}\,.
\end{gather*}
These bounds control the drift and logarithmic stage costs relative to~$\log\ell_m$.\\[3pt]
6 & \raggedright Exponents and coupling entry bounds & Depend on rows 1--5. The positive antitone exponents~$\theta_j$ control the quadratic loop bound in row~9. The bounds~$\beta_j^{\rm ent}$ are lower bounds on the reference coupling when stage~$j$ is used. Choose them to meet the finite maximum of the remaining stage requirements, including those of Sections~\ref{sec:partII-G.2}, \ref{sec:partII-I.1} and~\ref{sec:partII-J}. Entry bounds may be raised.\\[3pt]
7 & \raggedright $\bar\beta$, entry levels $m_j^{\rm ent}$, minimum periods $n_j^{\min}$ & Depend on rows 1--6. The levels~$m_j^{\rm ent}$ delay the first use of stage~$j$; $n_j^{\min}$ are its minimum lattice periods. Choose~$\bar\beta>1$ large enough for the initial coupling requirements and for~$t_m\le1$ at every level. Choose entry levels and periods to satisfy the remaining level and geometric requirements. Delaying entry preserves earlier requirements.\\[3pt]
8 & \raggedright $J_m,\ell_m,D_m$, $a_m,L_p$, $\reflevel{d}$ & Depend on rows 1--7. Set $D_0=1$, $D_{m+1}=\ell_mD_m$, $a_m=D_m^{-1}$, $s_m=\log D_m$, with $\ell_m=\ell^{\rm st}_{J_{m+1}}$ and $4\le J_m\le J_{m+1}\le J_m+1$, $J_m\to\infty$. Require $0<\theta_{J_m}\le J_m^{-1}$, $\log\ell_m\to\infty$, $\log\ell_m/s_m\to0$. Choose strictly increasing~$L_p$ with~$4\mid L_p$ and every positive integer dividing infinitely many~$L_p$. Require $L_pD_m\ge100(B_m+1)$ and $L_pD_{m+1}\ge100((J_{m+1}+3)\ell_m+1)$. Choose $J_{\reflevel{d}}\ge d+3$, with $\mathfrak q_{J_m}\ge d+1$, $\varpi_{J_m}\ge d$ for $m\ge\reflevel{d}$.\\[3pt]
9 & \raggedright Derived level scales & No new choices. Set
\begin{gather*}
\refbeta_m=\bar\beta+\slope s_m>1,\qquad
\cutoffsize_m=\exp((\log\log\refbeta_m)^2)\,,\\
t_m=\refbeta_m^{-1/2}\cutoffsize_m,\qquad
B_m=\lceil(\log\refbeta_m)^2\rceil\,,\\
\epsilon_m^{\rm reg}=\refbeta_m^{-11/10},\qquad
\epsilon_m^{\rm for}=e^{-(\cutoffsize_m)^{1/5}}\,,\\
\Lambda_m^{\rm loop}=2(\cutoffsize_m)^{\theta_{J_m}},\qquad
\mathfrak B_m=[\refbeta_m/2,2\refbeta_m]\,.
\end{gather*} These give the field thresholds, activity bounds and coupling bands. The quantity~$\Lambda_m^{\rm loop}$ bounds the weighted quadratic loop sum in Section~\ref{sec:partII-C}, \ref{lem:C1}.\\[3pt]
10 & \raggedright Actions, classes, recursion, trajectories, couplings; width~$w_{\rm tun}$ & Depend on rows 1--9. Choose the path actions and admissible subsets, the specified recursion and its trajectories for every finite~$N,p$, all with the common level-zero endpoint (Theorem~\ref{prop:MT}). In Section~\ref{sec:partII-E}, \ref{lem:E1.3}, $w_{\rm tun}>0$ is the half-width of the endpoint interval $\bar\beta+[-w_{\rm tun},w_{\rm tun}]$, independent of~$N,p$. The tuned bare coupling for endpoint~$\bar\beta+w_{\rm tun}s$ extends holomorphically in~$s$ to~$|s|<1$.\\[3pt]
11 & \raggedright Marked transfers, terminal maps, insertions & Depend on rows 1--10; impose reference normalization (Definition~\ref{def:D08}). Fix them before any prescribed volume sequence.\\[3pt]
12 & \raggedright $\mathsf p$, then $\mathsf q,S,F_d,\partial_a,\mathcal L$ & Prescribe arbitrary increasing~$\mathsf p$ first. Theorem~\ref{prop:IV} and Proposition~\ref{prop:FC} give the refinement and continuum fields. Theorem~\ref{prop:UV} retains the full inner limit.\\[3pt]
13 & \raggedright Comparison radii, frames, $J$, charges, $c_\star,R,T$ & Depend on row 12 and the fixed lattice family. Use Proposition~\ref{prop:MC}, then Theorem~\ref{prop:ST} and Proposition~\ref{prop:SC}. Refine only comparison radii.\\[3pt]
14 & \raggedright Slab operators, $\phases,\nu,S^\xi$, then~$\xi_*$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:PH} gives the phase decomposition. Choose~$\xi_*$ from the full-measure set with one-dimensional time-axis OS ground space (\ref{lem:H1.3}). This choice precedes reconstruction and uses only that ground-space property.\\[3pt]
15 & \raggedright Every-phase theories and continuations; $\Theta,\tracespectrum$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:RC} reconstructs the theories. Definition~\ref{def:D16} centers the trace in the already selected phase; Theorem~\ref{prop:TR} gives its short-distance law; \ref{lem:I1.2} supplies its spectral measure.\\[3pt]
16 & \raggedright OPE prescription; contact projections, distributions, bases, scales & Depend on rows 12--14 and the fixed family. Theorems~\ref{prop:OP} and~\ref{prop:CT} use the same original fields and correlation functionals. The contact projections, separating functionals and clearance are chosen separately from those used for the product coefficients.\\[3pt]
17 & \raggedright Estimate constants and thresholds & Depend only on the fixed data preceding them in each statement. They have no later position, volume or state dependence wherever uniformity is asserted.\\
