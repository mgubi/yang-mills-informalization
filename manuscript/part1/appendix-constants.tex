\section{Constants and order of choice}\label{sec:constants}

Table~\ref{tab:constant-choices} gives the choices for the construction. At each stage there are finitely many inequalities on constants chosen in the indicated order. Leading-map bounds are fixed before column bounds, columns before ratios, and these before the coupling entries. Later ratios and entries may be increased without changing earlier choices. The displayed inequalities are properties of the chosen construction.

1 & \raggedright Gauge normalization;\newline $\blockpenalty>0$ & Fix~$G$, its realization, probability Haar measure and metric, then~$\dimG,\casimir,b_0,\slope$ as in Definition~\ref{def:D01}; $\slope>0$. All later choices use these data.\\[3pt]
2 & \raggedright Frame and predictor cutoffs; axial factor & Depend on~$G$. The frame and predictor cutoffs select the unique-minimizer branches of the block kernel. On a radius-$R$ patch, the total link chord in an eligible axial frame is at most $a_{\rm ax}(2R+1)$ times the total plaquette chord, with $a_{\rm ax}\ge16$. Choose the cutoffs small enough for branch validity on every patch (Lemma~\ref{lem:B1}).\\[3pt]
3 & \raggedright Homogeneous bases, norms, sections, stencils & Depend on~$G,d$. Impose Taylor matching, reality and~$\hypercubic$ covariance (Lemma~\ref{lem:A1} and Definitions~\ref{def:D07}--\ref{def:D08}).\\[3pt]
4 & \raggedright Stage parameters & Depend on rows 1--3 and~$j$. Choose derivative orders~$\mathfrak q_j\ge6$, spatial powers~$\varpi_j\ge2$ and support exponents~$\sigma_j=100$. In Section~\ref{sec:partII-C}, \ref{lem:C1}, $w_j^{\rm path}>0$ multiplies path length in the exponential weight, and~$A_j^{\rm path}>0$ bounds the weighted row, cubic and quartic sums. Fix the entire nondecreasing sequence~$K_j\ge1$ bounding the leading coefficient maps and their inverses, including the finite symmetry averages, as specified before Lemma~\ref{lem:E1.1}. Then choose nondecreasing column bounds~$C_j\ge64K_{j+1}^2$, including the derivative factorials and finite basis-recombination constants, as in item~\ref{it:E-columns} preceding that lemma. These columns bound marked coefficient transport (Lemma~\ref{lem:E2.2}). Let $\gamma_j>0$ be half a uniform positive lower bound for the free effective plaquette forms in Lemma~\ref{lem:B2}, chosen for the top form and both possible incoming stages. Choose clipping coefficients with $0<16c_{{\rm lin},j}<c_{{\rm dir},j}\le\min(\gamma_j,1)$; the remaining free form then has the positive row factor used in Definition~\ref{def:D23}. Its health cutoff relaxes with distance through $e^{\operatorname{dist}/R_j^{\rm rel}}$, where $R_j^{\rm rel}>0$ and $R_j^{\rm rel}w_j^{\rm path}\ge12$. Let $d_j\ge0$ be the block-ratio-independent one-loop bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:C7}. Fix the reset half-width $r_{\rm res}=1$, then choose $\omega_j^{\rm RG}\ge d_j+1$ for Lemma~\ref{lem:C8}. Orders and powers tend to infinity and, along levels, are nondecreasing, as are support exponents. All ratio costs are now fixed.\\[3pt]
5 & \raggedright Odd ratios $\ell_j^{\rm st}$ & Depend on rows 1--4; choose $\ell_j^{\rm st}\ge3$ nondecreasing and above every ratio threshold. At $j=J_{m+1}$ and $\ell_m=\ell_j^{\rm st}$ require
\begin{gather*}
\frac{\omega_j^{\rm RG}}{\log\ell_m}\le\min(\slope/4,j^{-2})\,,\\
\frac{\log(1+A_j^{\rm path}+(w_j^{\rm path})^{-1}+C_j+R_j^{\rm rel}+\sigma_j)}{\log\ell_m}\le j^{-2}\,.
\end{gather*}
These bounds control the drift and logarithmic stage costs relative to~$\log\ell_m$. Impose also the graph and matching restrictions~\eqref{eq:E-graph-reserve}--\eqref{eq:E-matching-choices}, the local marked error restriction in item~\ref{it:E-graph} preceding Lemma~\ref{lem:E1.1}, and the ratio inequalities of~\eqref{eq:J-stage-room}. Include the source comparison constants through~\eqref{eq:E-log-cost}.\\[3pt]
6 & \raggedright Exponents and coupling entry bounds & Depend on rows 1--5. The positive antitone exponents~$\theta_j$ control the quadratic loop bound in row~9. The bounds~$\beta_j^{\rm ent}$ are lower bounds on the reference coupling when stage~$j$ is used. Choose them to meet the finite maximum of the remaining stage requirements, including those of Sections~\ref{sec:partII-G.2}, \ref{sec:partII-I.1} and~\ref{sec:partII-J}. Impose the comparison entry bounds~\eqref{eq:E-Gaussian-entry}, for each arity admitted at that stage. For Section~\ref{sec:partII-I.1}, raise the entry bounds to ensure $\ell_m\le\log\refbeta_m$ and $\max(\ell^{\rm st}_{J_m},\ell^{\rm st}_{J_m-1})\le\cutoffsize_m$ at every sufficiently large level, including stage transitions. Include both adjacent stage ratios and the finite coefficient-error thresholds in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:I-transfer}. Entry bounds may be raised.\\[3pt]
7 & \raggedright $\bar\beta$, entry levels $m_j^{\rm ent}$, minimum periods $n_j^{\min}$ & Depend on rows 1--6. The levels~$m_j^{\rm ent}$ delay the first use of stage~$j$; $n_j^{\min}$ are its minimum lattice periods. Choose~$\bar\beta>1$ large enough for the initial coupling requirements and for~$t_m\le1$ at every level. Choose entry levels and periods to satisfy the remaining level and geometric requirements, including the scale restrictions~\eqref{eq:E-marker-scales} and comparison intervals~\eqref{eq:E-window}. The marked construction uses Lemma~\ref{lem:J-marked-costs}, with weight restrictions~\eqref{eq:J-marked-weights} and entry restrictions~\eqref{eq:J-entrance}. Impose also the support-enlargement and occupancy entry requirements in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:J-lattice}, and the regular marked weight inequalities stated before Lemma~\ref{lem:J-marked-costs}. Delaying entry preserves earlier requirements.\\[3pt]
8 & \raggedright $J_m,\ell_m,D_m$, $a_m,L_p$, $\reflevel{d}$ & Depend on rows 1--7. Set $D_0=1$, $D_{m+1}=\ell_mD_m$, $a_m=D_m^{-1}$, $s_m=\log D_m$, with $\ell_m=\ell^{\rm st}_{J_{m+1}}$ and $4\le J_m\le J_{m+1}\le J_m+1$, $J_m\to\infty$. Require $0<\theta_{J_m}\le J_m^{-1}$, $\log\ell_m\to\infty$, $\log\ell_m/s_m\to0$. Choose strictly increasing~$L_p$ with~$4\mid L_p$ and every positive integer dividing infinitely many~$L_p$. Require $L_pD_m\ge100(B_m+1)$ and $L_pD_{m+1}\ge100((J_{m+1}+3)\ell_m+1)$. Choose $J_{\reflevel{d}}\ge d+3$, with $\mathfrak q_{J_m}\ge d+1$, $\varpi_{J_m}\ge d$ for $m\ge\reflevel{d}$.\\[3pt]
9 & \raggedright Derived level scales & The scales are determined by earlier choices. Set
\begin{gather*}
\refbeta_m=\bar\beta+\slope s_m>1,\qquad
\cutoffsize_m=\exp((\log\log\refbeta_m)^2)\,,\\
t_m=\refbeta_m^{-1/2}\cutoffsize_m,\qquad
B_m=\lceil(\log\refbeta_m)^2\rceil\,,\\
\epsilon_m^{\rm reg}=\refbeta_m^{-11/10},\qquad
\epsilon_m^{\rm for}=e^{-(\cutoffsize_m)^{1/5}}\,,\\
\Lambda_m^{\rm loop}=2(\cutoffsize_m)^{\theta_{J_m}},\qquad
\mathfrak B_m=[\refbeta_m/2,2\refbeta_m]\,.
\end{gather*} These give the field thresholds, activity bounds and coupling bands. The quantity~$\Lambda_m^{\rm loop}$ bounds the weighted quadratic loop sum in Section~\ref{sec:partII-C}, \ref{lem:C1}.\\[3pt]
10 & \raggedright Actions, classes, recursion, trajectories, couplings; width~$w_{\rm tun}$ & Depend on rows 1--9. Choose the path actions and admissible subsets, the specified recursion and its trajectories for every finite~$N,p$, all with the common level-zero endpoint (Theorem~\ref{prop:MT}). Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-tuning} gives the analytic variation used in the ultraviolet limit.\\[3pt]
11 & \raggedright Marked transfers, terminal maps, insertions & Depend on rows 1--10; impose reference normalization (Definition~\ref{def:D08}). Fix them before any prescribed volume sequence.\\[3pt]
12 & \raggedright $\mathsf p$, then $\mathsf q,P,S,F_d,\partial_a,\mathcal L$ & Prescribe arbitrary increasing~$\mathsf p$ first. Theorem~\ref{prop:IV} and Proposition~\ref{prop:FC} give the retained volume indices~$P=\mathsf p\circ\mathsf q$ and continuum fields. Theorem~\ref{prop:UV} retains the full inner limit.\\[3pt]
13 & \raggedright Comparison radii, frames, $J$, charges, $c_\star,R,T$ & Depend on row 12 and the fixed lattice family. Use Proposition~\ref{prop:MC}, then Theorem~\ref{prop:ST} and Proposition~\ref{prop:SC}. Refine only comparison radii.\\[3pt]
14 & \raggedright Slab operators, $\phases,\nu,S^\xi$, then~$\xi_*$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:PH} gives the phase decomposition. Choose~$\xi_*$ from the full-measure set with one-dimensional time-axis OS ground space (\ref{lem:H1.3}). This choice precedes reconstruction.\\[3pt]
15 & \raggedright Every-phase theories and continuations; $\Theta,\tracespectrum$ & Depend on earlier data. Theorem~\ref{prop:RC} reconstructs the theories. Definition~\ref{def:D16} centers the trace in the already selected phase; Theorem~\ref{prop:TR} gives its short-distance law; \ref{lem:I1.2} supplies its spectral measure.\\[3pt]
16 & \raggedright OPE prescription; contact projections, distributions, bases, scales & Depend on rows 12--14 and the fixed family. Theorems~\ref{prop:OP} and~\ref{prop:CT} use the same original fields and correlation table. The contact projections, separating functionals and clearance are chosen separately from those used for the product coefficients.\\[3pt]
17 & \raggedright Estimate constants and thresholds & Depend only on the fixed data preceding them in each statement. Their dependence is uniform in positions, volumes and states wherever asserted.\\
