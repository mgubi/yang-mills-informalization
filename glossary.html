This page translates the nicknames used in OpenAI's Lean 4 formalization of the construction of four-dimensional quantum Yang–Mills theory into ordinary mathematics. It is meant for mathematicians and mathematical physicists who want to read the formalization, or write an exposition of it. It accompanies the outline of the work items.

Every Lean name is a link to its definition at a fixed commit of yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028.

Reading. certain = read from the definition and its uses; probable = inferred from the fields and the documentation; tentative = partly guessed (where the role is unknown, the entry says "unclear"). These labels describe how an entry was interpreted; even certain does not mean an independent proof audit. They are separate from the unit outline statuses. An entry marked main statement occurs in a public definition or in the detailed construction statement; it need not be a conclusion of the public theorem.

Why a glossary

The formalization uses arbitrary English words for its objects: Treasury, Jewel, Sheet, Teller, Almanac, Solstice, and so on. Most of them carry no information about the mathematics. The names were evidently chosen to be distinct, not descriptive, so the code cannot be read from its identifiers alone. This glossary gives, for 276 declarations, the mathematical object each name stands for, with the file and line of its definition. It covers the declarations that occur in the main statements, together with the most widely used ones in each part of the proof; it is not exhaustive.

The main theorem

The public theorem is YangMills.jointExistence; see the exact statement and its limits. It fixes one regular Wilson family before a requested volume sequence, then supplies a refinement of that volume sequence and a hierarchy with four properties. The following account describes the detailed construction theorem, YangMills.constructed in Construction.lean. In plain mathematics, with the Lean names in brackets:

  • Fix a simple compact matrix gauge group G and a number b > 0, a numerical parameter of the block-spin kernel (KernelChoices.penalty).

  • There is a Wilson family C: the whole renormalization-group construction, consisting of lattice spacings a_N, torus periods L_p, couplings β_{N,p}, and the renormalized lattice observables for every label. It is internally consistent (C.Makes) and satisfies YangMills.WilsonRegularity: β_{N,p}/log(1/a_N) tends to a positive slope, the observables are continuous and gauge invariant, and each depends on a fixed finite stencil of links.

  • For every pair p of mesh and volume subsequences (an Extraction.Order) there is a refinement q of both, and

    • a limit Euclidean theory S, the family of all n-point functions of all labels (a Table);

    • a record F (a Final) containing the data fixing the renormalization-group trajectory, the axioms of the limit, the field algebra (derivatives, rotations, stress tensor), the decomposition into phases and the Wightman reconstruction;

    • the same reconstruction for every phase (an EveryPhase.Almanac);

    • the data of the operator product expansion (a Reader) and of the extraction of contact terms (Contacts.Devices);

    • a scaling sequence r_k → 0 for the comparison with the free Maxwell field (Climb.Scaling).

  • The conclusions are the public properties (the Wilson limit; reconstruction; the operator product expansion; a non-free trace test), the ordered limit YM.Extraction.Order.Ordered, YM.FullVolume.PositionsUV, YM.Spec.Calibrated, the independent checks YM.Independent.Core, the Wightman axioms for the selected phase and for each character (Headline.Reconstruction), the separated and nested operator product expansions, the trace exclusions, the extension across contact terms (Solstice.MultipleLight, Thresholds, SystemBasis), and the short-distance comparison with free Maxwell fields (Pert.FirstExtension, Pert.Position.FourMeters).

Public dictionary (from HierarchyBridge.lean and WilsonBridge.lean). The Hilbert space Hierarchy.H is the Hilbert space of the reconstructed theory of the selected phase. Field d is the quotient label space F_d = Label_d / null. toLabel is Table.pin. Character is the compact space of phases F.alpha.space.X, with its measure mu. correlation s is Table.useAt (spectator F s); for s = none it is the mixed theory S^mix, for s = some ξ the phase S^ξ. continuation is the analytic continuation F.A.waves.F. centeredTrace is TraceExtension.center F. For the Wilson family, period, denominator, beta and insertion are C.S.period, C.S.denominator, C.beta and C.seen.

In the construction the quotient spaces have compatible injective maps \(F_d \to F_e\) for \(d \leqslant e\), and their images in the response-function space give the common label space. This is a filtration by bounded degree, not a direct sum of separate degree spaces. The public hierarchy does not require these inclusions; see Definition 1.11.

Nickname families

The words are arbitrary, but several families of them are systematic. Where a family is described as a pattern, it is a pattern in the code and its docstrings, not a documented convention.

  1. Calendar and economics: the order in which constants are chosen. The renormalization-group argument needs its constants in a strict order: first the "pre-L" constants (orders, powers, support exponents, widths), then the block sizes L (odd, large), then the "post-L" constants (the smallness exponent θ, entry thresholds, volume shells), then a barrier β̄. The file Price/Intent.lean is titled "Calendar issuance API: Orders & constants chosen BEFORE block ratio". Accordingly Sheet holds the pre-L constants, Rolls the block sizes L_j, Protection the exponent θ, Delays the deadlines, Bar the barrier, Wait the windows, and Paid says that the schedule has "paid" every cost. Supply is the namespace of these. MapPrices, PostCost, Worth, Teller (one call of the step estimator: "prices and inputs"), Funds (the call is affordable), Stores, Cash, bill, toll, fee, price and cost are numerical bounds and their bookkeeping. "Budget", "price" and "cost" always mean a numerical bound or exponent in a polymer norm (for example SumBudget, BudgetPoint), never anything economic.

  2. Weather and seasons: contact terms. The namespace Contacts.Solstice (Lights, Sunshine, MultipleLight, Thresholds, Backdrop, Annulus, Run) is the extension of correlations across coincident points using the operator product expansion. Devices, Detector, Prescription and Reader are the fixed coefficient-extraction functionals (the "tomography" of the docstrings).

  3. Rooms and halls: from Euclidean to Hilbert space. Chamber is the Osterwalder–Schrader (GNS) Hilbert space for a time axis; Spread and Slab are time-slab word operators; Heat is the semigroup e^{-sH}; Contour, Waves and Surface are the analytic continuation and the Wightman distributions; Almanac (in EveryPhase) is the reconstruction data for all phases; Pantry, Pack, Docks and Stress.* (in EveryPhase.Stress) are the machinery for continuing the Ward identities and the stress tensor.

  4. Tables and labels. These names are used consistently. A Register is a label system with a scale for each degree; a Token is a homogeneous reference label; a Table is the family of all correlation functions; Field d is F_d; a Tag is a pair (d, element of F_d); Union is the union of all F_d. Table.Hierarchy is the Euclidean axioms of the limit theory; it is not the public YangMills.Hierarchy, which is the whole package.

  5. Extraction. Extraction.Order is a pair of a mesh subsequence N_j and a volume subsequence p_k; late means "for k large, then j large"; refine passes to further subsequences; Ordered states that the ordered limit holds. This is the subsequential structure of the detailed theorem. The public theorem requests only a volume subsequence and retains the full spacing sequence.

  6. Mathematics behind plain English words. Tree, Forest, Pines, Slice: tree gauge fixing. Cells, Tiling, Interior, rootTest: reflection-positivity cells and chessboard estimates (rootTest is \(|\mathbb{E} \prod_i \text{copy}_i F|^{1 / \sharp \text{cells}}\), the chessboard root). Marker: a nilpotent bookkeeping algebra for products of polymers. Bloch, Laurent, Circulant, Row, Momentum: Fourier analysis of translation-invariant kernels on the periodic lattice. Exterior, TypeFace, Body: lattice differential forms. Precision, PCalc, Horizon, Bed, Floor: linearization of the renormalization-group step and quadratic-form (Hessian) estimates. Stationary: saddle-point analysis. Pressure: estimates for one renormalization-group step. ConnectedActivityE6: connected-activity (cluster expansion) bounds. Ac: bookkeeping of connected words and clusters. Ward: sums in the Ward identity. Climb.Scaling: the scaling sequence for the free-Maxwell comparison. Trace*: the centered trace and the anomaly. Pert: the perturbative (one-loop) and free-Maxwell comparison. Maxwell, Wick: free-field models.

  7. Directory names without meaning. Next/, Push/, Further/, Extra/, Work/A01, RG05 to RG17, UVNext, SectorNext, MultiNext, PhysicalNext, AnalyticNext, AnalyticBridge, and the capitalized subfolders are folder names that carry no mathematical meaning.

  8. Short names which are not nicknames. c, f are the coarse and fine torus sides; N, p are the mesh and volume indices; m is the renormalization-group level; J is the regularity index of a level; L is the block size; d is the degree (weight) of a label; β̂ is the renormalized coupling at level m. "Denotational" means a representation as data, not as a quotient. "Junk at coincident points" means that functions are made total by arbitrary values where they are undefined.

About a third of the entries have reading probable or tentative, mostly in Part III, where the code is a very large set of technical estimate packages (polymer norms, cluster bounds).
