# Chapter 26. Short-distance limit: lattice correlators converge to free Maxwell (order zero)

*Part VII — Identification as Yang–Mills.* Proposed title, reading *probable*.

**Size:** 8,117 lines of Lean in 2,077 declarations used by the main theorem; 37 sections; 16 key statements.
