# Chapter 7. Analytic toolkit: jets, polydisc analyticity, Maxwell complex, Schatten-trace Lipschitz bounds

*Part tools — General tools.* Proposed title, reading *probable*.

**What this chapter does.** Polydisc analyticity and smooth jets, exterior algebra and Bianchi identity for Maxwell data, Laurent/Fourier machinery, and trace inequalities for products of nonnegative kernels (`Holder.finite_lip`, `TimeKernel.passage`: moving a gap center by m steps changes the trace by at most cost*m/r).
