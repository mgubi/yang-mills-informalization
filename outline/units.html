The proof is divided into thirty-two units grouped by mathematical subject in eight parts. Each page contains its outline, pinned Lean references and selected statements. Allocation of declarations to a unit is distinct from the mathematical inputs needed to understand its argument; see the overview and data.

All outlines are available. The status links below explain the three review rounds: draft, checked and edited. A status describes the review of the explanation; each unit's “What remains unexplained” section gives its remaining limits. Source-size figures are collected below the index.
