Conventions.

  • \(\Lambda_n = (\mathbb{Z}/ n\mathbb{Z})^4\), with \(n \geqslant 1\), is the periodic lattice with the maximum distance \(d\). A support is a subset of \(\Lambda_n\).

  • \(\text{cov}_B (X)\) is the least number of \(d\)-balls of radius \(B\) whose union is connected and covers \(X\) (coverNumber). Two properties are used throughout (I.1):

    • \(|X| \leqslant \text{ball}_B \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (X)\), with \(\text{ball}_B = (2 B + 1)^4\);

    • \(\text{cov}_B\) is subadditive on unions of overlapping sets.

  • A family of supports is connected if it is nonempty and its overlap graph is connected.

  • For \(f \geqslant 0\) on an arbitrary index set, \(\text{SB} (f ; C)\) means: \(f\) is summable and \(\sum f \leqslant C\) (SumBudget).

  • For polymers \((X_i, w_i)_{i \in \iota}\) with \(w \geqslant 0\), the site-sum condition \(\text{Hit}_B (w ; a, \gamma)\) means \(\text{SB} \left( i \mapsto \boldsymbol{1}[x \in X_i] \hspace{0.17em} w_i \hspace{0.17em} e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (X_i)} ; \gamma \right)\) for every site \(x\) (Ac.Hit, II.2).

  • All weights are real. An upper bound on an infinite sum of nonnegative terms includes summability, as in \(\text{SB}\).

  • For a relation \(R\) that is not symmetric, the graph used below has an edge between distinct occurrences \(a, b\) exactly when both \(R (z_a, z_b)\) and \(R (z_b, z_a)\) hold. The coefficient of a one-vertex tuple is \(1\).

1. What this unit proves

The unit consists of five packages.

  • (A) Mayer expansion.

    • The Mayer expansion of a hard-core polymer gas, with absolute bounds, and its re-exponentiation after forced polymers (III.5.1–III.5.6).

    • The verification of the smallness condition for gases of large-field seeds of a block-spin step (III.5.7–III.5.8).

  • (B) Vacuum normalization. A second, graph-free construction of the logarithm by deletion identities (III.5.9), and a trace-log formula (III.5.10).

  • (C) Sector decomposition. The decomposition of one block-spin integral into sectors labelled by packets of seeds, with a Gaussian gain per seed (III.5.11).

  • (D) Inverse decay. A Combes–Thomas bound (III.5.12).

  • (E) Gaussian bounds. Windowed inverses (III.5.13), and a uniform spectral gap and uniform decay for the precision matrix of the small-field Gaussian of a step (III.5.14).

III.5.1–III.5.7, III.5.9, III.5.10, III.5.12 and III.5.13 are general implications. III.5.8(c), III.5.11 and III.5.14 concern the block-spin step under the funding hypotheses of III.1; III.5.14 also fixes a patch satisfying its branch conditions.

Proposition III.5.1 (Mayer identity for a finite gas).

  • Data. Let \(\iota\) be finite, \(R\) a reflexive symmetric relation on \(\iota\) ("incompatible"), and \(w : \iota \to \mathbb{R}\). Let \(\Xi (w) = \sum_s \prod_{i \in s} w_i\), the sum over subsets \(s \subseteq \iota\) of pairwise compatible labels.

  • Ursell coefficient. For \(z = (z_0, \ldots, z_m) \in \iota^{m + 1}\), let \(G_R (z)\) be the graph on \(\{0, \ldots, m\}\) with an edge \(\{a, b\}\), \(a \neq b\), whenever \(R (z_a, z_b)\). Put \(\phi^T_R (z) = \sum_G (- 1)^{|G|}\), the sum over connected spanning subgraphs \(G \subseteq G_R (z)\).

  • Claim. Let \(\ell_m = \frac{1}{(m + 1) !} \sum_{z \in \iota^{m + 1}} \phi^T_R (z) \prod_a w_{z_a}\). If \(| \ell_m | \leqslant Cq^m\) for all \(m\), with \(C \geqslant 0\) and \(0 < q < 1\), then \(\exp \left( \sum_{m \geqslant 0} \ell_m \right) = \Xi (w)\).

Status: theorem. Lean: Gas.exact_polynomial; coefficient identities Coef.recurrence, Partition.partition.

Proposition III.5.2 (tree-graph bound). Let \(T\) be a graph on a finite vertex set with a distinguished vertex. Then \(| \sum_G (- 1)^{|G|} |\), summed over the connected spanning subgraphs \(G \subseteq T\), is at most the number of spanning trees of \(T\). In particular \(| \phi^T_R (z) |\) is at most the number of spanning trees of \(G_R (z)\). Status: theorem. Spanning trees are counted as acyclic parent maps towards the distinguished vertex. Lean: Graph.pay.

Proposition III.5.3 (pinned cluster bounds).

  • Data. \(\iota\) is any set; \(X : \iota \to\) supports of \(\Lambda_n\); \(B \in \mathbb{N}\); \(w : \iota \to [0, \infty)\); \(a \geqslant 0\); \(R\) is a relation on \(\iota\) with \(R (i, j) \Rightarrow X_i \cap X_j \neq \emptyset\). \(R\) need not be symmetric.

  • Smallness condition.

\(\displaystyle \text{Ready} (w ; a, \gamma, \delta, q) : \qquad \gamma > 0, \quad \delta > 0, \quad 4 \hspace{0.17em} \text{ball}_B \hspace{0.17em} \gamma / \delta \leqslant q < 1, \quad \text{Hit}_B (w ; \hspace{0.17em} a + \delta, \hspace{0.17em} \gamma) .\)

  • Notation. \(X (z) = \bigcup_a X_{z_a}\) for a tuple \(z\).

Then, for every site \(x\) and every integer \(m \geqslant 0\):

  • (a) \(\displaystyle \sum_{m' \geqslant m} \sum_{z \in \iota^{m' + 1} : x \in X (z)} \frac{| \phi^T_R (z) |}{(m' + 1) !} \prod_a w_{z_a} e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (X (z))} \leqslant \frac{\gamma \hspace{0.17em} q^m}{1 - q}\).

  • (b) The same bound holds for \(\sum_s \prod_{i \in s} \left( w_i e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (X_i)} \right)\). The sum runs over finite sets \(s \subseteq \iota\) with \(|s| \geqslant m + 1\) whose supports form a connected family containing \(x\).

  • (c) (Marked form.) Suppose \(Q \subseteq \iota\) and \(\text{SB} (i \mapsto \boldsymbol{1}[i \in Q] w_i e^{(a + \delta) \text{cov}_B (X_i)} ; b)\). Then the sum over \(z \in \iota^{m + 1}\) with some \(z_a \in Q\) of \(| \phi^T_R (z) | \prod_{j = 0}^m \left( w_{z_j} e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (X_{z_j})} \right) / (m + 1) !\) is at most \(bq^m\).

Each displayed bound is a summability statement of the form \(\text{SB}\). Status: theorem. Form (a) is used in III.2, III.10, III.11, III.12, III.13 and III.15. Lean: Ursell.log_hit_tail, Trees.touch_tail, Ursell.cluster_at, Ursell.cluster_tail; hypothesis Trees.Ready.

Proposition III.5.4 (convergence and logarithm of the gas). Let \(X, B\) be as in III.5.3, \(R\) reflexive and symmetric with \(R (i, j) \Rightarrow X_i \cap X_j \neq \emptyset\), \(w : \iota \to \mathbb{R}\) and \(a \geqslant 0\). Assume \(\text{Ready} (|w| ; a, \gamma, \delta, q)\). Then:

  • \(\Xi (w) = \sum_s \prod_{i \in s} w_i\), over finite pairwise compatible \(s \subseteq \iota\), converges absolutely, with \(\sum_s \prod_{i \in s} |w_i | \leqslant e^{n^4 \gamma}\).

  • The cluster series \(\sum_{m \geqslant 0} \sum_{z \in \iota^{m + 1}} \phi^T_R (z) \prod_a w_{z_a} / (m + 1) !\) converges absolutely. Its absolute sum is at most \(n^4 \gamma / (1 - q)\), and at most \(n^4 \gamma q^m\) over the clusters of \(m + 1\) labels.

  • \(\Xi (w) > 0\), and \(\log \Xi (w)\) equals the cluster series.

Status: theorem. Used in III.10. Lean: Gas.price_log, Gas.absolute, Gas.absolute_at.

Proposition III.5.5 (removing the polymers that meet a region). In III.5.4, take for \(R\) the overlap relation (\(R (i, j)\) iff \(i = j\) or \(X_i \cap X_j \neq \emptyset\)), with nonempty supports. For \(Y \subseteq \Lambda_n\) let \(w^Y_i = w_i \hspace{0.17em} \boldsymbol{1} [X_i \cap Y = \emptyset]\). Then

\(\displaystyle \Xi (w^Y) = \Xi (w) \hspace{0.17em} e^{- p}, \qquad |p| \leqslant \frac{|Y| \hspace{0.17em} \gamma}{1 - q},\)

where \(p\) is the sum of the cluster terms with \(X (z) \cap Y \neq \emptyset\).

  • Hence \(e^{- C |Y|} \Xi (w) \leqslant \Xi (w^Y) \leqslant e^{C |Y|} \Xi (w)\) with \(C = \gamma / (1 - q)\), uniformly in \(n\).

  • The clusters of at least \(m + 1\) labels that meet \(Y\) contribute at most \(|Y| \gamma q^m / (1 - q)\) in absolute value.

Status: theorem. Used in III.10. Lean: Gas.restores_log, Gas.missing_tail.

Proposition III.5.6 (re-exponentiation with forced polymers). Fix \(B \in \mathbb{N}\) and a common lattice \(\Lambda_n\).

  • Data. "Unforced" polymers \((X_i, u_i)_{i \in \iota}\) and "forced" polymers \((Y_k, d_k)_{k \in \kappa}\), all with nonempty supports, \(u, d \geqslant 0\); \(R\) a relation on \(\iota\) contained in the overlap relation.

  • Atoms. The atoms are of two kinds:

    • the forced labels \(k\), with support \(Y_k\) and weight \(d_k\);

    • the tuples \(z \in \iota^{m + 1}\), with support \(X (z)\) and weight \(| \phi^T_R (z) | \prod_a u_{z_a} / (m + 1) !\).

  • Constants. \(A \geqslant 0\), \(\delta' > 0\), \(t \geqslant 1\), \(0 \leqslant \sigma < 1\), \(\mu \geqslant 0\), \(\eta > 0\), \(q' < 1\).

  • Hypotheses. \(\text{Ready} (u ; A + \delta', \gamma, \delta, q)\), \(\text{Hit}_B (d ; A + \delta', c)\), and

\(\displaystyle \frac{t \hspace{0.17em} e^{\mu} c}{1 - \sigma} + \frac{t \hspace{0.17em} \gamma}{(1 - \sigma) (1 - q)} \leqslant \eta \leqslant \sigma, \qquad 4 \hspace{0.17em} \text{ball}_B \hspace{0.17em} \eta / \delta' \leqslant q' .\)

Fix a site \(x\) and \(m \geqslant 0\). Consider finite sets \(s\) of atoms with multiplicities \(n_{\alpha} \geqslant 1\) such that:

  • \(s\) contains at least one forced label;

  • \(\sum_{\alpha} n_{\alpha} \geqslant m\);

  • the supports of the atoms in \(s\) form a connected family containing \(x\).

Summed over all such pairs \((s, (n_{\alpha})_{\alpha \in s})\), the quantity

\(\displaystyle e^{A \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (\bigcup_{\alpha \in s} \text{supp} \hspace{0.17em} \alpha)} \prod_{\alpha \in s} \frac{\text{wt} (\alpha)^{n_{\alpha}}}{n_{\alpha} !}\)

is at most \(t^{- m} e^{- \mu} \hspace{0.17em} \eta / (1 - q')\).

Status: theorem. The general form, with an arbitrary selection predicate, is Multiple.estimates. Used in III.2, III.10, III.11 and III.13. Lean: OmitClusters.restore.

Proposition III.5.7 (gases of seed packets).

Setting. Let \(S\) be a block-spin step (III.1) from \(\Lambda_f\) to \(\Lambda_c\) with block side \(L\), and let \(l, B \in \mathbb{N}\).

  • A seed is a fine cell, a coarse bond or a coarse site. Its location is the projection of the cell’s vertices, the bond’s two endpoints, or the site itself. Each location lies within distance \(1\) of its anchor.

  • A packet \(A\) (Stores) is a finite set of seeds; \(|A|\) is its number of seeds.

  • Let \(D_0 (A)\) be the union of its seed locations and \(D_r (A)\) its radius-\(r\) neighbourhood. The skins are the radius-\(lB\) neighbourhoods of the individual seed locations; their union is the dome \(D (A) = D_{lB} (A)\). The packet is connected if its skins form a connected family. The sector and matrix estimates below use \(D_r (A)\) directly, rather than neighbourhoods of \(D (A)\).

  • Put

\(\displaystyle \tau_L = L^4 \hspace{0.17em} \text{card} \{ \text{plaquette orientations} \} + 5, \qquad K_{\text{hit}} = (2 lB + 3)^4 \hspace{0.17em} \tau_L, \qquad K_{\text{step}} = (4 lB + 5)^4 \hspace{0.17em} \tau_L .\)

(a) Packet sums. Assume \(a, C \geqslant 0\), \(0 \leqslant p_A \leqslant C \hspace{0.17em} e^{(s - g) |A|}\) for every connected \(A\), and \(\rho := K_{\text{step}}^2 \hspace{0.17em} e^{\hspace{0.17em} s - g + a (2 l + 3)^4} \leqslant q < 1\). Then for every coarse site \(x\) and every \(m \geqslant 0\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_{A \text{connected}, x \in D (A), |A| \geqslant m} p_A \hspace{0.17em} e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (D (A))} \leqslant K_{\text{hit}} \hspace{0.17em} C \hspace{0.17em} \frac{q^{\max (1, m)}}{1 - q} .\)

The same bound holds for an arbitrary family of variants with nonnegative weights, each nonzero variant assigned to a connected packet and supported in its dome, provided the sum of the variant weights of each packet is at most \(Ce^{(s - g) |A|}\). For variants, the sum is pinned by the actual support and uses its covering number.

(b) Mixed words.

  • Alphabet. Add optional polymers \((X_i, w_i)_{i \in \iota}\) on \(\Lambda_c\) with \(w \geqslant 0\) and lengths \(\lambda_i \geqslant 0\). A packet has footprint \(D (A)\) and length \(e_s |A|\).

  • Hypotheses (with \(\varepsilon, \delta, \gamma_{\text{root}} > 0\) and \(a, C, e_s, \mu_s \geqslant 0\)):

    • \(0 \leqslant p_A \leqslant Ce^{(s - g) |A|}\) on connected packets;

    • (a) holds with \(a + 2 \varepsilon + \delta\) in place of \(a\), \(s + 2 \varepsilon e_s + \mu_s\) in place of \(s\), and some \(q_p < 1\);

    • \(\text{Hit}_B (w \hspace{0.17em} e^{2 \varepsilon \lambda} ; \hspace{0.17em} a + 2 \varepsilon + \delta, \hspace{0.17em} \gamma_{\text{opt}})\);

    • \(K_{\text{hit}} Cq_p / (1 - q_p) + \gamma_{\text{opt}} \leqslant \gamma_{\text{root}}\);

    • \(4 \hspace{0.17em} \text{ball}_B \hspace{0.17em} \gamma_{\text{root}} / \delta \leqslant q < 1\).

  • Notation. For a finite set \(s\) of packets and optional labels, \(D (s)\) is the union of the footprints, and \(\text{ch} (s) = \sum_{\alpha \in s} (\text{cov}_B (\text{footprint of } \alpha) + \text{length of } \alpha)\).

  • Claim. For all \(m, k, r \in \mathbb{N}\), \(T \in \mathbb{R}\) and every site \(x\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_s \left( \prod_{\alpha \in s} \text{wt} (\alpha) \right) (1 + \text{ch} (s))^k \hspace{0.17em} e^{a \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (D (s))} \leqslant \frac{k! \hspace{0.17em} e^{\varepsilon}}{\varepsilon^k} \hspace{0.27em} e^{- \varepsilon T - \mu_s r} \hspace{0.27em} \frac{\gamma_{\text{root}} \hspace{0.17em} q^m}{1 - q},\)

the sum over connected \(s\) with \(x \in D (s)\), \(|s| \geqslant m + 1\), at least \(r\) seeds in total, and \(\text{ch} (s) \geqslant T\). Packets that are not connected have weight \(0\).

Status: theorem. The hypotheses of (b) are the records Mixed.Costs.Buy and Mixed.Accounts. (b) is used in III.2 and III.10–III.13. Lean: Packets.packet_income, Packets.packet_variants_tail, Mixed.mixed_hit, Mixed.ready_vertices.

Proposition III.5.8 (the numerical conditions hold at large coupling).

Notation. Fix the gauge group. In (a) and (b), also fix the block side \(L\) and \(l \in \mathbb{N}\). For the coarse coupling \(x\) put:

  • \(\beta_F = x + b \log L\), with \(b\) a constant of the group;

  • \(B = \lceil (\log x)^2 \rceil\);

  • \(P (s) = e^{(\log \log s)^2}\);

  • \(t = \beta_F^{- 1 / 2} P (\beta_F)\), the small-field threshold;

  • \(\text{bill} (x) = 1 + B + (\log \beta_F)^2\);

  • for \(\mu, u > 0\), \(M = \mu u \hspace{0.17em} P (\beta_F)^2\).

(a) Let \(a, D \geqslant 0\), \(d \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(z < 2\). For all sufficiently large \(x\) the following holds. Whenever \(s \leqslant D \hspace{0.17em} \text{bill}^d P (\beta_F)^z\), \(g \geqslant M\) and \(0 \leqslant C \leqslant \exp (D \hspace{0.17em} \text{bill}^d P (\beta_F)^z)\):

  • the numerical condition \(\rho \leqslant q < 1\) of III.5.7(a) holds with \(q = e^{- M / 2}\);

  • \(K_{\text{hit}} Cq^{\max (1, m)} / (1 - q) \leqslant e^{- M \max (1, m) / 4}\) for every \(m\).

If \(\beta \geqslant u \beta_F\), then \(\beta \mu t^2 \geqslant M\).

(b) Let in addition \(\varepsilon, \delta > 0\). For all large \(x\), all \(\beta \geqslant u \beta_F\), and all \(C, e_s \geqslant 0\), \(s \in \mathbb{R}\), and \(\gamma_{\text{opt}}\) with

\(\displaystyle C \leqslant e^{\text{fee}}, \qquad s + 2 \varepsilon e_s \leqslant \text{fee} := D \hspace{0.17em} \text{bill}^d P (\beta_F)^z, \qquad 0 \leqslant \gamma_{\text{opt}} \leqslant \delta / (16 \hspace{0.17em} \text{ball}_B),\)

the numerical inequalities of III.5.7(b) hold with:

  • gain \(g = \beta \mu t^2\);

  • mark \(\mu_s = \mu uP (\beta_F)^2 / 2\) (half of the guaranteed gain is extracted per seed);

  • \(q = 1 / 2\);

  • \(\gamma_{\text{root}} = e^{- \mu uP (\beta_F)^2 / 8} + \gamma_{\text{opt}}\).

The packet envelope and optional site-sum estimate in III.5.7(b) remain hypotheses on the actual weights.

(c) For a step-estimator \(\mathcal{T}\) (III.1), there is \(n\) such that for every \(L \geqslant n\) and all sufficiently large \(W\) there is \(\mu > 0\), chosen before \(l, d, u, a, D, z\), with the following property for every choice of \(l, d, u, a, D, z\) satisfying (a). For all sufficiently large \(x\), every funded step with block side \(L\) and coarse side \(\geqslant 100 (1 + B)\) satisfies the point charge of III.5.11 with constant \(\mu\). For every \(\beta \geqslant u \beta_F\), the numerical conclusions of (a) hold with \(g = \beta \mu t^2\). The threshold in \(x\) may depend on the fixed numerical parameters but not on the volume.

Proposition III.5.9 (logarithm by deletion).

(a) Abstract form.

  • Data. \(J\) finite; \(X : \iota \to\) nonempty subsets of \(J\) (\(\iota\) any set); \(v : \iota \to [0, \infty)\); a weight \(w\) on subsets of \(J\) with \(w \geqslant 1\) and \(w (P \cup P') \leqslant w (P) w (P')\) whenever \(P \cap P' \neq \emptyset\); \(a \geqslant 0\); \(0 \leqslant \delta \leqslant 1 - e^{- a}\).

  • Site condition. For every \(x \in J\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_{i : \hspace{0.17em} x \in X_i} w (X_i) \hspace{0.17em} v_i \hspace{0.17em} e^{a |X_i |} \leqslant \delta .\)

  • Gas and its pieces. For \(u : \iota \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(|u_i | \leqslant v_i\), let \(Z_u (B) = \sum_Q \prod_{i \in Q} u_i\) (\(B \subseteq J\)), the sum over finite families \(Q\) of labels with pairwise disjoint supports contained in \(B\). Let \(\lambda_u\) be the Möbius pieces of \(- \log Z_u\).

  • Claim.

    • \(Z_u (B) > 0\) and \(Z_u (B) = \exp \left( - \sum_{A \subseteq B} \lambda_u (A) \right)\) for every \(B\).

    • \(| \lambda_u (A) | \leqslant \lambda_{- v} (A)\), and \(\sum_{A \ni x} w (A) \hspace{0.17em} \lambda_{- v} (A) \leqslant a\) for every \(x\).

    • For every \(Q \subseteq J\), \(Z_u (J \setminus Q) = Z_u (J) \hspace{0.17em} e^l\), with \(l = \sum_{A \cap Q \neq \emptyset} \lambda_u (A)\) and \(|l| \leqslant a |Q|\).

(b) At a coarse field.

  • Data. Let \(\iota\) be countable, with nonempty supports \(X_i\). Let \(E_i (V)\) (\(i \in \iota\)) be functions of the gauge field on \(\Lambda_n\). Each is local in \(X_i\), gauge invariant and Borel, with \(|E_i (V) | \leqslant v_i\) whenever no site of \(X_i\) is flagged. Let \(\tilde{E}_i\) be \(E_i\) set to \(0\) when \(X_i\) contains a flagged site.

  • Hypotheses. \(M \in \mathbb{N}\), \(A, a \geqslant 0\), \(v_i \geqslant 0\), \(a (2 M + 1)^4 \leqslant 1\), \(\delta \leqslant 1 - e^{- a}\), and \(\sum_{i : \hspace{0.17em} x \in X_i} e^{(A + 1) \text{cov}_M (X_i)} v_i \leqslant \delta\) for every \(x\).

  • Claim. The gas \(Z_V\) of \(\tilde{E} (V)\) satisfies, for every \(V\) and every \(Q\):

    • \(Z_V (Q) > 0\) and \(Z_V (Q) = \exp \left( - \sum_{P \subseteq Q} \lambda_P (V) \right)\);

    • \(| \lambda_P (V) | \leqslant g (P)\), with \(g\) independent of \(V\), and \(\sum_{P \ni x} e^{A \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_M (P)} g (P) \leqslant a\);

    • \(Z_V (\Lambda_n \setminus Q) = Z_V (\Lambda_n) e^l\) with \(l = \sum_{P \cap Q \ne \emptyset} \lambda_P (V)\) and \(|l| \leqslant a |Q|\);

    • there are gauge-invariant Borel functions \(\lambda_P^{\text{raw}}\), local in the links with both endpoints in \(P\), such that \(\lambda_P (V) =\boldsymbol{1} [P \text{ contains no flagged site}] \lambda_P^{\text{raw}} (V)\). Thus the masked coefficients also use the flag tests on \(P\).

Status: theorem. (b) is used in III.11. Lean: ReLog.catalog, ReLog.omitted, ReLog.swept, ReLog.intake, ReLog.retest, ReLog.retain_mask.

Proposition III.5.10 (trace-log of a near-identity product). Let \(A\) be positive definite and \(C\) symmetric (real matrices), and \(S = AC - 1\) with \(0 \leqslant q < 1\) and \(\sum_j |S_{ij} | \leqslant q\) for every \(i\). Then:

  • \(\det C > 0\) and \(\det (1 + S) > 0\);

  • \(\sum_{n \geqslant 1} \frac{(- 1)^{n + 1}}{n} \hspace{0.17em} \text{tr} \hspace{0.17em} S^n\) converges absolutely, and its sum is \(\log \det (1 + S)\).

Status: theorem. Used in III.4. Lean: Round.exact_trace, Round.twin_log.

Proposition III.5.11 (sector decomposition of a block-spin step; gain per seed).

Setting. Fix a step \(S\) with block side \(L\), a step-estimator \(\mathcal{T}\), a tube \(T\) with \(\text{Funds} (S, T)\) (III.1), a coarse coupling \(x\) with threshold \(t\), and constants \(W, \kappa > 0\).

Sector of a packet. Say \((U, k, V)\) satisfies the sector prerequisites of \(A\) when:

  • each fine cell whose projected vertices meet \(D_{5 B} (A)\) has plaquette chord \(\geqslant t / 4\) if listed in \(A\), and \(\leqslant t / 2\) otherwise;

  • each coarse bond with an endpoint in \(D_{5 B} (A)\) has predicted wire chord \(\geqslant Wt\) if listed in \(A\), and \(\leqslant 2 Wt\) otherwise;

  • on \(D_{5 B} (A)\), the sites listed in \(A\) are exactly the sites flagged by \(V\) at the coarse threshold \(t_C = x^{- 1 / 2} e^{(\log \log x)^2}\);

  • the frame tag has positive frame mass at each site of \(D_{5 B} (A)\).

3. Strategy

For a finite gas, inclusion–exclusion writes the partition function as a sum over tuples. Splitting off the connected component of a distinguished occurrence gives the coefficient recurrence \((m + 1) g_{m + 1} = \sum_{k = 0}^m (k + 1) \ell_k g_{m - k}\). For the gas polynomial \(P (t) = \sum_n g_n t^n\) and cluster series \(L (t) = \sum_m \ell_m t^{m + 1}\), this becomes \(P' = L' P\). A geometric bound on the cluster coefficients justifies differentiation on an interval containing \([0, 1]\); then \(Pe^{- L} = 1\). Absolute bounds allow passage to arbitrary label sets through the directed family of finite subsets.

The bounds combine the tree-graph inequality with a sum over trees. A parent support \(Y\) meets labels of total inflated weight at most \(|Y| \gamma \leqslant \text{ball}_B \gamma \hspace{0.17em} \text{cov}_B (Y)\). The reserve exponent \(\delta\) absorbs each parent’s number of children through \(x^k \leqslant k!e^x\). Substochastic kernels sum the labels; the sum of the resulting child factorials over parent maps is at most \(4^m m!\). After cancellation of the occurrence factorial, the ratio per added vertex is \(4 \hspace{0.17em} \text{ball}_B \gamma / \delta\). Subadditivity of the covering number transfers these bounds to cluster unions. The resulting estimates retain site pins, tails, deleted regions and repeated forced polymers.

For seed packets, a spanning-walk count gives an entropy factor \(K_{\text{step}}^2\) per seed. The point charge supplies an energy gain \(\beta \kappa t^2\) per seed. Since \(t = \beta_F^{- 1 / 2} P (\beta_F)\) and \(\beta \geqslant u \beta_F\), the guaranteed gain is \(\kappa uP (\beta_F)^2\). At fixed \(L\), this dominates every cost \(\text{bill}^d P (\beta_F)^z\) with \(z < 2\). Half the gain can be retained as a mark for later estimates. The partition of unity over global seed records then splits the block-spin pushforward while preserving a common field, tag array and tree-gauge slice.

The deletion construction obtains another logarithm from the region recurrence. Its negative-logarithm pieces form a union-convolution series pinned at a chosen site. A weighted \(\ell^1\) bound closes induction on the region and compares signed activities with the gas of negative envelope activities. The final matrix estimates use a transported flat linearization: a gauge-fixed Poincaré inequality and a small-background comparison give a uniform spectral gap, while path bounds give weighted row and column estimates. Exponential conjugation yields inverse decay, and principal window inverses approximate the full inverse with error \(O (e^{- cB})\).

4.2 Tree-graph bound

Lemma 4.5 (Penrose partition; III.5.2). The absolute connected-graph sum is bounded by the number of spanning trees. Sketch:

  • To each connected spanning \(G\) attach its breadth-first tree from the distinguished vertex, breaking ties by a fixed enumeration.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The tree-graph argument is standard. The estimates must also retain arbitrary label sets, repeated occurrences, site pins and covering weights at the growing scale \(B = \lceil (\log x)^2 \rceil\). The entropy factor \(4 (2 B + 1)^4\) is sufficient because it grows only polynomially in \(\log x\).

The gain scale is \(P (\beta_F)^2 = e^{2 (\log \log \beta_F)^2}\). It absorbs \(\text{bill}^d P (\beta_F)^z\) for fixed \(d\) and \(z < 2\), but grows more slowly than every positive power of \(\beta_F\). The deletion route gives support-indexed logarithm coefficients and a common field-independent envelope; the graph route also gives occurrence tails and multiset bounds. Both give deletion ratios uniform in volume, although the full logarithm is extensive.

The sector estimate needs global seed records to preserve the local point-charge prerequisites under lattice orientations. Its remainder \(\ell_A\) has no volume-uniform bound here. For the Gaussian estimates, the substantive inputs are the gauge-fixed Poincaré inequality and control of the transported flat rows away from the flagged region. Finite range grows with \(B\); uniform exponential row bounds provide the useful control.
