1. What this unit proves

Except for group statements, assume \(S\) satisfies the Euclidean axioms: permutation symmetry, empty normalisation, unit deletion, translation invariance, \(B_4\)- and conjugation-covariance, coordinate reflection positivity for point words and smeared tests, smoothness, and for each arity, labels and derivative order \(j\), constants \(C \geqslant 0, A \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(\|D^j S (x)\| \leqslant Cr^{- A}\) whenever \(0 < r \leqslant 1\) and maximum-norm separation is at least \(r\), uniformly in positions. The plan consists of complex-linear maps \(\partial_a : F_d \to F_{d + 1}\). Its three laws are: derivative law \(S (\ldots, \partial_a v_k, \ldots ; x) = \partial_{x_k^a} S (v ; x)\), inclusion law \(S (\ldots, \iota v_k, \ldots) = S (v ; x)\), and orientation: inclusions commute with \(M, \Gamma\), derivatives commute with \(\Gamma\), and \(M_{\sigma} \partial_b = \sum_a \sigma_{ab} \partial_a M_{\sigma}\).

The raw maps are complex-linear \(p_a : F_d \to F_{d + 1}\) and real-linear \(Y : \mathfrak{so} (4) \to \text{End}_{\mathbb{C}} F_d\); spin rows \(T\) have the latter type. Write (R) for: \(J_{ab} \in F_4\) is symmetric, \(\Gamma\)-real, \(\sum_a \partial_a J_{ab} = 0\) in \(F_5\), \(M_{\sigma} J_{ab} = \sum_{ij} \sigma_{ia} \sigma_{jb} J_{ij}\), and \((J ; p, Y)\) obeys the stress laws. For every separated \((x_0, \ldots, x_n)\), labels \(B, q\), and real \(\chi \in C_c^{\infty}\) equal to \(1\) near \(x_0\) with closed support excluding the other points, these laws for \((J ; P, A)\) are

\(\displaystyle - \sum_{a, b} \int \partial_a \chi (y) V_b (y) S (J_{ab} (y), B (x_0), q) \hspace{0.17em} dy = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} S (P_j B (x_0), q), & V = e_j,\\ S (A_K B (x_0), q), & V = K (y - x_0) . \end{array} \right.\)

All identities concern separated insertions; no contact term is evaluated. Define

\(\displaystyle \mathcal{W}_K [T, P] (q ; x) = \sum_i \left[ S (\ldots, T_K q_i, \ldots ; x) + \sum_b (Kx_i)_b S (\ldots, P_b q_i, \ldots ; x) \right], \qquad \mathcal{W}_K [T] =\mathcal{W}_K [T, \partial] .\)

Proposition VI.1.1. Assume the derivative and orientation laws and (R). For every arity \(n\), degree \(d\), coordinate axis \(\mathbb{R}e_l\), every \(x \in \text{Conf}_n\) on that axis, all tags \(q\) and \(K \in \mathfrak{so} (4)\), \(\mathcal{W}_K [Y, p] (q ; x) = 0\). For every \(j \ne l\) and \(v_1, \ldots, v_n \in F_d\), \(\sum_i S (\ldots, p_j v_i, \ldots ; x) = 0\). Status: theorem. Lean: Outer.outer, Bid.LineSum.

Proposition VI.1.2. Assume all three plan laws, the ultraviolet record and free-Maxwell data of §2. There exists real \(c \ne 0\), independent of degree and axis, with \(p_a = c \partial_a\) on every \(F_d\). For all degrees, axes and \(K\), the rows \(D = c^{- 1} Y\) satisfy (C) \(\partial_b D_K + \sum_i K_{ib} \partial_i = D_K \partial_b\) and (L) \(\mathcal{W}_K [D] = 0\) on every coordinate axis, for all labels and \(K\). Status: theorem. Lean: Bid.pressured, Bid.formed.

Proposition VI.1.3. Assume the derivative law and spin rows \(T\) satisfying (C),(L). Then \(\mathcal{W}_K [T] (q ; x) = 0\) for every arity, separated configuration, tags and \(K\). Status: theorem. Lean: Ward.global.

Proposition VI.1.4. Assume the inclusion and derivative laws, VI.1.3's conclusion, and compatibility of \(T\): it commutes with \(\Gamma, \iota\), kills \(\boldsymbol{1}\), and \(M_{\sigma} T_K = T_{\sigma K \sigma^{- 1}} M_{\sigma}\). Assume internal row erasure: for some \(N\), every \(d \geqslant N\), and every alternating real-bilinear family \(\Delta (u, v) \in \text{End}_{\mathbb{C}} F_d\) that is \(\Gamma\)-real, covariant under determinant-one signed permutations, kills \(\boldsymbol{1}\) and preserves every \(\iota_{e \to d} (F_e)\), \(e \leqslant d\), vanishing insertion sums for all words, separated configurations and \(u, v\) imply \(\Delta = 0\). Then \([T_u, T_v] = T_{[u, v]}\) for all \(d, u, v\). Status: theorem; erasure is proved here. Lean: Work.A08.answer.

Proposition VI.1.5. Under VI.1.2's hypotheses, for every \(d\) there is a complex-linear isomorphism \(V_d : F_d^{\text{free}} \to F_d\) intertwining \(M, \Gamma\) and satisfying \(D_K V_d = V_d \mathcal{L}_K^{\text{free}}\) for every \(K\). Thus \(R_d = V_d R_d^{\text{free}} V_d^{- 1}\) is a continuous \(O (4)\) representation, equals \(M\) on \(B_4\), commutes with \(\Gamma\), has generators \(D\) satisfying the global Ward identity of VI.1.3. The \(V_d\) are chosen independently across degrees. Such an action with specified generators is a rotation port. Status: theorem. Lean: Bid.services.

Proposition VI.1.6. Assume the inclusion and derivative laws and a rotation port \(R\) with generators \(T\) satisfying VI.1.3's global Ward identity. For all \(k \in O (4)\), tags and separated \(x\), \(S (R (k) v ; kx) = S (v ; x)\). Moreover \(R\) fixes \(\boldsymbol{1}\), commutes with inclusions, retains the port properties, and \(R_{d + 1} (k) \partial_a v = \sum_i k_{ia} \partial_i R_d (k) v\) for every \(d, a, v\). Status: theorem. Lean: Work.A14.answer.

Proposition VI.1.7. Assume the derivative law, a covariant rotation action satisfying VI.1.6's laws, (R), \(p = c \partial\) with \(c \ne 0\), and generators \(c^{- 1} Y\). There exists symmetric, real, divergence-free \(J'\) with \(R_4 (k) J'_{ab} = \sum_{ij} k_{ia} k_{jb} J'_{ij}\) for every \(k\), satisfying the stress laws with \((P, A) = (\partial, c^{- 1} Y)\). The translation shell, Ward identity (13), therefore equals \(\partial_{x_0^b} S (B (x_0), q)\) for every window and labels. Status: theorem. Lean: Work.A15.answer.

Proposition VI.1.8. Spatial rotations and coordinate boosts generate \(SO^+ (1, 3)\). Under VI.1.2's hypotheses, choose \(V, R, J'\) as above: \(B_d (\Lambda) = V_d B_d^{\text{free}} (\Lambda) V_d^{- 1}\) commutes with every inclusion \(F_d \hookrightarrow F_e\), \(d \leqslant e\), for every \(\Lambda\). These actions give a representation on the union, continuous on each \(F_d\), equal to \(R_d\) on spatial rotations and commuting with \(M_{\theta_t} \Gamma\). For every \(d\), spatial \(i\) and \(v \in F_d\), its boost derivative at zero is \(- iD_{K^{(3 i)}} v\). Status: theorems. Lean: Work.A16.answer, Work.A17.answer, Spin.reach.

Proposition VI.1.9. Given §2's free data, infrared triple and ultraviolet record, all six assembly clauses hold: raw compatibility, normalisation, rotation descent, covariance, averaged stress laws, and Lorentz compatibility. With the inclusion and derivative laws, there exist \(c, (V_d), J'\) satisfying VI.1.2 and VI.1.5–8, all three plan laws and the resulting local field laws. Status: conditional theorem. Lean: Outer.commission, Commission.picks.

3. Strategy

Conservation turns shell laws into collinear Ward identities; free comparison fixes their normalisation. Positivity converts collinear two-point jets into analytic OS vectors, allowing propagation to every separated configuration. Exact intertwiners transport the free rotations; covariance then gives compatibility across degrees. Rescaling and averaging give the tensorial stress, and boost ODE uniqueness gives Lorentz compatibility.

4. Steps

  1. Moving the centre inside a fixed window gives \(\partial_b Y_K + \sum_c K_{cb} p_c = Y_K \partial_b\) and \(\partial_b p_i = p_i \partial_b\) by differentiating its shell laws. Work.A02.answer.

  2. Cutoffs of widths \(\lambda^{100}, \lambda^{150}, \lambda^{300}\) cancel two terms by stress symmetry; reflection positivity and spectral estimates bound the exterior flux by \(O (\lambda^{- 1})\), giving the collinear identity without a mass gap or clustering. Outer.blows. (supporting analysis partly read)

  3. Finite free collinear tests have a one-dimensional kernel; persistence of rank under scaling forces \(p = c \partial\). Climb.extends_form.

  4. Analytic comparison determines two-point jets; positivity gives factorial vector-derivative bounds. Extreme-point moves and density propagate vanishing angular sums. Analysis.blossoms, Ward.global.

  5. Free row erasure removes the bracket defect. Relative-Casimir projection and iteration \(x \mapsto 3 x^2 - 2 x^3\) produce exact intertwiners: the compressed defect satisfies perfect commutator relations, which force sufficiently small operators to vanish. Work.A08.answer, Spin.meet.

  6. Group generation integrates covariance; Haar averaging of \(c^{- 1} J\) preserves the stress laws. Boost ODE uniqueness proves compatibility across degrees. Work.A14.answer, Work.A15.answer, run_boost.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Diagonal stress components require a conservation estimate beyond reflection-parity arguments. Vanishing smooth jets alone do not justify continuation; positivity supplies analyticity. Exact intertwiners identify the required \(O (4)\) action and signed permutations.

6. Notation

\(S\): correlation table; \(F_d\): finite-dimensional complex label spaces of degree \(\leqslant d\) modulo null labels; \(\iota\): inclusions; \(\boldsymbol{1}\): unit; \(B_4, M\): signed permutations and their action; \(\Gamma\): conjugation; \(\text{Conf}_n\): distinct points in \(\mathbb{R}^4\). \(K \in \mathfrak{so} (4)\) is real skew, with \([u, v] = uv - vu\) and \(K^{(ba)} = E_{ba} - E_{ab}\); time is axis \(3\), and \(\theta_t\) reverses it. \(\lambda = e^k\) is the cutoff scale.

7. What remains unexplained

Supporting flux analysis, including contact-extension normalisation, was not checked fully (probable). The ultraviolet hypotheses of the input producers are not discharged here. Centring of \(J'\) is not proved here; neither sign of \(c, \kappa\) is specified. Lean elaboration and transitive axiom dependencies were not checked.
