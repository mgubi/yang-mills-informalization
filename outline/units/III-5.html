1. What this unit proves

All propositions are theorems under the stated hypotheses. Infinite nonnegative bounds include summability.

Proposition III.5.1. For finite labels, reflexive symmetric incompatibility \(R\) and real \(w\), put \(\ell_m = \sum_{z \in \iota^{m + 1}} \phi_R^T (z) \prod_a w_{z_a} / (m + 1) !\). If \(| \ell_m | \leqslant Cq^m\) for every \(m \geqslant 0\), \(C \geqslant 0\), \(0 < q < 1\), then \(\Xi (w) = \exp (\sum_m \ell_m)\). Gas.exact_polynomial

Proposition III.5.2. On every finite graph with a distinguished vertex, the absolute alternating sum over connected spanning subgraphs is bounded by its number of spanning trees. Graph.pay

Proposition III.5.3. For arbitrary labels, supports \(X_i \subseteq \Lambda_n\), \(B \in \mathbb{N}\), \(w \geqslant 0\), \(a \geqslant 0\), \(R \subseteq\) overlap and \(\text{Ready} (w ; a, \gamma, \delta, q)\), every site \(x\) and \(m \geqslant 0\) satisfy

\(\displaystyle \sum_{k \geqslant m} \sum_{z \in \iota^{k + 1} : x \in X (z)} \frac{| \phi_R^T (z) | \prod_a w_{z_a}}{(k + 1) !} e^{a \text{cov}_B (X (z))} \leqslant \frac{\gamma q^m}{1 - q} .\)

If \(Q \subseteq \iota\) has total inflated weight \(\sum_{i \in Q} w_i e^{(a + \delta) \text{cov}_B (X_i)} \leqslant b\), the order-\(m\) cluster sum marked by \(Q\), with individual covering weights, is \(\leqslant bq^m\). Proposition III.5.4. For the same supports, real \(w\), reflexive symmetric \(R \subseteq\) overlap, \(a \geqslant 0\) and \(\text{Ready} (|w| ; a, \gamma, \delta, q)\), the gas converges absolutely with absolute sum \(\leqslant e^{n^4 \gamma}\); its cluster series converges absolutely to \(\log \Xi (w)\), with absolute sum \(\leqslant n^4 \gamma / (1 - q)\) and order-\(m\) bound \(n^4 \gamma q^m\). 3. Strategy

Component decomposition identifies the finite-gas logarithm. Tree estimates give geometric cluster tails; absolute convergence extends the identity to arbitrary labels. Seed energy dominates packet entropy. Deletion gives another logarithm; exponential conjugation and the spectral gap control windowed inverses.

4. Steps

  1. For gas polynomial \(P\) and cluster series \(L\), splitting the distinguished component gives \(P' = L' P\), hence \(Pe^{- L} = 1\). Coef.recurrence

  2. Penrose intervals cancel free edges; parent factorials cost at most \(4^m m!\), absorbed by the reserve exponent. Graph.pay Trees.trees_afford

  3. Count packets by spanning walks and compare their entropy with \(P (\beta_F)^2\). Packets.stores_count Delay.fits

  4. Deletion induction bounds Möbius logarithm pieces by negative-envelope pieces. ReLog.swept

  5. Sum allocations, then extract the orientation-averaged point charge. Oriented.turn_charge

  6. Poincaré and background comparison give the spectral gap; conjugation gives decay, and window residuals occur beyond \(R\). Aff.surplus Windows.window_price

5. Where the difficulty lies

The bounds retain pins, covering weights and repeated labels. Global seed tests survive orientations. The sector remainder has no volume-uniform bound here.

6. Notation

\(\Lambda_n = (\mathbb{Z}/ n\mathbb{Z})^4\), \(n \geqslant 1\); \(d\) is maximum distance; \(\text{cov}_B\) is connected ball-cover number; \(\text{ball}_B = (2 B + 1)^4\). \(\text{Hit}_B (w ; a, \gamma)\) means every site sum of \(w_i e^{a \text{cov}_B (X_i)}\) is \(\leqslant \gamma\). \(\text{Ready}\) adds \(\gamma, \delta > 0\), \(4 \text{ball}_B \gamma / \delta \leqslant q < 1\), using exponent \(a + \delta\).

\(\Xi\) sums compatible finite sets; \(\phi^T\) is the connected spanning alternating sum on the occurrence graph (edges require mutual \(R\)); singleton coefficient \(1\); \(X (z) = \cup_a X_{z_a}\). Order \(m\) means \(m + 1\) occurrences. Seeds are fine cells, coarse bonds or sites; packets are finite seed sets; \(D_r (A)\) is the radius-\(r\) neighbourhood of seed locations (projected cell vertices, bond endpoints, or site). Skins are individual radius-\(lB\) neighbourhoods; \(D (A)\) is their union, connectedness their overlap connectivity. \(\tau_L = L^4 \text{card} \{ \text{plaquette orientations} \} + 5\), \(K_{\text{hit}} = (2 lB + 3)^4 \tau_L\), \(K_{\text{step}} = (4 lB + 5)^4 \tau_L\).

\(\beta_F = x + b \log L\), \(B = \lceil (\log x)^2 \rceil\), \(P (s) = e^{(\log \log s)^2}\), \(t = \beta_F^{- 1 / 2} P (\beta_F)\), \(\text{bill} = 1 + B + (\log \beta_F)^2\). \(\lambda_u\) denotes Möbius pieces of \(- \log Z_u\). \(\mathcal{E}_A\) assesses energy anchored in \(D_{2 B} (A)\) with truncated walks. \(\pi_{\beta}\) is the inverse product of single-bond normalizations; \(\ell_A\) is the remaining absolute sector integral. \(D\) is the transported flat row matrix, using truncated curl/path rows and linearized block constraints, replica transport and centred path gauges in every orientation. Keep row centres outside \(D_{8 B} (A)\) and nontrunk columns with projected endpoints outside \(D_{10 B} (A)\); normalize by the square root of the orientation count. Its range is \(\lfloor B / 100000 \rfloor + R_{\text{pred}} + 2\). Weighted sums at rate \(\eta\) use factors \(e^{\eta d (a_i, a_j)}\). A windowed inverse uses column \(j\) of the principal inverse on \(P (j)\), extended by zero.
