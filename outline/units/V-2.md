## 1. What this unit proves

All arities include zero; statements hold for every label tuple and Schwartz test unless restricted. Tables $S,T$ share a register; $T$ respects $S$ means $N_d(S)\subseteq N_d(T)$ for every degree, where $N_d$ consists of labels vanishing with arbitrary insertions. Write (H) for: both Euclidean hierarchies; respect; differential-plan inclusions compatible with both tables and derivatives with $T$; a spread satisfying the slab bound, smoothness in insertion positions on every valid slab, and insertion derivative laws. The slab bound is $\|A_d(w)\|\le C^{|w|}(|w|/r)^{a|w|}$ for buffers and pairwise maximum-norm distances at least $r\in(0,1]$, with $C>0$, $a\in\mathbb N$ uniform in width and length for fixed degree and representative-norm bounds.

(SC) means continuity in strict complex times and spatial positions, holomorphy in times, and agreement with $T$ at ordered imaginary times. (PG) means $|F_n^y(x)(v)|\le Ck^{-m}$ whenever all height gaps are at least $k\in(0,1]$, with $C\ge0$, $m\in\mathbb N$ depending on $n,v$. Put $\psi_v(y;f)=\int F_n^y(x)(v)f(x)\,dx$; (I), (HC), (CR) mean integrability, continuity on $E_n$ and $\partial_a\psi(y;f)=\psi(y;-i\partial_{\underline a}f)$ for every real height direction $a$ within $E_n$. (BV) means $\psi_v(y;f)\to W_n(v)(f)$ as $y\to0$ through ordered heights, without a fixed direction.

## 3. Strategy

Bounded slab insertions construct strict-time correlations. Boundary values and boosts give covariant distributions with forward spectral support. Fourier–Laplace continuation and permutation symmetry give locality. Heated vectors transfer positivity and translations to the word Hilbert space, where left multiplication defines fields.

<a id="unit-steps"></a>

## 4. Steps

1. Heat factors telescope, removing the slab width; contractions give polynomial growth. [C02.matrix_pad](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/C02.lean#L156-L166).
2. Taylor's integral remainder controls boundary limits uniformly over nondecreasing heights. [Waves.maintains](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Shift/Breath.lean#L78-L101).
3. Continue rotation identities, then integrate boost derivatives to obtain covariance. [C09.boost_invariant](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/C09.lean#L374-L399).
4. The Laplace identity forces nonnegative suffix energies; Lorentz covariance upgrades this to cone support. [tents](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Shift/Closing.lean#L106-L121).
5. Fourier–Laplace damping extends $F$ to the tube. Exponential decay gives complex differentiability in Schwartz space. [C13.analytic_interior](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/C13.lean#L113-L125).
6. Imaginary boosts construct two germs; equal Wick-slice jets identify them, yielding boundary exchange. [C20.complex_germ](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/C20.lean#L67-L87).
7. Gram limits give an isometric embedding into the OS space; left multiplication defines fields, and the embedding transfers positive energy. [universal_embed](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Fields/Motion.lean#L31-L53).

<a id="unit-difficulty"></a>

## 5. Where the difficulty lies

Boost derivatives require finite-dimensional label levels and Schwartz estimates. Locality requires complete jets at equal pair times. Independent heating scales require unrestricted boundary limits.

## 6. Notation

$\mathcal H,\Omega,H\ge0,U$: OS space, vacuum, Hamiltonian and translations. $\mathcal F=\bigcup_d F_d$: quotient labels; $J$: reflection adjoint; $b^*$: reversed starred word. $E_n=\{y_1<\cdots<y_n\}$; $F_n^y(x)=F_n(x^0+iy;\vec x)$; $\underline y=((\vec0,y_k))_k$. $\mathcal T_n=\{z:\mathrm{Im}(z_{k+1}-z_k)\in V_+\}$, with unrestricted first imaginary point. $V_+=\{x:|\vec x|<x^0\}$. Pairings are Euclidean; $\tilde\Lambda=\mathsf r\Lambda\mathsf r$, with spatial reversal $\mathsf r$. $\mathcal L^\uparrow_+$ is the proper orthochronous Lorentz group; $K$ is its label action. The closed imaginary cone replaces $V_+$ by $\bar V_+$. $\mathcal FW(f)=W(\hat f)$; Fourier phases are $e^{-i\langle x,p\rangle}$, inverse normalization $(2\pi)^{-4n}$. $g_*f=f\circ g^{-1}$ slotwise; $\rho$ is the rotation Lie action.
