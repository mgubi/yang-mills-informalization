1. What this unit proves

For VII.4.1–4 assume (RG): grid approximants, simple gauge Lie algebra, per-step and cluster RG estimates, and the continuum extraction record. Fix any coherent prescription and its real covariant Reader on the final record; VIII.1 supplies these data. Its strata \(E_j \subset F_j\) have bases \(O_{j, \kappa}\) fixed by conjugation, with coefficients \(c_{j, \kappa}\).

An \((r, M, \eta)\)-cluster satisfies \(|x_i - o| \leqslant Mr\), \(|x_i - x_{i'} | \geqslant \eta r\) for \(i \ne i'\). Spectators are \(c\)-clear if \(|y_j - o| \geqslant c\), \(|y_j - y_{j'} | \geqslant c\) for \(j \ne j'\). Small-radius thresholds are uniform in configurations and states.

Proposition VII.4.1 (separated OPE). Fix finite label lists \(a, b\), \(M \geqslant 0\), \(\eta, c, \varepsilon > 0\), and \(D \in \mathbb{N}\). For all sufficiently small \(r\), every such cluster and clear spectator configuration gives separated \((x, y)\) and \((o, y)\) and

\(\displaystyle \|\mathcal{E}_D \|_{\mathcal{A}} \leqslant r^{D + 1 - w (a) - \varepsilon}, \qquad \mathcal{E}_D = M (a, x ; b, y) - \sum_{j \leqslant D, \kappa} c_{j, \kappa} (a ; x, o) M ((j, O_{j, \kappa}), o ; b, y) .\)

The same bound holds in every phase and the mixed state. For each fixed channel \((j, \kappa)\), \(|c_{j, \kappa} | \leqslant r^{j - w (a) - \varepsilon}\) uniformly on clusters for sufficiently small \(r\). Coefficients are independent of spectators, state and \(D\). Status: theorem. Lean: Orbits.Foundation.

Proposition VII.4.2 (integrated remainder). With VII.4.1's fixed data, let \(K\) be a measurable space, \(\mu\) a positive measure, \(q \in L^1 (\mu ; \mathbb{C})\), \(\int |q| \hspace{0.17em} d \mu \leqslant B\), \(B \geqslant 0\). Let configurations be a.e.-measurable and satisfy the geometry a.e. on \(\{q \ne 0\}\). For all sufficiently small \(r\), for the mixed state or any a.e.-measurable phase map, the weighted correlation, truncation and remainder are integrable, and \(| \int q \hspace{0.17em} s (\mathcal{E}_D) \hspace{0.17em} d \mu | \leqslant Br^{D + 1 - w (a) - \varepsilon}\). The pointwise threshold works for every \(K, B\); every probability mixture of phases satisfies the remainder bound of VII.4.1. Status: theorem. Lean: estimates_weighted.

Proposition VII.4.3 (nested OPE). Fix a finite rooted tree with inner root, leaf labels \(v\), output \((m, \kappa)\), inner truncations \(D_p\), \(M \geqslant 0\), \(\eta, \varepsilon > 0\). A plot assigns leaf points and inner anchors \(z_p\); positive radii satisfy \(s_{\text{root}} = r\), \(s_p \leqslant s_{\text{parent} (p)}\). Every subtree lies within \(Ms_p\) of \(z_p\); positions in different children are separated by \(\eta s_p\). Put \(\rho_p = s_p / s_{\text{parent} (p)}\), and let \(w_p\) be the degree below \(p\). Over non-root inner nodes define

\(\displaystyle \text{toll}_m = r^{m - w (v) - \varepsilon} \prod_p \rho_p^{- w_p - \varepsilon}, \qquad \text{nest}_D = \sum_p \rho_p^{D_p + 1} .\)

Writing \(c_{m, \kappa} = c_{m, \kappa} (v ; z_{\text{leaves}}, z_{\text{root}})\), for all sufficiently small \(r\), \(|c_{m, \kappa} | \leqslant \text{toll}_m\) and \(|c_{m, \kappa} - c^{(D)}_{m, \kappa} | \leqslant \text{toll}_m \text{nest}_D\). Here \(c^{(D)}\) recursively multiplies node coefficients and sums inner-child channels through \(D_p\), leaving leaves unchanged; the root output is fixed and \(D_{\text{root}}\) is unused. For fixed spectators \(b\) and \(\delta > 0\), separated mutually and from every plot point by \(\delta\), the degree-\(m\) remainder has norm, and absolute value in every state, at most \(\text{toll}_{m + 1}\).

For a.e.-measurable plots and spectators satisfying this geometry a.e. on \(\{q \ne 0\}\), VII.4.2's integration conclusion holds: direct and composed coefficients are integrable after weighting, with \(| \int qc \hspace{0.17em} d \mu | \leqslant B \text{toll}_m\) and \(| \int q (c - c^{(D)}) \hspace{0.17em} d \mu | \leqslant B \text{toll}_m \text{nest}_D\). Weighted tested correlations, truncations and remainders are integrable, with remainder bound \(B \text{toll}_{m + 1}\) for the mixed state or any a.e.-measurable phase map. Radii remain fixed during integration; thresholds are uniform in radii, measures, weights, \(B\) and configuration maps. Status: theorem. Lean: Nesting.Uniform.

Proposition VII.4.4 (contacts). Fix contact devices sharing the Reader's prescription and the final record's rotations. For \(k\) labels \(a_i \in F_{d_i}\), any state \(s\), and \(0 < s_0 \leqslant B_0 / 2\), \(B_0 = \min (1, \text{cl} (w (a)))\), the forest kernel defines a distribution \(\mathcal{D}_a^s\) of order at most \((2 + w (a)) k\): on each compact \(K\), its absolute integral is bounded by \(C_K \|f\|_{C^{(2 + w (a)) k}}\). It equals \(\int fS^s (a ; x) \hspace{0.17em} dx\) for tests supported on distinct configurations. Distributions also exist on slices \(o + P (E)\), with \(P\) a linear isometry whose range contains a group diagonal's normal space, and \(o\) on that diagonal.

Shell and iterated forest sums give the pairing, with integrable kernels and summable \(L^1\) norms, under these stage-family hypotheses: a partition \(Q\) of \(g\); affine isometric placements based on block diagonals and containing their normals; positive subtraction lengths \(s\); \(R_i \in (0, 1]\); common ceiling \(2 s \leqslant B \leqslant \min (1, \text{cl} (\sum_i d_i))\). A common frame scales each block normal by \(R_i\) and preserves its orthogonal complement. Closed stages are bounded in frame coordinates and within normal distance \(B\); nonsingleton blocks satisfy \(R_i \leqslant C_1 s\). Original and spectator labels have uniform norm bounds; spectator positions and labels are continuous on stages and constant in block normal directions. All nonempty subgroup centroids of different blocks and all spectators are uniformly separated. Constants may depend on the fixed family.

For \(Q = \{g\}\), \(g \ne \emptyset\), pure normal dilation \(R \in (0, 1]\), and \(f_R (x) = R^{- 4 (|g| - 1)} f (R^{- 1} x_{K_g} + x_{K_g^{\perp}})\), assume rescaled-stage support and \(\|f\|_{C^N} \leqslant H\), \(N = (2 + \sum_i d_i) (|g| - 1)\). For every \(\varepsilon > 0\), the absolute kernel integral is \(\leqslant CHR^{u - w (a_g) - \varepsilon}\): \(u = 0, n, n + 1\) for full, weight-\(n\), and degree-\(n\) remainder. The latter two require stages within normal distance \(\text{cl} (n)\) of the \(g\)-diagonal. For \(|g| > 1\), tests away from its diagonal agree with the expression retaining proper-subcluster subtractions.

Device strata have bases fixed by conjugation, containing the unit in degree zero. Every positive sequence \(f_n\) admits \(0 < t_n \leqslant f_n\), \(4 t_{n + 1} < t_n\), \(t_n \to 0\), \(2 (n + 1) t_n \leqslant \text{cl} (n)\). Status: theorem. Lean: provided.

Proposition VII.4.5 (slab bound). Assume construction properties, ordered generic point extraction, Euclidean hierarchy, IV.1's corrected field estimates, and the cell hypothesis: reflection positivity and chessboard inequalities at every cutoff, with centred one-field bounds \(\leqslant Aa_m^{- d - \varepsilon} + \eta\) late in the extraction, as in IV.1. For every \(D \in \mathbb{N}\), \(R > 0\), there is \(C_0 > 0\), with \(P = 4 (D + 1)\), such that every nonempty word \(w\), degrees \(\leqslant D\), coefficient norms \(\leqslant R\), \(r\)-buffered in \((0, d)\), \(d > 0\), \(0 < r \leqslant 1\), admits a level \(b\) and width \(0 < s \leqslant d\), \(s \in a_b \mathbb{N}\), containing \(w\) in \((0, s)\). Every positive-time exterior word \(v\) then has \(B_v\) with

\(\displaystyle |S (\Theta v_n \cdot v_n) | \leqslant B_v \rho^{4 n} \quad (n \in \mathbb{N}), \qquad \rho = C_0^{|w|} (|w| / r)^{P |w|},\)

where \(v_n\) precedes \(v\) by \(2 n\) consecutive width-\(s\) slabs holding alternately \(w\) and its reflection. Iterated Schwarz gives \(\|T_s (w)\| \leqslant \rho\) in V.1. Status: theorem. Lean: seats.

Proposition VII.4.6 (prescription). A probe is a finite separated token configuration, anchored at its first point. The correlation maps \(\text{sp}_n\) of anchored fields against probes are injective and inclusion-compatible. For modes indexing bases of \(F_n / \iota (F_{n - 1})\), \(F_{- 1} = 0\), there exist vectors \(u_i \in F_{\deg i}\) and finitely supported complex probe weights \(\lambda_i\), such that for every \(n\), \(\lambda_i^{(n)} (\iota u_j) = \delta_{ij}\) for degrees \(\leqslant n\), and \(\phi = \sum_{\deg i \leqslant n} \lambda_i^{(n)} (\phi) \iota u_i\) for every \(\phi \in F_n\). Here \(\lambda_i^{(n)} = \sum_p \lambda_i (p) \text{sp}_n (\cdot) (p)\). Higher-degree tests vanish on \(F_n\); one prescription serves every truncation. Status: theorem. Lean: Prescription.chosen.

3. Strategy

Probes reconstruct finite-degree fields. RG replaces clusters by fields plus controlled remainders; translated chessboard moments give ground-algebra norm bounds, hence estimates in every phase. Exact reconstruction of replacement fields gives the grid OPE. Derivative induction extends it to arbitrary configurations, and bringing auxiliary scale heights together gives arbitrary \(\varepsilon\)-loss. Recursive replacements yield trees; weight-dependent normal Taylor subtraction makes contact kernels integrable, with partial collisions treated by finer partitions.

4. Steps

  1. Extend biorthogonal probes degree by degree; average rotations and conjugations and remove lower degrees. Reader.

  2. Fuse nearby RG groups, retaining separated groups; obtain a degree-\(t\) replacement with remainder gain \((a_m / a_k)^{t + 1 / 16}\). funding.

  3. Bound translated moments by chessboard roots; full phase support and Gelfand give norm control. Cards.carrying.

  4. Descend in degree from crude high-degree bounds; grid distance compensates the derivative degree increase. interpolated.

  5. Telescope replacements over trees, merging children at the parent's rate and expanding others to sufficiently high auxiliary degree. This controls each node's truncation. Fusion.tariff.

  6. Subtract weight-dependent normal Taylor jets; finer-partition induction handles partial collisions and sums dyadic shells. With \(d_h = \sum_{i \in h} d_i\), \(q_h = 4 (|h| - 1)\), and \(L_h = \max (0, d_h + 1 - q_h)\), subtraction leaves shell exponent \(q_h + L_h - d_h \geqslant 1\). glues_total.

  7. Apply two-scale chessboard bounds to reflection-positive slab trains; choose levels with \(a_k \geqslant c (r / |w|)^4\) to obtain \(P = 4 (D + 1)\). Iterated Schwarz removes the exterior cost. replicas.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The \(1 / 16\) gain margin comes from RG transport. Norm control ensures spectator and state uniformity; partial collisions require more than radial integrability. Estimates allow arbitrary \(\varepsilon\)-loss, without identifying logarithmic corrections.

6. Notation

\(w (a) = \sum_i d_i\); \(F_d\): degree-\(d\) fields; \(\mathcal{A}\): ground algebra; \(M\): compressed word; \(S^s\): correlation for \(s \in X \sqcup \{ \text{mix} \}\), with \(X\) the phase spectrum; \(a_k\): lattice spacing; \(\text{cl} (n)\): device clearance; \(K_g\): normal space of the \(g\)-diagonal.

7. What remains unexplained

The internal polymer estimates and their constants, rotated comparisons, high-horizon tree replacement and partial-collision sector induction need fuller accounts. Device projections are not identified with Reader coefficients. Uniqueness, extraction independence, infinite-order convergence, contact positivity, contact Ward identities and perturbative agreement are not asserted.
