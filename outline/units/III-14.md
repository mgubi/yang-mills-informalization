## 1. What this unit proves

**Proposition III.14.1 (the construction run; main output).** Let $G$ be a simple compact gauge group and $b>0$. Then there exist

- a *run* $R$ (a complete, internally consistent RG construction for $G$: schedule of block factors, kernel choices, histories at all cutoffs $N$ and periods $p$, the Wilson family),
- a *campaign* $T$ for $R$ (the bookkeeping that makes the later extraction and observable estimates available), and
- a family of *pillars* $W$ for $(R,T)$ (retained witnesses: the histories $\text{yields}_{N,p}$ with their step data, and the bounds that later units quote),

such that

1. the penalty of the kernel of the clock of $R$ equals $b$;
2. $R$ is *funded* for the fixed bundle of local-estimate constants called `garment10`. That is, the local budget, attentiveness, divergence of the block lengths ($R.Q\to\infty$), fixed support exponent $100$, the OPE logarithm-on-budget, halo and thermal gates, and existence of the "sky" data all hold simultaneously;
3. the native component recipe is *attached* to $R$. The flat, "sworn" (marked-kernel) and dual suppliers, selected from the original data before any block length, are realized on the very levels and horizon of $R$, with their numerical receipts paid.

*Status:* theorem. The only hypotheses are $G$ simple and $b>0$. The statement says "Finite observable comparisons are proved after this choice".

**Proposition III.14.3 (flat quorum: geometric ultraviolet rate for the quadratic part).** Let $G$ be simple. There are a price map $X$ and floors $E$ such that for every level system $a$ with block factors $\ge E$ and satisfying the delay condition beyond $e$, there exist

- kernels $F_i$ on the faces (one for each stage $i\ge e$), and
- constants $A,B\ge0$, $c>0$, $d,l\in\mathbb N$,

such that for every horizon $H$ sufficient for a price map $P$ and for every reporting scale $k$ the following three statements hold.

- (bounded) For every stage $i\ge e$, the limit kernel $F_i$ has row cost at most $B\,\Lambda_{k,i}^{\,l}$ in the width $w_i$. Here $\Lambda_{k,i}=2+Z_k+|s_i-s_k|$ is the "clock" or age of stage $i$ as seen from scale $k$, with $s_i$ the log-scale of stage $i$.
- (converges) For every stage $i\ge e$, the entries of the quadratic part at stage $i$, computed with Wilson lattice cutoff $n$, converge to $F_i$ as $n\to\infty$.
- (rate) For all $i\le N$, the same row cost bound holds for $\mathrm{output}_{N,i}-F_i$ with
$$A\,\Lambda_{k,i}^{\,d}\,\Big(\frac{a_N}{a_i}\Big)^{c},$$
where $a_N$ is the lattice spacing at cutoff $N$. This is a power rate in the spacing ratio, with polynomial loss in the age, in the *same* width $w_i$ (no loss of width as the number of faces grows).

Here "output" is the full position-space quadratic log-germ coefficient of the effective action (the Hessian convention, no factor $1/2$), at order zero in the vacuum prescription. A "row cost" is the weighted sup, over a test face and a width-$w$ exponential weight, of the row sums of the kernel in the face variable, summed over the other face (see Mesh.lean docstring, scope paragraph).
*Status:* theorem. It does not claim anything about the off-curl two-form extension, positive-order or source kernels (said in the module docstring of [`Mesh.lean`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG08/Flat/Mesh.lean)).

**Proposition III.14.5 (the precision-difference identity for the flat step).** For two cutoffs, the Schur-complement precision difference at the coarse level is the bilinear pullback, through the two (different) means $T,W$, of the precision difference at the fine level:
$$K(Y')-K(H')=W^{\!\top}\big(K(Y)-K(H)\big)\,T .$$
Consequently a folded, neutral, covariant curvature representative at the fine level is pushed to one at the coarse level with the folded radius multiplied by a neutral-coin constant $\ll1$, with no Lipschitz estimate on the Fourier transform at the pole.

## 3. Strategy

The endpoint is an assembly. `constructionRun` calls the general run-selection theorem for the default dynamics and the minted custody data, with the given penalty $b$, and with two extra inputs: the family of native choices (Prop. III.14.2) and its numerical floors. The theorem returns a run, a campaign and pillars together with the funded property. A second theorem, [`of_preselection`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Integration/ActualRunNativeComponentRecipe.lean#L71-L77), then binds the recipe to *that* run: the sheet of the run is the "raised" original sheet, its prices are the "raging" prices, and the recipe's numerical receipts are exactly what the selection theorem was told to ensure. The point of the design, stated in the module docstrings, is that no replacement family, horizon or kernel is chosen after the run: all component estimates are quoted on the run's own levels and horizon.

The mathematics proper is the flat-representation chain, which proves that the *quadratic part of the effective action* converges as $a\to0$ with a rate. This is the part of Balaban's programme that controls the Gaussian (free-field) background in the renormalized region. The idea is a telescoping in the RG scale.

1. At the finest stage $N$ the difference of the quadratic forms at two cutoffs $n\ge N$ is a bounded kernel (`at_end`).
2. One step to a coarser stage multiplies the folded radius of its curvature representative by a small "quote" factor: by the precision-difference identity, the coarse precision difference is the pullback of the fine one by the means $T,W$ of the blocking, and the means are strip-bounded (`to_here`, via `toward`).
3. Iterating, the cost satisfies a fine-to-coarse recursion $f_i\le f_{i+1}\,\mathrm{drop}+\text{forcing}$ with a contracting factor, and an elementary solver (`fade_solve`) turns it into a polynomial-in-age times geometric rate.

A feature specific to this proof is the treatment of the pole: the curvature precision has a harmonic zero (constants/zero modes), so the kernel is never inverted. One carries a representative *modulo null directions*, checked only after composition with the exterior derivative (the predicate $\mathrm{Sigil}$). The Lean also avoids appealing to qualitative continuum convergence, from which no rate would follow (docstring of `World.lean`).

## 4. Steps

### 4.1 Flat representations

**Definition 4.1 (flat representation).** Let $a$ be a level system and $H$ a horizon. For cutoffs $N\le n$ and stage $i$, an $(N,n,i)$-*flat representation* of width $\sigma$ and cost $p$ is a one-body kernel $K$ on the faces which is
- *folded* of radius $p$ in width $\sigma$ (weighted $\ell^1$ with exponential weight $e^{\sigma|\cdot|}$; the norm that also governs the perturbative vacuum recursion),
- *neutral* (annihilates constants in each argument),
- *covariant* (invariant under the lattice symmetries), and
- *tested* by every torus side $t$ at stage $i$: there is a two-form matrix $A$ matching $K$ with $d^{\top}Ad=$ the difference of the quadratic forms at cutoffs $n$ and $N$ on that torus.

*Lean:* [`Flat.FlatRep`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG08/Flat/World.lean#L36-L40), with [`Sigil`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG08/Packets/FlatStep.lean#L43-L44). The definition is the tool; nothing is to be proved.

**Lemma 4.2 (the finest stage).** If $0<\sigma\le\alpha_{J_N}$ there is a flat representation at stage $N$ with width $\sigma$ and cost $\mathrm{topTab}_N=|\text{Faces}|\cdot2\,\Omega_{J_N}$ ($\Omega$ = the strip constant of the price map).
*Sketch:* $K=\mathrm{saddle}(Y)-\mathrm{saddle}(Z)$, the difference of the saddle-point (Schur complement) kernels at the two cutoffs; each saddle kernel is $\Omega$-strip bounded by the sufficiency hypothesis on $H$, and `turn_class` converts a pair of strip bounds into the folded, neutral, covariant and tested properties.
*Lean:* `Flat.at_end` ([`World.lean#L42-L51`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG08/Flat/World.lean#L42-L51), private).

**Lemma 4.3 (one step to a coarser stage).** Let $K$ be flat at stage $i+1$ with width $u$ and cost $p\ge0$, and let $0<\sigma\le\mathrm{bridgeWidth}_i$ and $2\sigma\le L_i u$. Assume the Towers at the fine cutoff are the destination data (so the means are *travelling*, i.e. satisfy the strip estimate). Then the pushforward
$$K'=\mathrm{push}\big(K;\,T,U\big),\quad T,U=\text{the fine-share kernels at cutoffs } n,\,N,$$
is flat at stage $i$ with width $\sigma$ and cost $\mathrm{quote}_i(u)\cdot p$, where $\mathrm{quote}_i(u)=\mathrm{neutralCoin}(L_i,u,\mathrm{header})$ is the neutral-coin constant, which decays in $L_i$.
*Sketch:* apply `toward` (Prop. III.14.5). Neutrality and covariance are preserved by the push. The folded radius is multiplied by the neutral-coin constant because the kernel is neutral, so its mass cancels at long range (`push_null`): the condition $2\sigma\le L u$ is what lets one shrink the width and use the cancellation. ## 5. Where the difficulty lies

- **The unit is a delivery point, not a single theorem.** The endpoint is 15 lines, and its content is that a single run simultaneously satisfies the funded properties and the recipe. The difficulty is the *order of choices* (floors from the original sheet before $L$, bill after $L$, penalty $b$ fixed first). This is bookkeeping, but it is the reason the Lean has so many "price" objects. The written proof should state the order once (cf. III.2).
- **The flat rate is the real analysis.** The mechanism is classical: the difference between the quadratic forms for two cutoffs is a fixed bounded kernel at the fine stage and contracts under each block step because the kernel is neutral. What is new is that it is done *without* inverting the curvature precision (which has zero modes) and *without* Fourier-space Lipschitz estimates at the pole. A representative modulo null directions is carried and tested only after applying $d$. Everything is in the same width, with no loss growing with the number of faces.
- **Scope is narrower than the unit title suggests.** The flat rate covers the order-zero quadratic germ only, not the off-curl extension or any positive-order or source kernel (module docstring of `Mesh.lean`). The full set of kernels is the content of the `Necks/`, `Rudder/` and `Trill/` packages, which have not been examined in detail. A reader should not read "UV rate" as "rate for the whole effective action".
- **Funding is a hypothesis that is satisfied by choice, not derived.** In Prop. This is a genuine constraint relating the rate of growth of the widths to the block factors; `spacing_choice` provides it only beyond a length threshold $E$. Because $E$ is a floor on $L$, which must precede the block lengths, this is one of the places where the circular dependence in the order of constants is resolved.
- **The recipe is "formal".** The docstring of `NativeComponentRecipe.lean` says the realized marked data is not identified with the physical marked packets and that the full joint selection theorem is not proved here. What is proved is that *given* the floors and the paid bill, the three supplier statements hold on the run's levels. The identification is made elsewhere (see §7).
