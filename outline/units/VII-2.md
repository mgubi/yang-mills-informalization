**Conventions.** $G$ is a compact matrix group with simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$; $\dim\mathfrak g$ is the real dimension. $C_A$ is the adjoint Casimir of I.1, defined by $-\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{ad}_x\mathrm{ad}_y)=C_A\langle x,y\rangle$. The constants are
$$\kappa_G=\frac{11\,C_A}{24\pi^2},\qquad b_0=\frac{\kappa_G}{2}=\frac{11\,C_A}{48\pi^2},\qquad d_2=-\frac{\kappa_G}{4}=-\frac{b_0}{2},\qquad A_G=12\Big(\frac{d_2}{b_0}\Big)^2\frac{\dim\mathfrak g}{\pi^4}=\frac{3\dim\mathfrak g}{\pi^4}.$$
$\kappa_G$ is the slope of the coupling schedule $\hat\beta_k=\bar\beta+\kappa_G\log(1/a_k)$ of the renormalization group (RG) at level $k$ (II.2, III.9). In the normalization of the free label $\mathcal O$ below, $\beta=1/g^2$.

- $F$ is a final record (VIII.1) over a Wilson family $C$, an extraction order $f$ and a limit table $S$.
- $F_d$ is the space of degree-$d$ field labels; $\langle\cdot\rangle_{\xi_*}$ is the one-point function of the selected phase.
- $T=(T_{\mu\nu})$ is the Ward-normalized stress tensor of VI.1.
- $\mathsf F$ is the free Maxwell table: $\dim\mathfrak g$ independent free Maxwell fields, with propagator $\langle A A\rangle=1/(4\pi^2|x|^2)$.
- $\mathcal O\in\mathsf F_4$ is the Wick-ordered label $\tfrac14\sum_{\mu,\nu}F^a_{\mu\nu}F^a_{\mu\nu}$, and $T^{\rm free}$ is the traceless free Maxwell stress tensor.
- $G_\Theta(r)=S^{\xi_*}\big(\Theta(-\tfrac r2e_0)\,\Theta(\tfrac r2e_0)\big)$ is the time-axis two-point function of the selected phase.

## 1. What this unit proves

The unit defines the centered trace $\Theta$ and proves four results about it:
- the trace anomaly at the level of fields: at scale $r$, $\Theta$ equals $-\kappa_G\beta(r)^{-1}\mathcal O+o(\beta(r)^{-1})$, where $\beta(r)\sim\kappa_G\log(1/r)$ is the running inverse coupling;
- the resulting short-distance law $r^8\log^2(1/r)\,G_\Theta(r)\to 3\dim\mathfrak g/\pi^4$, and the corresponding law for the spectral measure;
- the uniqueness (rank one) of the conserved stress tensor;
- two exclusions of free-field descriptions.

Most of the lines prove the field-level anomaly, Proposition VII.2.4. That proof is a renormalization-group argument: a lattice Ward identity is transported through the block-spin steps, and a one-loop computation fixes the coefficient.

**Proposition VII.2.1 (the centered trace).** Let
$$\Theta:=\sum_\mu T_{\mu\mu}-\Big\langle\sum_\mu T_{\mu\mu}\Big\rangle_{\xi_*}\mathbf 1\in F_4 .$$
(a) $\langle\Theta\rangle_{\xi_*}=0$. $\Theta$ is real, invariant under every $K\in O(4)$ for the rotation action of VI.1, and invariant under the time-reflection adjoint. The same holds for the trace centered in any phase table respecting $S$.

(b) In the reconstructed Wightman theory of the selected phase, the vector $\iota(\Theta)$ (the field label of $\Theta$) is fixed by the conjugation $J$ and by the Lorentz group. Moreover $\langle\Omega,\Phi_\Theta(f)\Omega\rangle=0$ for every Schwartz $f$.

(c) $\Phi_\Theta(f)=\Phi_{\sum_\mu T_{\mu\mu}}(f)-\big\langle\sum_\mu T_{\mu\mu}\big\rangle_{\xi_*}\big(\int f\big)\,\mathrm{id}$.

(d) If $r^8\log^2(1/r)\,G_\Theta(r)\to A>0$, then $\iota(\Theta)\neq0$.

**Proposition VII.2.2 (spectral representation).** There is a unique positive measure $\mu_\Theta$ on $\mathbb R^4$ with the following properties.
- It has tempered growth and an energy budget $\mu_\Theta\{p_0\le R\}\le A R^k$.
- It is supported in the closed forward cone $\bar V_+$ and is Lorentz invariant.
- $e^{-sp_0}\mu_\Theta$ is finite for $s>0$, and $G_\Theta(s)=\int e^{-sp_0}\,d\mu_\Theta(p)$ for $s>0$.
- For every Schwartz $\varphi$, the spectral measure of the translation group in $\Phi_\Theta(\varphi)\Omega$ is $|\hat\varphi|^2\mu_\Theta$.
- For every Schwartz test $\phi$ on $(\mathbb R^4)^2$, the Wightman function $W_2(\Theta,\Theta)(\phi)$ equals $\int\widehat\phi^{\,\rm wave}\,d\mu_\Theta$.

In particular $G_\Theta$ is nonnegative and nonincreasing on $(0,\infty)$.

## 3. Strategy

*Physical picture and the approach taken.* Classically the Yang–Mills stress tensor is traceless. After renormalization, dilations are broken by the running coupling and $\Theta=\frac{\beta(g)}{2g^3}F^2$. The proof does not use a regularized operator identity. It characterizes $T$ by two properties: uniqueness (the conserved admissible tensors form a line, VII.2.6) and the Ward normalization. At each scale $r_i$ of a scaling sequence it then builds an explicit conserved tensor out of three lattice-built tensors:
- the canonical tensor $T^{(L)}$, which is traceless at tree level;
- the pure-trace tensor $T^{(\Delta)}=\delta_{\mu\nu}\mathcal O$;
- a third traceless structure $T^{(\Omega)}$.

The candidate is $X_i=w_iT^{(L)}+c_{i,0}T^{(\Omega)}+c_{i,1}T^{(\Delta)}$, where $w_i\approx\beta(r_i)$ is the inverse coupling. The canonical part $w_iT^{(L)}$ is not conserved: its divergence, read through the functional $\rho$ that measures the trace direction, tends to $-\kappa_G$. This is the running of $\beta$. Conservation therefore forces $c_{i,1}\to-\kappa_G/4=d_2$. Uniqueness and the Ward normalization identify $X_i$ with $T$ after rescaling. Taking the trace leaves $4c_{i,1}\mathcal O\to4d_2\mathcal O$, the only surviving term. Since $\beta(r)\sim\kappa_G\log(1/r)$, this gives $\Theta_r\approx-\mathcal O/\log(1/r)$, with no remaining dependence on $b_0$.

*The one-loop input.* The value $-\kappa_G$ comes from a lattice Ward identity at a single RG level, a "dart". A dart is a lattice stress tensor whose divergence equals the first variation of the action along a displacement field, through fourth order in the field. Darts at a given level are obtained from finer levels by block-spin transport. The transport preserves the Ward identity, because the first variations of the fine and coarse effective actions agree to fourth order; errors contract by $\frac12$ per step. The level-5 "payoff" of the dart is then compared with the infrared-regulated one-loop effective action,
$$\tfrac12\log\det(\text{gauge Hessian}+\mu^2)-\log\det(\text{ghost operator}+\mu^2).$$
Its $F^2$ coefficient has the pole $-\kappa_G\log(1/\mu)$ per plane, $12$ ordered planes in total. A mean-value argument in $\log\mu$ turns this pole into the limit $-12\kappa_G$ for the payoff. This is Balaban's renormalization-group framework (III) together with a background-field one-loop computation; what is specific here is that the anomaly coefficient is extracted from the Ward identity carried along the RG flow.

*From fields to the law, and the exclusions.* By free-field convergence (VII.1), the two-point function of $w_ir_i^4U_i^{-1}\Theta$ converges to $(4d_2)^2\cdot3\dim\mathfrak g/(\pi^4|x|^8)$, and $\log(1/r_i)/w_i\to1/\kappa_G$. Rotation and translation invariance move the points to the time axis. Monotonicity of $G_\Theta$ in the separation removes the lattice rounding of points. Finally, every null sequence has a good subsequence, which upgrades the subsequential limit to $r\downarrow0$. A Tauberian argument transfers the law to the spectral measure. The exclusions use only the law and $\mu_\Theta$:
- every Wick profile expands in integer powers and logarithms, which are incompatible with $r^{-8}\log^{-2}(1/r)$;
- a relatively local Fock realization forces each $n$-photon component of $\Theta$ to be dilation-polynomial of total degree at most $3$; this bounds $\mu_\Theta\{p_0\le R\}$ by $R^7$, against $R^8/\log^2R$.

### 4.5 The short-distance law

**Lemma VII.2.p (law along a Scribe sequence at fixed configurations).** Let $x=(x_0,x_1)$ have distinct coordinates in every axis, with a window neighbourhood. Let $x^{(i)}\to x$ be its lattice roundings at level $i$. Then
$$r_i^8\log^2(1/r_i)\;G_\Theta\big(r_i|x_0^{(i)}-x_1^{(i)}|\big)\to\frac{A_G}{|x_0-x_1|^8}.$$
*Proof sketch.*
1. Write $U_i(a_i)=w_ir_i^4\Theta$, where $a_i=U_i^{-1}(w_ir_i^4\Theta)$.
2. The two-point function in degree 4 is quadratic, $\langle(pu)(pu)\rangle=p^2\langle uu\rangle$.
3. By $O(4)$ and translation invariance, any pair of points is moved to the time axis (`axis_shape`), so the left side equals $(\log(1/r_i)/w_i)^2\,S^{\xi_*}\big(U_ia_i(r_ix_0^{(i)})\,U_ia_i(r_ix_1^{(i)})\big)$.
4. Since $a_i\to4d_2\mathcal O$, the free-field convergence (`rainbow`) and the normalization of $\mathcal O$ give the limit $\kappa_G^{-2}(4d_2)^2\cdot3\dim\mathfrak g/(\pi^4|x|^8)=A_G/|x|^8$.

### 4.6 Exclusions

**Lemma VII.2.s (power–logarithm expansions cannot carry the law).** Suppose $f=u+O(1)$ as $s\downarrow0$, where $u$ is a finite real combination of $s^a\log^k s$ with $a\in\mathbb Z$ and $k\in\mathbb N_0$, and suppose $s^8\log^2(1/s)\,f(s)\to A$. Then $A=0$.
*Proof sketch.* The $O(1)$ part contributes $s^8\log^2(1/s)\cdot O(1)\to0$. Along $s=2^{-n}$, $s^8\log^2(1/s)\,u(s)$ is a combination of $c_t\,\rho_t^{\,n}n^{k_t+2}$ with $\rho_t=2^{-(8+a_t)}$. Every term has a factor $n^{k_t+2}$ with $k_t+2\ge2$, so no term tends to a nonzero constant. Order the terms lexicographically by $(\rho_t,k_t+2)$. If the leading term has $\rho_t<1$, all terms tend to $0$. Otherwise the leading term diverges and dominates the others, so a finite limit is impossible unless its coefficient vanishes.

## 5. Where the difficulty lies

*Hard.* Three pieces carry the mathematics, and all three are needed for the exact constant.
- The transport of the lattice Ward identity through the RG with fourth-order matching (VII.2.d–e).
- The one-loop pole with coefficient exactly $\kappa_G$ (VII.2.f, using III.7), and its conversion into a value by the mean-value argument (VII.2.g–h).
- The control of all remainders to $o(\hat\beta^{-p})$ for every $p$ (VII.2.k). The anomaly is a $1/\beta$ effect, so every error must be smaller than $1/\beta$ by a margin. The bounds on $c_i$ grow like $e^{\frac16(\log\log\hat\beta)^2}$ and are beaten by decay factors $e^{-\frac15(\log\log\hat\beta)^2}$.

The identification of the explicit conserved tensor with $T$ (VII.2.n) is short once the rank-one statement and the Ward normalization are available.

*Routine.*
- Centering and the symmetries of $\Theta$.
- The passage from subsequential limits at rounded points to the full limit (monotonicity and the subsequence principle).
- The Tauberian step.
- The Wick exclusion.

The Fock exclusion is a standard type of argument (dilation spectral decomposition and a growth count), but the step from relative locality to dilation-polynomial identities (`cauchy_point`, `binds`) is a real lemma and was not read.

*Unusual or worth checking.*
1. *Logic of dependence.* The law is derived from the gallery property, which is established while the ultraviolet run is chosen (III.2): the same Wilson family must carry the trace certificates. The exclusions are proved from the law, not independently. Their public form combines the two (`publicTrace`).
2. *The cancellation of $b_0$ is a consistency check, not a tautology.* The identities $\kappa_G=2b_0$ and $d_2=-\kappa_G/4$ are definitions. The content is:
   - $c_{i,1}\to-\kappa_G/4$, where $\kappa_G$ comes from the one-loop integral (VII.2.f);
   - $\log(1/r)/w\to1/\kappa_G$, where $\kappa_G$ is the slope of the coupling schedule.

   Only because both are $\kappa_G$ is the amplitude $A_G=(4d_2/\kappa_G)^2\cdot3\dim\mathfrak g/\pi^4=3\dim\mathfrak g/\pi^4$, independent of $C_A$. Had the one-loop slope differed from the schedule, the amplitude would differ by the square of their ratio.
3. *Geometry of the points.* The law is first obtained at the generic configuration $(0,\frac s2(1,1,1,1))$, as the lattice machinery requires distinct coordinates in each axis. It is then rotated to the time axis, which uses the full $O(4)$ invariance of $\Theta$ from VI.1.
4. *Precision.* The payoff certificate is accurate to $\hat\beta_k^{-100}$, much more than the argument needs.
5. *Scope of the exclusions.* The Wick class is exactly the public class. A generalized free field with spectral density $\frac{A_G}{1536\pi}(p^2)^2/\log^2|p|$ matches the leading behaviour in VII.2.5(b) and is not excluded. The Fock exclusion concerns only vacuum matrix elements with coherent states, that is, relative locality of $\Phi_\Theta$ with respect to free photons.

## 7. What remains unexplained
1. **Content of the darts and of the payoff.** The objects in a dart are jets of functions on link space (`Peat`, `Gal`). The payoff is assembled from `lush`, `tidal`, `crest`, `floft` and `Holt`. They were read only far enough to say that $\mathrm{midPay}_q$ is a normalized second-order color-$q$ coefficient of the trace of the displacement field, corrected by a lattice total derivative (probable). The writer should extract the exact formula, and the exact meaning of $y_{di}$ in Lemma VII.2.f.
2. **`saga`.** The identity $X_w=\mathrm{seaD}_\mu+\mu^2K_w(\mu)+o(1)$, uniform in $\mu\in[b',c]$, was read as a statement only. Its proof (folder `Lamp`) is where the Ward relation of the dart meets the one-loop determinant. The resolvent-identity reading is probable.
3. **Super-polynomial remainders.** The stage and cluster estimates behind `legacy` and `gapC` (folders `Terrace`, `Cask`, `Shepherd`, `Slate/Phantom`), and the pullback tools `from_center` and `differentiation`, were read as statements only. The exponents $\frac15$ and $\frac16$ in $e^{\pm a(\log\log\hat\beta)^2}$ come from these lemmas. Also unread: `brass`, which decomposes the level-5 readout into the payoff term and errors of size at most $e^{-(\log\log\hat\beta)^2}$ and $\hat\beta^{-30}$ (skimmed only).
4. **Third reference tensor.** $\Omega=\mathrm{st}(0,0,1)$ is traceless with $\rho(\Omega)=0$. Its explicit form in curvatures (`Margin/Mallet.lean`, `Toca`) was not identified.
5. **Fock exclusion.** The step from relative locality to vanishing Cauchy data (`local_position`, `cauchy_point`) and then to dilation-polynomial identities (`binds`) was read as statements only, as were `log_high` and `does_not_fit`.
6. **Position-space variant (VII.2.9).** The folders `Atlas`, `Pith`, `Sundial` and `Link` were read only at the level of the bundle `Result`. The frames `XScheme` and `ShellNorm`, and the relation of `stepField` to $\mathcal O$, remain to be described.
7. **Unit boundaries.** Lemmas VII.2.c and VII.2.p–r and VII.2.u are filed in VIII.1 by the declaration table, `StarPath` and `pays_sky` in VII.6, and `absoluteGeode` in III.7. The pieces of `YM/Integration` and `YM/RG08/Calendar` assigned here are the trace-test specification and RG bookkeeping records; they were not opened beyond `ManuscriptTraceTests`.
