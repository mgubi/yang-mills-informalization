1. What this unit proves

For VII.5.1–3, let \(G \subset U (q)\) be compact, \(q \geqslant 1\). Write \(2 b_0 = \kappa_G = 11 C_{\mathfrak{g}} / (24 \pi^2)\), \(a_m = (\prod_{k < m} b_k)^{- 1}\), \(s_m = \log (1 / a_m)\), with odd \(b_k \geqslant 3\). The trajectory has inverse couplings \(\beta_{N, p, m}\), bare endpoint \(\beta_{N, p} = \beta_{N, p, N}\), reference value \(\beta_{N, p, 0} = \bar{\beta} > 0\), and \(g_{N, p, m} = \beta_{N, p, m}^{- 1 / 2}\). Simple means real simple and nonabelian. The running clause says that for every \(\varepsilon > 0\), eventually in \(m\),

\(\displaystyle | \beta_{N, p, m} - (\bar{\beta} + 2 b_0 s_m) | \leqslant \varepsilon s_m\)

uniformly for \(N \geqslant m\) and \(p \in \mathbb{N}\).

Proposition VII.5.1. If the family satisfies the construction conditions (II.2), then for every \(\varepsilon > 0\) there is \(m_0 \geqslant 1\) such that

\(\displaystyle | \beta_{N, p, m} / s_m - 2 b_0 | < \varepsilon \qquad (m \geqslant m_0, N \geqslant m, p \in \mathbb{N}) .\)

Status: theorem; only the running clause is used, without simplicity. Lean: YM.Pert.Asymptotics.zeroUp_bound.

Corollary VII.5.2. Under the construction conditions and simplicity of \(\mathfrak{g}\), the Wilson family satisfies (Reg1): for every \(\varepsilon > 0\) there is \(N_0 \geqslant 1\) such that \(| \beta_{N, p} / s_N - b_{\ast} | < \varepsilon\) for all \(N \geqslant N_0\) and \(p \in \mathbb{N}\), where \(b_{\ast} = 2 b_0 > 0\). Status: theorem in VIII.1. Lean: YangMills.regular.

Proposition VII.5.3. Under the same hypotheses, for any lattice-level and volume sequences \((N_i), (k_i)\) with \(i \leqslant N_i\) eventually,

\(\displaystyle g_{N_i, k_i, i} \sqrt{s_i} \longrightarrow (2 b_0)^{- 1 / 2}, \qquad g_{N_i, k_i, i}^2 s_i \longrightarrow (2 b_0)^{- 1} .\)

Status: theorem; the second limit follows by squaring. Lean: YM.Pert.Asymptotics.comparison.

For VII.5.4–5, \(\mathcal{F}_d, \mathcal{F}_d^{\circ}\) are finite-dimensional complex label spaces of degree at most \(d\) in Euclidean hierarchies on \(S\) and its free Maxwell comparison table, on the same reference labels. Derivative plans have inclusions \(\iota_{d \to e}\) and derivatives \(\partial_{d, a}\). Exact compatibility means preserving inclusions, signed permutations \(\Gamma_4\), conjugation and units.

Proposition VII.5.4. Fix the free Maxwell data of VI.2, including the finite separating tests and boundary, polynomial and internal-rotation conditions. Its plan \(W\) satisfies the inclusion, derivative and orientation axioms; \(J^{\circ}\) satisfies Ward identities with translation part \(\kappa \partial^W\) and rotation part \(\kappa\) times the infinitesimal rotation action, where \(\kappa \ne 0\) and \(\partial^W_4 \ne 0\). Let \(V\) satisfy the inclusion and derivative axioms. Suppose \(J \in \mathcal{F}_4^{4 \times 4}\) is symmetric, real, \(\Gamma_4\)-covariant and satisfies \(\sum_a \partial^V_{4, a} J_{ab} = 0\) for every \(b\), and \((J, p, Y)\) satisfies the local Ward identities. If some \(c \in \mathbb{R}\) satisfies \(p_{4, a} = c \partial^V_{4, a}\) for all \(a = 1, \ldots, 4\), there exist \(D, N, M \in \mathbb{N}\), \(R_0 \geqslant 1\) such that \(c / \xi_k \to \kappa\) for every tracking scaling sequence at every level \((n, d, j, R) \geqslant (N, D, M, R_0)\) componentwise (VI.1). Also, for any hierarchy and local Ward identities, \(p \ne 0\) implies \(J \ne 0\). A scaling sequence has complex-linear isomorphisms \(U_k (d) : \mathcal{F}_d^{\circ} \to \mathcal{F}_d\), positive strictly decreasing \(r_k \to 0\) and \(\xi_k > 0\). Tracking \((V, W, J)\) at \((n, d, j, R)\) requires exact compatibility through \(\max (d, 4)\), convergence of transported correlations at \(r_k x\) for each fixed list of at most \(n\) fields of degrees at most \(d\), to the free correlations, with all Fréchet derivatives through order \(j\), uniformly on \(\{x : \|x\| \leqslant R, \|x_a - x_b \|_{\infty} \geqslant R^{- 1} (a \ne b)\}\), and \(\xi_k^{- 1} r_k^4 U_k (4)^{- 1} J_{ab} \to J^{\circ}_{ab}\).

Status: theorems. Lean: YM.Extra.WeakScaling.tracks_ratio, flux_nontrivial.

Proposition VII.5.5. Let \(V, W\) satisfy the inclusion and orientation axioms. For every \(d\) there is a complex-linear, \(\Gamma_4\)-equivariant, conjugation-preserving retraction \(P_d\) of \(\iota^W_d := \iota^W_{d \to d + 1}\). For any complex-linear isomorphisms \(A_d : \mathcal{F}_d^{\circ} \to \mathcal{F}_d\), define

\(\displaystyle X_0 = A_0, \qquad X_{d + 1} = \iota^V_d X_d P_d + A_{d + 1} (1 - \iota^W_d P_d) .\)

Fix \(D \in \mathbb{N}\). If \(A_d\) preserve symmetries and units for \(d \leqslant D\), then \(X_d\) are exactly compatible through degree \(D\). For sequences of such isomorphisms \(A_d^{(k)}\) satisfying \((A_{d + 1}^{(k)})^{- 1} \iota^V_d A_d^{(k)} v \to \iota^W_d v\) for every \(d < D, v\), one has \((A_d^{(k)})^{- 1} X_d^{(k)} \to 1\) in operator norm for every \(d \leqslant D\). These \(X_d^{(k)}\) are eventually bijective; replacing nonbijective terms by \(A_d^{(k)}\) gives isomorphisms \(\tilde{X}_d^{(k)}\) with \((A_d^{(k)})^{- 1} \tilde{X}_d^{(k)} v \to v\). If the original frames preserve symmetries and units through \(D\), the replacements are eventually exactly compatible simultaneously through \(D\). Status: theorem. Lean: retraction, adjusted_exact, perturb_limit, relative.

Proposition VII.5.6. Along any filter: (a) for an injective linear map \(f : E \to X\) between finite-dimensional real normed spaces, \(f (a_y) \to f (e)\) implies \(a_y \to e\); (b) continuous real-linear maps \(f_y, g : (\mathbb{R}^4)^n \to \mathbb{C}\) satisfy \(f_y \to g\) in operator norm if \(f_y (e_{i, a}) \to g (e_{i, a})\) at all \(4 n\) coordinate vectors. Status: theorem. Lean: norm_fit, norm_fit_deriv.

Definition VII.5.7. In gauge-invariant radial-jet polynomials, with curvature weight 2 and derivatives adding 1, for \(t \in \mathbb{R}^4\) set

\(\displaystyle \mathfrak{s}= \tfrac{1}{4} \sum_{i, j} \langle F_{ij}, F_{ij} \rangle, \quad T^{(t)}_{ab} = \delta_{ab} \left( t_0 \boldsymbol{1}+ t_1 \sum_i \langle F_{ai}, F_{ai} \rangle + 4 t_2 \mathfrak{s} \right) + (1 - \delta_{ab}) t_3 \sum_i \langle F_{ai}, F_{bi} \rangle .\)

Here \(F, \partial F\) are linear curls of degree-0 and degree-1 radial Taylor coefficients. These are polynomial identities before quotienting by null fields. Put \(T^{\text{can}} = T^{(0, 1, - 1 / 4, 1)}\), \(\mu^1_b = \sum_i \langle F_{bi}, \partial_b F_{bi} \rangle\), \(\mu^2_b = \sum_{i, j} \langle F_{ij}, \partial_b F_{ij} \rangle\), \(E_b = \sum_{a, i} \langle F_{bi}, \partial_a F_{ai} \rangle\), and, for \(w \in \mathbb{R}^3\),

\(\displaystyle \beta (w) = w_0 \mu^1 + w_1 \mu^2 + w_2 E, \quad \delta (w) = \beta (2 (w_0 - w_2), 2 w_1 + w_2 / 2, w_2) .\)

Both \(\mu^1, \mu^2\) belong to the real space \(\mathcal{R}_1\) of real, \(\Gamma_4\)-covariant vectors of degree at most 5. Every degree-0 radial jet is realised by a constant-curvature free Maxwell field with affine potential. Status: definitions and two lemmas. Lean: raw, basisV, bareVector, shell_affine.

Definition VII.5.8. Fix a family, nondecreasing \(K : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(K \geqslant 1\), degree \(d\), stages \(\ell_d \leqslant k \leqslant N\), volume \(p\), and \(u \in \mathbb{R}\). The sharp read-out bound asserts \(u \|c_k \|_1 \leqslant 4 (1 + n_k) w\) for every \(w \geqslant 0\) and every chain of insertion presentations \(A_i\) at the origin, with coefficient vectors \(c_i\), remainder costs \(q_i\) and densities \(\mathcal{D}_i\), satisfying the admissibility conditions of III.12 (regular and forced remainders, with square-root forced allowance), \(c_{\ell_d} = 0\), and, for every \(\ell_d \leqslant i < k\),

\(\displaystyle \begin{aligned} \mathcal{D}_i & = b_i^d \mathcal{T}^{(i)}_{\beta_{N, p, i + 1}} \mathcal{D}_{i + 1},\\ \|c_i - B_i S_i c_{i + 1} \|_1 & \leqslant b_i^{- 3 / 8} (\|Pc_{i + 1} \|_1 + q_{i + 1}),\\ q_i & \leqslant K_i \|PS_i c_{i + 1} \|_1 + b_i^{- 3 / 8} q_{i + 1} . \end{aligned}\)

For each \(\ell_d \leqslant i < k\), costs \(q_i, q_{i + 1}\) are nonnegative and \(B_i\) is a complex-linear automorphism commuting with \(P\), with \(\|B_i^{\pm 1} c\|_1 \leqslant K_i \|c\|_1\) for every \(c\). For \(\ell_d \leqslant i \leqslant k\), the admissible remainder cost is at most \(q_i\), and \(q_k \leqslant w + n_k (w +\|c_k \|_1)\). Status: definition. Lean: YM.MergeProof.Accent.SharpRead.

3. Strategy

The inverse coupling follows a linear reference trajectory in logarithmic scale. Uniform one-step errors sum to \(o (s_m)\) around \(\bar{\beta} + 2 b_0 s_m\). Division by \(s_m \to \infty\) removes the reference coupling. The auxiliary arguments compare two limits proportional to the same nonzero derivative, which determines the stress normalisation. Averaged retractions split each inclusion; correcting frames recursively on the included subspace makes compatibility exact, while the relative correction tends to the identity.

4. Steps

  1. Given \(\beta_{N, p, 0} = \bar{\beta}\) and transfer errors bounded by \(\delta_{m + 1}\) with \(\delta_{m + 1} / \log b_m \leqslant J (m + 1)^{- 2} \to 0\), summation gives the uniform running clause. Split off a finite initial segment and bound the remaining error by a small multiple of \(s_m\) (III.2). trajectory.

  2. Divide by \(s_m\); specialise to \(m = N\) for (Reg1), and use continuity of inverse square root for VII.5.3; positivity uses simplicity. zeroUp_bound, comparison.

  3. A04–A05 give derivative and Ward limits; a linear functional nonzero on \(\partial^W_4\) extracts \(c / \xi_k \to \kappa\). tracks_ratio.

  4. Average retractions over \(\Gamma_4\) and conjugation; the recursive correction preserves inclusions and tends to the original frames in relative operator norm, ensuring eventual invertibility. retraction, perturb_limit.

  5. At the polynomial level, sign cancellation gives covariance of \(\mu^1, \mu^2\); constant curvature realises every degree-0 jet on a Maxwell solution. momentum1_mem, shell_affine.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The analytic work lies upstream in the one-loop estimate, coupling reset and shooting. This unit establishes the leading lattice logarithm; the main theorem retains only a positive slope through (Reg1).

6. Notation

\(C_{\mathfrak{g}}\) is the Casimir for \(\langle X, Y \rangle = - q^{- 1} \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} \text{tr} (XY)\). For an orthonormal basis \((e_i)\), \(C_{\mathfrak{g}} = - (\dim \mathfrak{g})^{- 1} \sum_i \text{tr}_{\mathfrak{g}} ((\text{ad} \hspace{0.17em} e_i)^2)\), with value 0 if \(\mathfrak{g}= 0\). In VII.5.8, \(\ell_d\) is the family’s reference stage, \(P\) removes the unit coordinate, \(\|c\|_1 = \sum_{\alpha} |c_{\alpha} |\), \((S_i c)_{\alpha} = b_i^{d - d_{\alpha}} c_{\alpha}\), \(K_i = K (J (i + 1))\); \(n_k\) is the column number. Here \(d_{\alpha}\) is label weight and \(J\) the scale-stage index. In VII.5.4, \(\xi_k > 0\) is stress normalisation.

7. What remains unexplained

A declaration establishing cutoff removal, infinite-volume limits and comparison with a coupling from continuum correlations has not been identified (tentative). Tracking-sequence existence belongs to VI.1 and VII.1; the read-out bound requires upstream one-step hypotheses.
