1. What this unit proves

For fine/coarse levels, primes denote coarse data and \(V = (\sqrt{c} \hspace{0.17em} 1, R)\), where \(c\) is the direct clip coefficient. The diagonal identities (D) and roll identities (R) are

\(\displaystyle \begin{equationsbase} d^{\ast} HdW & = Q^{\ast} d^{\ast} H' d, & QW & = 1 - \kappa^{- 1} d^{\ast} H' d,\\ T^{\ast} d & = dQ, & TD' d & = DdW ;\\ M_f^{\ast} M_f + M_n^{\ast} M_n + O^{\ast} O & = 1, & \sqrt{\kappa} M_n^{\ast} & = V' d,\\ M_f V' d & = VdW, & M_n V' d & = \kappa^{- 1 / 2} d^{\ast} H' d,\\ OV' d & = 0, & M_f^{\ast} Vd & = \sqrt{\kappa} M_n^{\ast} Q. \end{equationsbase}\)

The defect identity (Δ) is

\(\displaystyle H' {(H'}^{- 1} - T^{\ast} H^{- 1} T - \kappa^{- 1} dd^{\ast}) H' = d_2^{\ast} Pd_2 .\)

Under (D),(R), for all fine/coarse potentials \(A, B\), setting \(Z = A - WB\) gives

\(\displaystyle \langle dA, HdA \rangle + \kappa |QA - B|^2 = \langle dB, H' dB \rangle + \langle dZ, HdZ \rangle + \kappa |QZ|^2 .\)

Proposition III.3.1 (catalogue). For every kernel choice \(K\), there exist sequences \(\alpha_j, \eta_j, \gamma_j > 0\), \(\Omega_j \in \mathbb{R}\), \(\lambda_j \geqslant 0\), \(b_j \in \mathbb{N}\), \(j \in \mathbb{N}\), depending only on \(K\). For every history of index \(j \geqslant 4\) and clip coefficient \(0 < c \leqslant \gamma_j\), its explicit metric data \((D, D^{- 1}, H, H^{- 1}, R)\) lie in \(\mathcal{S} (\alpha_j, \Omega_j)\), with

\(\displaystyle H^{- 1} = D^{- 1} + \lambda_j (d_2 D^{- 1})^{\ast} (d_2 D^{- 1}) .\)

On every torus, \(DD^{- 1} = HH^{- 1} = 1\), \(c 1 + R^{\ast} R = H\), \(\hat{H} (0) = 1\), \(Hd = Dd\); \(H, D\) are symmetric with bounds \(\eta_j 1 \leqslant H, D \leqslant \Omega_j 1\). For every gauge group, \(d^{\ast} Hd\) is hypercubic invariant; empty histories give \(D = 1\).

For every flight \((j, t)\), \(j, t \geqslant 4\), \(t \in \{j - 1, j\}\), and difference word \(|u| \leqslant j - 2\),

\(\displaystyle \nabla_u W \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha_j, \Omega_j L^{- 1 - |u|}), \qquad \nabla_u T \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha_j, \Omega_j L^{- 2 - |u|}) .\)

Its rolled kernel \(\mathcal{P}_{\lambda_j, \lambda_t} \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha_j, \Omega_j)\); if \(L \geqslant b_j\), its symbol is Hermitian with \(\eta_j 1 \leqslant \hat{\mathcal{P}} \leqslant \Omega_j 1\) at every real momentum. Status: theorem. Lean: pledges.

Proposition III.3.2 (families). For every gauge group \(G\) and kernel choice \(K\), a catalogue as above exists such that every sheet \(\mathfrak{S}\) with kernel \(K\) admits a second table \((\alpha'_j, \Omega'_j, \eta_j)\). For every trajectory with that kernel and the sheet's stage profile satisfying, at every level and step respectively,

\(\displaystyle c_i \leqslant \gamma_{J (i)}, \qquad L_i \geqslant b_{J (i + 1)},\)

there are pre-repair and output families simultaneously for all \(N \geqslant 0\), \(0 \leqslant i \leqslant N\). They share the explicit history metrics with amendments \(\lambda_{J (i)}\) and flight kernels \(W, T\) of index \((J (i + 1), J (i))\) for \(i < N\); bare histories are empty and \(D^{N, N} = 1\). Before repair, III.3.1's bounds and symmetry hold, as do (D) and (Δ) on the schedule torus pairs of each step, with \(P \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha_j, \Omega_j)\) Hermitian and bounded below by \(\eta_j\) on \(\text{ran} \hspace{0.17em} \sigma_2\) at real momenta, \(j = J (i + 1)\). After repair, (D),(R) hold there; metric and difference bounds use the second table, retaining \(\eta_j\), and

\(\displaystyle M_n, O \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha'_j, \Omega'_j), \qquad M_f \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha'_j, \Omega'_j L_i^{- 2}) .\)

Status: theorem under the credit condition (C1), (C2) as hypothesis. The variant before_paid (assigned to III.4) discharges it from the III.9 sheet and the III.2 payment record. Lean: retained_support.

Proposition III.3.3 (all tori). Every family satisfying III.3.2's output conditions, for any table, satisfies (D),(R) for every \(N\), \(m < N\) and positive sides \(c, f\) with \(f = cL_m\), using the same kernels and level clips. Status: theorem. Lean: complete_roll.

Proposition III.3.4 (rolled positivity). For fixed kernel choice \(K\) and integers \(j, t \geqslant 4\), there exist \(C_0, B > 0\) depending only on these data. For every \(\lambda \geqslant C_0\), \(\mu \geqslant 0\), there exist \(\eta, \sigma, \Omega > 0\) such that every flight \((j, t)\) has \(\mathcal{P}_{\mu, \lambda} \in \mathcal{S} (\sigma, \Omega)\); if \(\mu B \leqslant L^2\), its symbol is Hermitian with \(\eta 1 \leqslant \hat{\mathcal{P}} \leqslant \Omega 1\) at all real momenta. Status: theorem. Lean: Flight.frontier.

Proposition III.3.5 (filters). Define \(\text{rail}_M (z) = M^{- 1} \sum_{s = 0}^{M - 1} e^{isz}\) for positive integers \(M\), \(\text{plank}_M (z) = M^{- 1} \sum_{|s| \leqslant (M - 1) / 2} e^{isz}\) for odd \(M\), and \(a_L (p) = \prod_a \text{plank}_L (p_a)\). For all sizes where defined and all \(z \in \mathbb{C}\), both line filters satisfy \(F_L (Mz) F_M (z) = F_{LM} (z)\) and \(|F_M (z) | \leqslant e^{M | \text{Im} \hspace{0.17em} z|}\); plank is even, \(2 \pi\)-periodic and real on \(\mathbb{R}\). Absolute \(c_{\ast}, c_1 > 0\) give \(| \text{rail}_M (z) | \geqslant c_{\ast}\) when \(M |z| \leqslant 3 \pi / 2\), and \(| \text{plank}_M (z) | \geqslant e^{- c_1 M / L}\) for real \(z\), \(M \leqslant L\), \(L |z| \leqslant 3 \pi / 2\). Absolute \(B\) bounds both filters at \((q_{\ell})_a\), \(q_{\ell} = (p + 2 \pi \ell) / L\), by \(B / (1 + | \ell_a |)\) for odd \(L\) and \(| \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} p_a | < 5 \pi / 4\), \(| \text{Im} \hspace{0.17em} p_a | < \pi / 4\). Status: theorem. Lean: plank_bind, principal_plank, alias_rail.

Proposition III.3.6 (curvature covariance). Fix \(\alpha, \eta > 0\), \(\Omega \in \mathbb{R}\). A real 2-form kernel \(f\) is firm if \(f \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha, \Omega)\), \(\hat{f} (0) = 1\), and \(\eta | \omega |^2 \leqslant \langle \omega, f_n \omega \rangle \leqslant \Omega | \omega |^2\) on every torus; symmetry is not assumed. On \(U = \{p \ne 0 : |p_a | < 3 \pi / 2\} \subset \mathbb{R}^4\), set

\(\displaystyle \Phi_f = \sigma_1^{\ast} \hat{f} \sigma_1 + \sigma_0 \sigma_0^{\ast}, \qquad G_f = \sigma_1 \Phi_f^{- 1} \sigma_1^{\ast}, \qquad G_{\infty} = s |p|^{- 2} s^{\ast},\)

where \(s\) wedges with \(ip\). There exist \(b > 0\) and \(C_n \geqslant 0\) for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), depending only on \((\alpha, \Omega, \eta)\) and additionally on \(n\) for \(C_n\), such that for every firm \(f\), \(p \in U\) and complex 1-form \(x\),

\(\displaystyle \text{Re} \langle x, \Phi_f x \rangle \geqslant b |p|^2 |x|^2, \quad \|D^n G_f \| \leqslant C_n |p|^{- n}, \quad \|D^n (G_f - G_{\infty})\| \leqslant C_n |p|^{1 - n} .\)

For scalar \(f \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha, \Omega)\), \(\hat{f} \in C^{\infty}\) with \(\|D^n \hat{f} \| \leqslant A_n \Omega\) everywhere, \(A_0 = 1\), \(A_n = (n / \alpha)^n\) for \(n \geqslant 1\). Status: theorems. Lean: tank_full, gem_quote, radial_prices.

Proposition III.3.7 (nonlinear calculus). Status: theorems.

(a) For \(b, k \in \mathbb{N}\), \(k \leqslant b\), there exists \(C = C (b, k) \geqslant 1\). If \(L \geqslant 1\), \(M \geqslant 0\), and \(p : \mathbb{Z}^4 \to E\), with \(E\) a real normed space, satisfies \(\| \nabla_u p (x)\| \leqslant ML^{- 1 - |u|} (1 + L^{- 1} |x|_1)^b\) for every \(x\) and \(|u| \leqslant k\), then its degree-at-most-\(i\) Newton pieces \(P_i (x) = \sum_{| \nu | = i} \binom{x}{\nu} \nabla^{\nu} p (0)\) obey \(\|P_i (x)\| \leqslant CML^{- 1 - i} (1 + |x|_1)^b\) for \(i < k\); the remainder \(p - \sum_{i < k} P_i\) obeys the same bound with \(i = k\). Lean: capital.

(b) Let \(d \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(f : E \to F\) be \(C^d\) at \(0\) between real normed spaces, with finitely many continuous linear \(p_v : E \to E\), \(\sum_v p_v = 1\), integer weights \(Y_v \geqslant 1\). If all derivatives through \(d\) of \(f (\sum_v t^{Y_v} p_v z)\) vanish at \(0\) for every \(z\), then \(D^m f (0) (x_1, \ldots, x_m) = 0\) for all \(m \in \mathbb{N}\) whenever integers \(k_i \geqslant 1\) satisfy \(\sum_i k_i \leqslant d\) and \(p_v x_i = 0\) for \(Y_v > k_i\). Lean: cancel.

(c) Let \(b, d \in \mathbb{N}\), \(b \leqslant d\), \(n \geqslant 2\), \(o \in \Lambda_n\), and \(X \subset \Lambda_n\) finite with \(10 \hspace{0.17em} \text{dist}_{\infty} (x, o) < n\) for every \(x \in X\) (torus distance). Suppose \(u : \text{Config} (G, \Lambda_n) \to \mathbb{C}\) is gauge invariant, depends only on links with both endpoints in \(X\), and \(u \circ \exp\) is \(C^b\) at \(0\). If \(u (U_{J, o} (t))\) has zero derivatives through \(b\) for every radial polynomial connection jet through weight \(d\) (degree \(r - 1\) has weight \(r \geqslant 2\)), then \(D^m (u \circ \exp) (0) (w_1, \ldots, w_m) = 0\) for all \(m \in \mathbb{N}\), integers \(p_i \geqslant 1\), \(\sum_i p_i \leqslant b\), whenever the link field \(w_i\) is polynomial of degree \(\leqslant p_i - 1\) in centred initial-site coordinates on those links; other links are unrestricted. Here \(U_{J, o} (t)\) denotes based holonomies with weight-\(r\) components multiplied by \(t^r\). Lean: wiped.

(d) For every \(j \in \mathbb{N}\), every family of homogeneous degree-\((j + 1)\) polynomials \(Q_a : \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathfrak{g}\) (\(a = 1, \ldots, 4\)) decomposes as \(Q_a = K_a + \partial_a r\), where \(r = (j + 2)^{- 1} \sum_a x_a Q_a\) has degree \(j + 2\) and \(K\) has degree \(j + 1\) with \(\sum_a x_a K_a = 0\). Lean: locale.

(e) There exists \(C (G) > 0\): if \(0 \leqslant b \leqslant 1\), \(\tau \geqslant C (G) b^2\), \(v\) is a Lie-algebra link field with \(|v_x | \leqslant b\) on links within distance \(5\) of \(Y\), and plaquettes touching \(Y\) have chord distance \(\leqslant \tau\) from \(1\) under \(\exp (v)\), those bounds persist under \(\exp (sv)\) for all \(0 \leqslant s \leqslant 1\). Lean: strings.

3. Strategy

Solve the Gaussian block-spin problem by alias sums. Cancel the singular principal-alias poles before inversion; smoothing and geometric dilation along histories give uniform strip bounds. Amend inverse metrics away from exact forms to make the defect positive, then construct the isometry blocks without changing \(W, T\) or the quadratic forms on potentials.

4. Steps

  1. History filters have uniform alias decay and a principal-alias lower bound; geometric dilation controls products of filters. Train.smooth_upper.

  2. Lagrange's identity gives the diagonal curvature metric from the auxiliary covariance; cancelling poles gives analytic invertible symbols. Train.bend_firm.

  3. Alias algebra gives (D), including zero momentum. Flight.tangent.

  4. Koszul exactness factors the defect through \(d_2\); amendments replace its 3-form factor by \(C + \lambda - \mu S^{\ast} S\), with \(S^{\ast} S = O (L^{- 2})\). Flight.rebased.

  5. Fix the catalogue, impose the credit bounds and apply F06–F09 to construct (R); periodisation limits extend identities to all tori. Bid.pinning, Echo.allAcross.

  6. Coercivity and the resolvent identity give the covariance comparison; weighted Taylor expansion and radial decomposition give the nonlinear vanishing results. gem_quote, covers.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Bounds must hold uniformly over arbitrary history lengths and block sizes. Positivity is established at real momenta before thinning the analytic strip. Harmonic forms require defect identities on all tori. Repair constants depend on direct clip coefficients; cross-kernel decay is measured on the coarse lattice.

6. Notation

\(\Lambda_n = (\mathbb{Z}/ n\mathbb{Z})^4\); \(d_r\) acts on real \(r\)-cochains, \(d = d_1\), \(\sigma_r = \hat{d}_r\), and \(\ast\) is adjoint. \(R \in \mathcal{S} (\alpha, \Omega)\) means, for every entry, \(\sum_y e^{\alpha |y|_1} |R_{ab} (y) | \leqslant \Omega\). \(\hat{R}, R_n\) denote Fourier symbols and periodisations; aliases use centred representatives. \(J (i)\) is the stage index and \(c_i\) the direct clip coefficient. \(D^n\) denotes full Fréchet derivatives with multilinear operator norm; \(\nabla_u\) denotes fine forward differences. The cochain inner product counts cells. Histories comprise odd blocks \(L \geqslant 3\), stage indices \(J \geqslant 4\) dropping by at most one; a flight \((j, t)\) adds one block to an index-\(j\) history, giving index \(t \in \{j - 1, j\}\). \(Q\) averages potentials, \(W\) minimises the quadratic action, \(T\) transports curvature, and \(M_f, M_n, O\) are the isometry blocks; \(C, S\) denote the unamended 3-form defect and transport. Towers.

7. What remains unexplained

The iterated Feynman-gauge interpretation remains probable. Gauge and harmonic zero modes, Gaussian determinants and integration-variable changes require further treatment in III.4 and III.10. F06–F07 and analytic coefficient extraction are inputs here. III.3.6 asserts no position-space continuum limit or contact-term statement.
