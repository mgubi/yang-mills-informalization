1. What this unit proves

Throughout, \(G\) satisfies I.1, \(\mathfrak{g}\) is its Lie algebra, spacetime indices run from zero to three, and \(c\) indexes an orthonormal colour basis. A Maxwell wave is a smooth \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued two-form satisfying \(df = 0\) and \(\sum_a \partial_a f_{ab} = 0\). The algebra \(\mathcal{W}\) consists of complex polynomials in homogeneous on-shell Taylor coefficients of order \(j\), weighted \(j + 2\); \(\mathcal{P}_d\) is its \(\text{Ad} (G)\)-invariant sector of weight at most \(d\). Wick correlations contract only between sites, using the curl–curl of \(G_0 (x) = (4 \pi^2 |x|^2)^{- 1}\). Set

\(\displaystyle T^{\text{free}}_{ab} = \sum_{c, i} F^c_{ai} F^c_{bi} - \tfrac{1}{4} \delta_{ab} \sum_{c, i, k} F^c_{ik} F^c_{ik} .\)

Let \(\Theta\) be its bilinear polarization, normalized by \(\Theta (f, f) = 2 T^{\text{free}} (f)\); \(j^v_a = \sum_b v_b \Theta_{ab}\) and \(\Phi_{\chi} (j) = - \sum_a \int \partial_a \chi \hspace{0.17em} j_a\).

Proposition VI.2.1 (free table). For every register \(L\) and \(d \geqslant 0\), substitution gives a complex linear isomorphism \(\iota_d : \mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_d \to \mathcal{P}_d\); a label is null exactly when its polynomial vanishes on every on-shell Taylor tower. Correlations are Wick moments. The table satisfies IV.1's Euclidean hierarchy, including point and smeared reflection positivity, and is real-analytic at distinct points. Each fixed correlation derivative is bounded by \(Cr^{- A}\) for mutual sup-distances at least \(r\), \(0 < r \leqslant 1\), with \(A, C \geqslant 0\). Taylor differentiation gives \(\partial^{\text{free}} : \mathcal{F}_d^{\text{free}} \to \mathcal{F}_{d + 1}^{\text{free}}\) with inclusion, derivative and orientation laws. The \(O (4)\) action and generators \(\rho\) satisfy VI.1's rotation laws and preserve correlations. The stress is symmetric, real, traceless, centred and conserved in degree five. Simplicity is unnecessary. Status: theorem. Lean: hierarchy, fields, realStress.

Proposition VI.2.2 (local charge). Let \(U\) be open, \(x \in U\), \(f\) a Maxwell wave on \(U\), \(v\) a smooth conformal Killing field on \(\mathbb{R}^4\), and real \(\chi \in C_c^{\infty} (U)\) equal one near \(x\). Put \(f^{p, x} (y) = \langle p (F) (x) F (y) \rangle_0\). For each component probe \(p = F^c_{il}\),

\(\displaystyle \Phi_{\chi} (j^v (f^{p, x}, f)) = - p (\mathcal{L}_v f) (x), \qquad \mathcal{L}_v f = d \iota_v f.\)

If \(\mathcal{L}_v f\) is a Maxwell wave, this holds for every probe of every order \(j \geqslant 0\). Translations, centred rotations and dilation give respectively \(- \partial_a [p (f)]\), \(- (\omega \cdot p) (f)\) and \(- (j + 2) p (f)\) at \(x\). Flux vanishes for two Maxwell waves near \(\text{supp} \hspace{0.17em} \chi\), and for two generated fields with source \(x\) when \(v\) is homogeneous about \(x\) of degree zero or one. Status: theorem. Lean: Maxwell.Free.local_force, jet_force, two_flux_zero.

Proposition VI.2.3 (polynomial Ward identities). For \(n \geqslant 0\), distinct \(x_0, \ldots, x_n\), \(p_i \in \mathcal{W}\), and real \(\chi \in C_c^{\infty}\) equal one near \(x_0\) with spectators outside its support,

\(\displaystyle - \sum_{a, b} \int \partial_a \chi (y) v_b (y) \langle T^{\text{free}}_{ab} (y) \prod_i p_i (x_i) \rangle_0 \hspace{0.17em} dy = - \langle (Dp_0) (x_0) \prod_{i > 0} p_i (x_i) \rangle_0 .\)

Here \((v, D)\) is \((e_a, \partial_a)\), \((\omega (y - x_0), \rho (\omega))\) for \(\omega \in \mathfrak{so} (4)\), or \((y - x_0, \varepsilon)\), where \(\varepsilon\) multiplies monomials by weight. Thus \(\tau T^{\text{free}}\) has translation/rotation rows \(- \tau \partial^{\text{free}}, - \tau \rho\) for every real \(\tau\); normalization to \(+ \partial^{\text{free}}\) gives \(- T^{\text{free}}\). Status: theorem. Lean: Maxwell.Free.moment_force.

Proposition VI.2.4 (free stress line). Suppose \(G\) is simple and \(W\) satisfies the free derivative and orientation laws. Its derivative maps are \(\partial^{\text{free}}\). Let \(\mathcal{Z}\) be the real space of centred symmetric real signed-covariant degree-four tensors, and \(\mathcal{V}\) the real signed-covariant degree-five vectors. Then

\(\displaystyle \text{div} : \mathcal{Z} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{V}, \quad \dim_{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{Z}= 3, \quad \dim_{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{V}= 2, \quad \ker \text{div} =\mathbb{R}T^{\text{free}} .\)

Every lighthouse with this plan and stress \(\tau T^{\text{free}}\), \(\tau \ne 0\), satisfies (FD). Status: theorem. Lean: certificate.

Proposition VI.2.5 (Maxwell data). For simple \(G\), every Wilson family \(C\) and real \(\tau \ne 0\), \((\partial^{\text{free}}, R, \tau T^{\text{free}}, - \tau)\) satisfies all lighthouse laws and extends to Maxwell data for \(C\). The derivative is nonzero in degree four. For all sufficiently large \(d\), finitely many derivatives of Ward sums at distinct collinear configurations perpendicular to the component axis detect exactly \(\mathbb{R} \partial^{\text{free}}\) among complex linear vector maps \(\mathcal{F}_d^{\text{free}} \to \mathcal{F}_{d + 1}^{\text{free}}\) that kill the unit, are real, signed-covariant and preserve every lower-degree image with degree raised by one.

The datum includes the hierarchy, VI.1's Casimir data, and compatible degreewise continuous proper orthochronous Lorentz representations, agreeing with spatial rotations, commuting with time-reflected conjugation and having boost generator \(- i \rho\). For every \(d \geqslant 4\), any alternating real-bilinear family of complex linear endomorphisms of \(\mathcal{F}_d^{\text{free}}\) \(T (u, v)\), \(u, v \in \mathfrak{so} (4)\), that is real, equivariant under orientation-preserving signed permutations, kills the unit and preserves lower-degree images, vanishes if all its Ward sums vanish for every \(u, v\), every arity \(m \geqslant 0\), every degree-\(d\) field tuple and every distinct-point configuration. Every Maxwell datum can retain its plan, rotations, hierarchy and Lorentz representation while acquiring stress \(\tau T^{\text{free}}\), full stress rotation covariance and (FD). Its Ward constant is necessarily \(- \tau\). Status: theorem. Lean: maxwell_supplies, FreeStress.fire, required.

Proposition VI.2.6 (strict positivity). For every axis \(a\), \(x_a > 0\) and nonzero \(p \in \mathcal{W}\), \(\langle (\vartheta_a p) (\theta_a x) p (x) \rangle_0 > 0\). Every finite set of distinct coordinate probes has positive-definite reflected propagator Gram matrix there. Finite families of positive-half-space words, with distinct sites within each word, satisfy reflection positivity. For every \(d \geqslant 0\), a polynomial in \(\mathcal{P}_d\) annihilated by all distinct-point spectator tests from \(\mathcal{P}_d\) is zero. Status: theorem. Lean: detects, ghosts_out.

Proposition VI.2.7 (limit stress). Assume II.2's Wilson construction conditions, ordered smeared convergence and generic point limits along \(F\) to a table \(S\) with Euclidean hierarchy \(H\), an inclusion/derivative plan \(W\) on \(S\), a free hierarchy \(H_0\), and a lighthouse with inclusion, derivative, orientation and (FD) laws. Assume VI.2.8's station property and \(\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{F}_d^S \leqslant \dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathcal{F}_d^{\text{free}}\) for all \(d \geqslant 0\). There exists an approaching stable scaling \(X = (r_k, \xi_k, U_{k, d})\), initially with \(\xi_k = 1\). For every such scaling, interacting divergence \(\mathcal{Z}^S \to \mathcal{V}^S\), defined as in VI.2.4, is onto with one-dimensional real kernel. There exist \(0 \ne J \in \mathcal{Z}^S\) with zero divergence and an approaching stable refinement \(Y\) by subsequence and change of \(\xi_k\) such that

\(\displaystyle \xi_k^{- 1} U_{k, 4}^{- 1} (r_k^4 J_{ab}) \to T_{0, ab} .\)

For every finite arity/degree/derivative bound and \(R \geqslant 1\), tails give tracks intertwining symmetries, units and inclusions through degree \(\max (d, 4)\), satisfying the stress limit above, with correlation derivatives converging uniformly on \(K_{R, n}\) for each fixed tuple; tails may depend on tuple and tolerance. Thus \(J\) satisfies VI.1's comparison law. Status: conditional theorem. Lean: Stations.equivalences, produce_current.

Proposition VI.2.8 (stations). Fix the extraction data of VI.2.7. A depth-\(D\) station consists of \(r > 0\) and complex linear lattice lifts \(A : \mathcal{F}_d^{\text{free}} \to \text{Ref}_d\), \(d \leqslant D\). Admissibility requires eventual uniform boundedness, reality, signed covariance and preservation of the degree-zero unit, and, for every tuple of arity \(n \leqslant n_{\max}\) and degrees at most \(D\), smooth complex test supported in \(K_{R, n}\), \(M > 0\) with \(\|t\|_{\infty} \leqslant M\), and \(\eta > 0\), eventual lattice/free smeared discrepancy along the ordered cutoff tails at most \(\varepsilon M \prod_i \|v_i \| + \eta\), using test \(r^{- 4 n} t (\cdot / r)\). An admissible station yields complex linear continuum maps with the same symmetries and estimate without \(\eta\):

\(\displaystyle \left| \int t (y) \left[ \langle \prod_i Uv_i (ry_i) \rangle^S - \langle \prod_i v_i (y_i) \rangle_0 \right] dy \right| \leqslant \varepsilon M \prod_i \|v_i \| .\)

The cutoff tail may depend on the tuple, test, \(M\) and \(\eta\). The station property requires these for every \(D, n_{\max} \geqslant 0\), \(R \geqslant 1\) and \(\varepsilon, \delta > 0\), with \(r \leqslant \delta\). Balanced grid, strip and finite-difference estimates of detailed Lemma VI.2.s, for all finite bounds, imply it. Status: definitions and conditional theorems. Lean: Station.Admissible, harvested, realizes_stations.

Proposition VI.2.9 (normalized limit). Let \(C\) be a Wilson family, \(F\) an extraction order, \(S\) a table with plan \(W\), \(M\) a Maxwell datum with lighthouse Ward constant \(\kappa \ne 0\), and \(I = (J_0, p^I, Y^I)\). Assume VIII.1's ultraviolet-data conditions for these objects. Assume \(W\)'s inclusion/derivative laws, \(0 \ne J_0 \in \mathcal{Z}^S\), (FD), and an approaching stable scaling with \(\xi_k^{- 1} U_{k, 4}^{- 1} (r_k^4 J_{0, ab}) \to T_{0, ab}\). Every symmetric real conserved signed-covariant \(J \in (\mathcal{F}_4^S)^{4 \times 4}\) satisfying translation Ward rows \(W\) and some rotation Ward rows obeys

\(\displaystyle U_{k, 4}^{- 1} (r_k^4 J_{ab}) \to \kappa^{- 1} T_{0, ab} .\)

For every finite free-field tuple, any mixture of transported fields and rescaled stress slots converges with every derivative uniformly on compact distinct-point sets to the free correlation with every stress slot replaced by \(\kappa^{- 1} T_0\); the interacting insertions are evaluated at \(r_k y_i\). Canonically \(\kappa = - 1\), so each stress slot tends to \(- T^{\text{free}}\). Status: conditional theorem. Lean: Pert.meter_physical, Pert.mixed_leading.

3. Strategy

Strict reflected positivity identifies quotient fields with on-shell polynomials. Local flux gives the Ward identities; finite-dimensional classification gives the conserved stress line. Stations produce comparison isomorphisms. Transporting derivatives and recentering tensors transfers divergence rank to the interacting table. Conservation determines its stress line, and translation Ward identities fix its normalization.

4. Steps

  1. Factor reflected Wick pairings through a positive-definite Gram matrix and the Fock inner product; invertibility excludes residual polynomials. core.

  2. Smooth the Green kernel, integrate by parts twice and remove the approximate identity to obtain \(-\mathcal{L}_v f\); differentiate the source for higher probes. local_force, jet_force.

  3. Split Wick contractions by the destinations of the stress legs. Homogeneity and source-free conservation cancel the unmixed terms. fees.

  4. Classify three tensors and at most two vectors; two independent propagator tests give divergence rank two. rank_on.

  5. Bounded-degree spectators detect arbitrary weights; dilation and inversion eliminate derivative candidates after subtracting a scalar derivative. Rotational Ward identities and quarter turns eliminate alternating internal rows, completing Maxwell data with the Lorentz and Casimir inputs. scanner, erasing.

  6. Balanced strip-volume and grid-interpolation errors give stations. Compactness of bounded lifts and ordered smeared convergence produce continuum panels. cutoff_balance, harvested.

  7. Correct station panels to preserve inclusions; separating tests give injectivity, and the dimension bound gives isomorphisms. Stations.equivalences.

  8. Integration by parts gives \(r_k U_{k, d + 1}^{- 1} W_a U_{k, d} \to \partial_a^{\text{free}}\); gradient closure and Fourier inversion give smooth convergence. Recentring then permits rank transfer. travels_jet, differential_rank.

  9. Compactness on a normalized stress sphere selects a conserved limit; (FD) identifies it with \(T_0\) after one sign choice. Writing \(c (J - \langle J \rangle \boldsymbol{1}) = J_0\), comparing Ward rows gives \(c / \xi_k \to \kappa\) and the physical limit. produce_current, meter_physical.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Exact isomorphisms and recentering are needed for rank transfer. Bounded-degree detection and alternating Ward-row rigidity require more than positivity. Conservation fixes a line; Ward normalization fixes its scale and sign.

6. Notation

\(\theta_a, \vartheta_a\): reflection in \(y_a = 0\) and reflected conjugation. A probe of order \(j\) is a real linear functional on the homogeneous order-\(j\) on-shell Taylor space. \(\mathcal{F}_d^S\): degree-\(d\) fields. \(T_0, \kappa\): lighthouse stress and Ward constant. \(R, \rho\): free rotations and their generators. \(\text{div} J = \sum_a W_a J_{ab}\). (FD): onto divergence, one-dimensional kernel, nonzero centred conserved stress. \(K_{R, n}\): configurations of total norm at most \(R\) and mutual sup-distance at least \(R^{- 1}\). Scaling: isomorphisms \(U_{k, d}\), strictly decreasing \(r_k \to 0\), \(\xi_k > 0\); approaching means, for each fixed field tuple, the rescaled interacting/free correlation difference tends to zero uniformly against smooth complex tests supported in any fixed compact subset of \(\text{Conf}_n\) with sup norm at most one; \(\text{Conf}_n\) denotes distinct-point configurations; stable means eventual compatibility with reality, signed symmetries, units and inclusions.
