1. What this unit proves

Hypotheses throughout. Fix a Wilson family, extraction and limit table \(S^{\text{mix}}\) satisfying Final, constructed in VIII.1. This includes the Euclidean axioms; a compact metric phase space \(X\) with full-support Borel probability \(\nu\); continuous characters \(\chi_{\xi}\), indexed bijectively by \(X\), of the commutative C\(^{\ast}\) ground-space slab algebra \(\mathfrak{A}\), with \(\xi \mapsto \chi_{\xi} (M)\) continuous and \(\int \chi_{\xi} (M) d \nu = \langle \omega, M \omega \rangle\) for every \(M \in \mathfrak{A}\); spatial translations trivial on the mixed ground space; phase tables satisfying the Euclidean axioms and \(S^{\xi} (w) = \chi_{\xi} (M_w)\) for distinct-point words, where \(M_w\) is their time-translated compressed slab operator; the selected reconstruction at \(\xi_{\ast}\) and continuations of \(S^{\text{mix}}, S^{\xi_{\ast}}\); and the VI.1 field algebra, derivatives, rotations, Euclidean stress tensor and phase Ward identities.

Proposition V.3.1 (every phase). There exist OS spaces of \(S^{\xi}\) with slab operators, continuations \(\textsf{F}^{\xi}\) of \(S^{\xi}\) with boundary distributions \(W^{\xi}\), and theories \(\mathfrak{W}_{\xi} = (\mathcal{H}_{\xi}, \mathfrak{D}_{\xi}, \Omega_{\xi}, \phi_{\xi}, U_{\xi}, P_{\xi})\) for every \(\xi \in X\), agreeing with V.1–V.2 at \(\xi_{\ast}\). The common label space \(\mathbb{F}\), generated by the \(F_d\), has the selected star operation and Poincaré action; \(\mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\) is the dense invariant word domain, \(\Omega_{\xi}\) a unit vacuum, and \(U_{\xi}\) a strongly continuous unitary Poincaré representation on the Hilbert space \(\mathcal{H}_{\xi}\). Fields \(\phi_{\xi} (a, f)\) use \(a \in \mathbb{F}\), \(f \in \mathcal{S} (\mathbb{R}^4)\). For every phase:

  1. Vacuum field products equal \(W^{\xi}\); \(\phi_{\xi} (a, f)^{\ast}\) extends \(\phi_{\xi} (a^{\ast}, \bar{f})\); spacelike separated fields commute on \(\mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\); \(U_{\xi}\) implements Poincaré covariance; the translation spectrum lies in \(\bar{V}_+\). Every nonzero \(a \in \mathbb{F}\) has some \(f \in \mathcal{S} (\mathbb{R}^4)\) with \(\phi_{\xi} (a, f) \ne 0\) on \(\mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\); translation-invariant vectors are multiples of \(\Omega_{\xi}\).

  2. A unitary \(J_{\xi} : \mathcal{H}_{\xi} \to \mathcal{H}^{\text{OS}}_{\xi}\) sends \(\Omega_{\xi}\) to \(\omega_{\xi}\), intertwines translations and spectral projections, and sends word vectors to boundary values of the corresponding Euclidean vectors at imaginary times.

  3. The OS ground space is \(\mathcal{D}_{\xi} =\mathbb{C} \omega_{\xi}\).

  4. For every \(x \in \mathcal{H}_{\xi}\), \(P^0_{\xi} x = x\) iff \(x \in \mathbb{C} \Omega_{\xi}\).

  5. For every compactly supported finite sum of words \(A\), some \(M \geqslant 0\) satisfies \(\mu_A^{\xi} (\{0 < p^4 \leqslant u\}) \leqslant Mu^2\) for all \(\xi \in X\), \(0 < u \leqslant 1\).

  6. For every such \(A\), some \(M \geqslant 0\) satisfies, for all \(\xi \in X\), \(s \geqslant 1\),

    \(\displaystyle 0 \leqslant \langle A \Omega_{\xi}, (e^{- sH_{\xi}} - P^0_{\xi}) A \Omega_{\xi} \rangle \leqslant Ms^{- 2} .\)

  7. A bounded complex-linear \(B\) is scalar iff, for every \(a, f\) and \(u, v \in \mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\), \(u \in \text{dom} \hspace{0.17em} \phi_{\xi} (a, f)^{\ast}\) and \(\langle \phi_{\xi} (a, f)^{\ast} u, Bv \rangle = \langle u, B \phi_{\xi} (a, f) v \rangle\).

  8. Proposition V.3.y holds.

  9. For every \(n\), \(a \in \mathbb{F}^n\), \(g \in \mathcal{S} ((\mathbb{R}^4)^n)\), \(\xi \mapsto W_n^{\xi} (a ; g)\) is continuous and \(\int_X W_n^{\xi} (a ; g) d \nu = W_n^{\text{mix}} (a ; g)\). For every distinct-point word \(w\), \(\int_X S^{\xi} (w) d \nu = S^{\text{mix}} (w)\).

Status: theorem under the stated hypotheses. Lean: all_characters. Items 5–7 are also Propositions V.3.n (light_bound), V.3.o (inverse_square) and V.3.t (commutant).

Proposition V.3.2 (no exceptional phase). For every \(\xi \in X\) and \(x \in \mathcal{H}_{\xi}\), \(P^0_{\xi} x = \langle \Omega_{\xi}, x \rangle \Omega_{\xi}\). Status: theorem. Lean: all_plant.

Remark V.3.3 (additional consequences). Under V.3.1, for every \(\xi\) and \(x \in \mathcal{H}_{\xi}\), being fixed by \(e^{- sH_{\xi}}\) for all \(s \geqslant 0\), being translation invariant, and belonging to \(\mathbb{C} \Omega_{\xi}\) are equivalent. For every degree \(d\) and degree-\(d\) token coefficient vector \(v\), nullness for \(S^{\xi}\) is equivalent to vanishing of the field of its \(F_d\) class on every test. For every \(O \in O (4)\), \(b \in \mathbb{R}^4\), coordinate \(a\), finite family of distinct-point words \(w_i\) in \(\{x : (O^{- 1} (x - b))^a > 0\}\) and coefficients \(c_i \in \mathbb{C}\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_{i, j} \overline{c_i} c_j S^{\xi} (\theta_{O, b, a} w_i \hspace{0.17em} w_j) \geqslant 0.\)

Here \(\theta_{O, b, a}\) reverses the word, reflects its points across the hyperplane, and transports the \(a\)-axis label reflection through \(O\). Status: theorem consequences, unused by the main theorem. Lean: fixedHeat, exactly_fixed, recognizes, allPlanes.

Proposition V.3.y (stress tensor). For every \(\xi \in X\):

  • (T0) \(\textsf{F}^{\xi}\) extends holomorphically to the full forward tube (consecutive imaginary differences in the open forward cone), with continuous tested partial boundaries on the closed cone, equal to \(W^{\xi}\) at zero. Each adjacent pair has label-independent tied heights tending to zero with equal exchange germs at spacelike separation.

  • (T1)–(T4) For every proper orthochronous Poincaré transformation \(g\), Schwartz \(h\), indices \(a, b \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}\) and \(u, v, x \in \mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\), \(U_{\xi} (g) \mathfrak{D}_{\xi} \subset \mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\) and

    \(\displaystyle U_{\xi} (g) T_{ab} (h) x = \sum_{i, j} \Lambda (g)_{ia} \Lambda (g)_{jb} T_{ij} (h \circ g^{- 1}) U_{\xi} (g) x,\)
    \(\displaystyle T_{ab} = T_{ba}, \qquad \langle T_{ab} (h) u, v \rangle = \langle u, T_{ab} (\bar{h}) v \rangle, \qquad \sum_a \eta_{aa} T_{ab} (\partial_a h) = 0.\)

  • (T5) Fix \(B \in F_d\), a Killing field \(K\), compact \(\mathcal{O} \subset \mathbb{R}^4\), \(m \in C_c^{\infty} (\mathbb{R}^4)\) supported in \(\mathcal{O}\), real \(f \in C_c^{\infty} (\mathbb{R})\) with \(\int f = 1\), and complex \(c \in C_c^{\infty} (\mathbb{R}^3)\) equal to \(1\) on an open ball. Suppose every \((\vec{y}, s)\) over its complement, for \(s \in \text{supp} \hspace{0.17em} f\), is strictly spacelike to every point of \(\mathcal{O}\). On \(\mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\),

    \(\displaystyle i [Q_K (f, c), \phi_{\xi} (B, m)] = \phi_{\xi} (\Sigma_K B, m) - \sum_a \phi_{\xi} (B, \partial_a (K^a m)) .\)

  • (T6) Under these conditions, \(i [Q_K (f, c), A]\), for every finite sum of words \(A\) supported in \(\mathcal{O}\), is the sum replacing one factor at a time by (T5), preserving order, for any representatives in the \(F_d\).

Status: theorem. Lean: Stress.force.

2. What it uses

  • IV.1: Euclidean axioms (Seed).

  • V.1: character decomposition, copy bounds, OS realization and almost-everywhere vacuum uniqueness (Cluster.lifts).

  • V.2: reconstruction, polynomial one-point slab bounds and C01–C29 analytic estimates (Relativistic).

  • VI.1: field algebra and Euclidean stress Ward identities (Locals).

  • OS reconstruction; spectral theorem; Vitali–Montel, Morera and Liouville theorems; dominated convergence.

3. Strategy

Uniform slab domination transfers reconstruction to every phase. Character continuity passes through holomorphic continuation to boundary distributions. Locality supplies uniform low-energy and heat decay; full support and continuity extend vacuum-subtracted decay from almost every phase to every phase. Its large-time limit identifies the vacuum projection. Separately, Euclidean vector recovery proves surjectivity; continued Ward identities give stress charges.

4. Steps

  1. Uniform reconstruction. Write \(\Pi_{\xi} (d, w)\) and \(\Pi (d, w)\) for phase and mixed slab operators. Repeated reflection and taking roots give \(\| \Pi_{\xi} (d, w)\| \leqslant \| \Pi (d, w)\|\) for every positive thickness and distinct-point slab word. Apply V.2 phasewise. Lean: sameBound, almanac.

  2. Boundary continuity. Uniform polynomial bounds, Vitali’s theorem and a Taylor formula with integrable remainder prove continuity and barycenters of \(W^{\xi}\). Lean: word_continuity, barycenter.

  3. Surjectivity. Tests with compact frequency support and exponential damping recover positive-height Euclidean vectors in \(\text{ran} \hspace{0.17em} J_{\xi}\); totality and closedness give surjectivity. Lean: onto.

  4. Low energy. For compactly supported \(A\), locality makes the time-averaged odd vacuum commutator compactly supported in space. The nonnegative weight \((1 - \cos E) / E\) (zero at \(E = 0\)) and a momentum bump give shell mass \(O (u^2)\): spatial volume contributes \(u^3\), the weight contributes \(u\). Dyadic summation yields item 5; Laplace integration yields item 6. Lean: light_bound, inverse_square.

  5. Every vacuum. Heat forms are continuous in \(\xi\). Almost-everywhere uniqueness and full support extend vacuum-subtracted decay everywhere; strong heat limits and density give V.3.2. Lean: extends_decay, all_plant.

  6. Irreducibility. Glue bounded half-plane matrix elements by weak centrality; Morera and Liouville make them constant. Vacuum uniqueness forces the commutant to be scalar. Lean: commutant.

  7. Stress charges. Continue Euclidean contact identities through spatially integrated current jumps, then remove spectator cutoffs and take word-vector matrix elements to obtain (T5). Lean: Stress.through_jump, Stress.local_action.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Uniform analytic boundary bounds, Euclidean vector recovery and joint current limits require care. Exchange germs agree only at spacelike separation; current limits are spatially integrated. The \(u^2\) estimate does not imply a mass gap. Uniqueness of the phase itself remains undecided.

6. Notation

\(\mathcal{D}_{\xi}\) is the fixed space of the OS heat semigroup; \(\mathfrak{D}_{\xi}\) is the Wightman word domain. Time is coordinate \(4\); \(P^0_{\xi} = P_{\xi} (\{p^4 = 0\})\), \(\mu_A^{\xi} (B) = \langle A \Omega_{\xi}, P_{\xi} (B) A \Omega_{\xi} \rangle\), and \(e^{- sH_{\xi}} = J_{\xi}^{\ast} e^{- sH^{\text{OS}}_{\xi}} J_{\xi}\). A compactly supported word sum has all tests in \(C_c^{\infty}\). Write \(\eta = \text{diag} (- 1, - 1, - 1, 1)\), \(\zeta_4 = - i\), \(\zeta_j = 1\), \(T_{ab} (h) = \phi_{\xi} (- \zeta_a \zeta_b T^{\text{E}}_{ab}, h)\), and \(Q_K (f, c) = \sum_b T_{4 b} (K^b (c \otimes f))\). Here \(K (x) = k + Vx\), \(V^{\textsf{T}} \eta + \eta V = 0\), and \(\Sigma_K\) is its infinitesimal label action.

The reconstruction family is Almanac (L.chambers, L.contours, L.actors, L.J); word sums are SumOfWords; the charge is Stress.Charge.

7. What remains unexplained

The C01–C29 estimates remain analytic inputs from V.2. The current-jump argument needs explicit tube faces and spacelike neighborhoods; integrated limits do not supply pointwise stress limits.

  • K-V.3.1 Almanac (numbered result) — A bundled data structure of contours and reflection constructions indexed by characters, used to run the Wightman reconstruction before vacuum uniqueness is known.

  • K-V.3.2 pure_clock (key result) — The integrated current jump at Euclidean external arguments converges, as the time parameter tends to zero from above, to the stated task value.

  • K-V.3.3 through_jump (numbered result) — The jump of the current converges to the prescribed constant jointly as the configuration tends to p and the parameter tends to 0+.

  • K-V.3.4 verdict (key result) — Partial and joint real distribution limits of the current jump hold, identifying the limit of tested Wightman-type boundary values with the prescribed expression.

  • K-V.3.5 Schwartz_shells (numbered result) — The shell identity for stress-tensor matrix elements extends from compactly supported to arbitrary sentences by approximation.

  • K-V.3.6 local_action (key result) — The time-smeared stress charge acts by commutator on a local field with compactly supported test as the expected Lorentz/translation action when the spatial cutoff is equal to one near the support.

  • K-V.3.7 force (key result) — For the reconstructed systems in all phases, the stress operator satisfies the force relations with the energy, rotations and derivatives.

  • K-V.3.8 all_characters (main result) — There exists a family of reconstructed theories, one for each character phase, that is completed, i.e., satisfies all reconstruction requirements.

  • K-V.3.9 strict_evaluation (numbered result) — Strict-height waves evaluated at points are continuous in the character, and their integral over characters equals the base value.

  • K-V.3.10 inverse_square (key result) — After subtracting the full zero-energy projection, the vacuum-subtracted word correlations decay at an inverse-square rate uniformly over characters.

  • K-V.3.11 onto (main result) — The boundary isometry is surjective, so there are no missing directions.

  • K-V.3.12 light_bound (key result) — The spectral measure of low energies below u of word states is bounded by a constant times u squared, uniformly in the character.

  • K-V.3.13 irreducible (key result) — An operator is weakly central for the reconstructed theory exactly when it is a scalar multiple of the identity.

  • K-V.3.14 all_plant (key result) — The zero-energy spectral projection of each character theory is the projection onto the vacuum.
