1. What this unit proves

All arities include zero; statements hold for every label tuple and Schwartz test unless restricted. Tables \(S, T\) share a register; \(T\) respects \(S\) means \(N_d (S) \subseteq N_d (T)\) for every degree, where \(N_d\) consists of labels vanishing with arbitrary insertions. Write (H) for: both Euclidean hierarchies; respect; differential-plan inclusions compatible with both tables and derivatives with \(T\); a spread satisfying the slab bound, smoothness in insertion positions on every valid slab, and insertion derivative laws. The slab bound is \(\|A_d (w)\| \leqslant C^{|w|} (|w| / r)^{a |w|}\) for buffers and pairwise maximum-norm distances at least \(r \in (0, 1]\), with \(C > 0\), \(a \in \mathbb{N}\) uniform in width and length for fixed degree and representative-norm bounds.

(SC) means continuity in strict complex times and spatial positions, holomorphy in times, and agreement with \(T\) at ordered imaginary times. (PG) means \(|F_n^y (x) (v) | \leqslant Ck^{- m}\) whenever all height gaps are at least \(k \in (0, 1]\), with \(C \geqslant 0\), \(m \in \mathbb{N}\) depending on \(n, v\). Put \(\psi_v (y ; f) = \int F_n^y (x) (v) f (x) \hspace{0.17em} dx\); (I), (HC), (CR) mean integrability, continuity on \(E_n\) and \(\partial_a \psi (y ; f) = \psi (y ; - i \partial_{\underline{a}} f)\) for every real height direction \(a\) within \(E_n\). (BV) means \(\psi_v (y ; f) \to W_n (v) (f)\) as \(y \to 0\) through ordered heights, without a fixed direction.

Proposition V.2.1. A16 and C01–C29 hold simultaneously, each with its own hypotheses. Status: theorem. Lean: reconstructionArcs.

Proposition V.2.2. Under (H), linear bounded-operator insertions \(B_p\), \(p \geqslant 0\), exist, given for \(p > 0\) by the central one-point slab operator of width \(2 p\), with \(\|B_p (u)\| \leqslant Cp^{- a}\) for \(0 < p < 1\), for each \(u\), with \(C > 0\), \(a \in \mathbb{N}\). The empty product is \(\| \Omega \|^2\). Their vacuum products, separated by \(e^{- (\text{Im} \hspace{0.17em} w - 2 p) H} e^{i \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} wH} U (\vec{a})\), are independent of widths \(0 < p < 1\) with gaps \(> 4 p\), agree with \(T\) at imaginary times, and define \(F\) satisfying (SC), (PG). Status: theorem. Lean: C01, C02, C03.

Proposition V.2.3. (SC), (PG) imply (I), (HC), (CR). Separately, (I), (HC), (CR), (PG) produce a multilinear tempered boundary family \(W\) satisfying (BV), hence every ordered radial limit. Status: theorem. Lean: C04, jointly.

Proposition V.2.4. Under (SC), (PG), (CR), (HC), (BV), for ordered \(y\) and compactly supported \(f\), \(W_n (v) (\hat{f}) = \psi_v (y ; \widehat{e^{\langle \underline{y}, \cdot \rangle} f})\) (L). Under (SC), (PG), (I), (L), Fourier support lies in \(\mathcal{P}_n = \{ \sum_k p_k^0 = 0, \sum_{k \geqslant j} p_k^0 \geqslant 0 \forall j\}\). With Lorentz covariance it lies in \(\mathcal{V}_n = \bigcap_{\Lambda} \tilde{\Lambda}^{- 1} \mathcal{P}_n\); thus total momentum vanishes and suffix momenta belong to \(\bar{V}_+\). Status: theorem. Lean: C05, C06, tents.

Proposition V.2.5. Unconditionally, spatial rotations and coordinate boosts generate \(\mathcal{L}^{\uparrow}_+\); \(g \mapsto g_{\ast} f\) is Schwartz-continuous and \(t^{- 1} (\Lambda_a (t)_{\ast} f - f) \to - D_a f\), where \(D_a = \sum_k (x_k^a \partial_{x_k^0} + x_k^0 \partial_{x_k^a})\). Assume both hierarchies, inclusions compatible with \(S\), respect, Euclidean covariance, rotation laws and spin compatibility. Under (SC), (PG), (I), (CR), (HC), infinitesimal rotations give \(\sum_k \psi_{v^{(k)}} (y ; f) = \psi_v (y ; D_{a, y} f)\), for \(y \in E_n\) and each spatial axis \(a\), where \(D_{a, y} = D_a + i \sum_k y_k \partial_{x_k^a}\) and \(v^{(k)}\) replaces \(v_k\) by \(- i \rho (e_0 \wedge e_a) v_k\). Under (SC), (PG), that identity and (BV), \(W_n (K (\Lambda) v) (g_{\ast} f) = W_n (v) (f)\) for every Poincaré transformation \(g = (a, \Lambda)\). With \(S\) symmetric, inclusions compatible with \(S\), spin compatibility and induced star \(J\), this covariance makes the word Gram form invariant and word matrix elements continuous. Status: theorem. Lean: C07, C09, C28.

Proposition V.2.6. For \(p \in \mathcal{V}_n\), \(\langle p, \eta \rangle \geqslant 0\) on the closed imaginary cone, and locally \(\langle p, \eta \rangle \geqslant c |p|\) inside, for some \(c > 0\). Smooth cutoffs \(\chi_n\), equal to one on the \(1\)-neighbourhood of \(\mathcal{V}_n\), supported in its open \(3\)-neighbourhood and with polynomially bounded derivatives, exist. For \(\eta\) in the closed imaginary cone, \(e^{- \langle \eta, \cdot \rangle} \chi_n\) has temperate growth and its product with each test varies continuously in Schwartz space. The function \(\mathcal{W}_n (v) (z) = (2 \pi)^{- 4 n} (\mathcal{F}W_n (v)) (e^{i \langle \cdot, z \rangle} \chi_n)\) is holomorphic on \(\mathcal{T}_n\). For interior \(\eta\), its integrals represent \(W_n (v) [\eta] (f) = (\mathcal{F}W_n (v)) (e^{- \langle \eta, \cdot \rangle} \chi_n \check{f})\). Assuming damping, cone support, holomorphy, this integral representation, (SC), (PG), (I), (L), it equals \(F_n^y\) at \(x + i \underline{y}\) (TC). Cone support and covariance imply \(\mathcal{W}_n (K (\Lambda) v) (\Lambda z) =\mathcal{W}_n (v) (z)\). Status: theorem. Lean: C10, C15, C16.

Proposition V.2.7. Assume (SC), (TC), cone support, damping, spin compatibility, tube covariance, symmetric \(S, T\), \(T\) smooth off coincidences, respect and inclusions compatible with \(S\). There are spectator profiles approaching zero, equal at the adjacent pair and otherwise ordered, each admitting imaginary-boost paths into both tubes. Spin boosts extend entirely; the two resulting holomorphic germs agree on the zero-real-time slice and locally represent partial boundary values. Then for every adjacent swap \(\sigma\), \(W_n (v) (f) = W_n (v \circ \sigma) (f \circ \sigma)\) whenever \(\text{supp} f \subset \{x : x_{i + 1} - x_i \text{ spacelike} \}\). This joint locality implies exchange of adjacent letters in any word whose two test supports are spacelike separated. Status: theorem. Lean: actor, C27.

Proposition V.2.8. Heated vectors are the insertion products of V.2.2 acting on \(\Omega\), multiplied on the left by \(e^{- (y_1 - p) H} e^{ir_1^0 H} U (\vec{r}_1)\) at heights \(y\) and real points \(r\); the empty vector is \(\Omega\). Under (H), V.2.2 and oriented \(P\), heated OS vectors have inner product \(F^{(- x_n, \ldots, - x_1, y_1, \ldots, y_m)} (r_n, \ldots, r_1, q_1, \ldots, q_m) (Jv_n, \ldots, Jv_1, w_1, \ldots, w_m)\) for positive ordered heights \(x, y\) and widths in \((0, 1)\), with heights and gaps exceeding four times their respective widths. Under (H), slab insertions and spin compatibility, their smeared vectors \(\Phi_t (b)\), using heights \((t, 2 t, \ldots, nt)\) and width \(\min (t, 1) / 8\), have Bochner-integrable defining integrands and satisfy \(\Phi_t (a \cdot b) = U (a) \Phi_t (b)\) for every \(t > 0\), word \(b\) and translation \(a\). Under (H), (SC), (BV), these properties and the pointwise Gram identity, \(\langle \Phi_s (b), \Phi_{s'} (c) \rangle \to W (b^{\ast} c)\) independently as \(s, s' \downarrow 0\). Tensor products are dense in Schwartz space. With this density, equal null spaces, both hierarchies, inclusions compatible with both tables, (SC), (TC), vanishing of \(W (a (u, f) c)\) for all \(a, c, f\) implies \(u = 0\). Every boundary family \(W\) and antilinear \(J\) give bilinearity of \((u, f) \mapsto W (a (u, f) c)\) and continuity of \((f, g) \mapsto \langle (u, f) a, (u \prime, g) c \rangle_W\). Status: theorem. Lean: C24, C26.

Proposition V.2.9. A realization comprises spin-compatible \(K\), induced \(J\), fields bilinear in labels and tests on a dense invariant domain equal to the span of vacuum word vectors, a unit vacuum fixed by a strongly continuous unitary Poincaré representation, covariance \(U (g) \varphi (u, f) = \varphi (K (\Lambda) u, g_{\ast} f) U (g)\), the identity \(\langle \varphi (u, f) \psi, \chi \rangle = \langle \psi, \varphi (Ju, \bar{f}) \chi \rangle\) on the domain, continuous smeared-field vectors, local commutativity, and faithfulness: \(u \ne 0\) implies \(\varphi (u, f) \ne 0\) for some \(f\). It has vacuum expectations \(W\), translation spectral measure supported in \(\bar{V}_+\), and only scalar multiples of the vacuum translation invariant. Status: definition. Lean: Realizes.

Proposition V.2.10. Fix rotation and real structures, induced involutive \(J\), and spin compatibility. Assume word bilinearity, two-test continuity, word locality, label faithfulness, continuous word matrix elements and invariant Gram form. If vectors satisfy V.2.8's Gram limit, \(\Phi_s (\varnothing) \to \Omega\), and eventual translation covariance for each word and translation, and \(T\) is normalized, unital and permutation symmetric, V.2.9 holds except possibly vacuum uniqueness. If the heat-fixed space is \(\mathbb{C} \Omega\), uniqueness also holds. Status: theorem. Lean: harvest.

3. Strategy

Bounded slab insertions construct strict-time correlations. Boundary values and boosts give covariant distributions with forward spectral support. Fourier–Laplace continuation and permutation symmetry give locality. Heated vectors transfer positivity and translations to the word Hilbert space, where left multiplication defines fields.

4. Steps

  1. Heat factors telescope, removing the slab width; contractions give polynomial growth. C02.matrix_pad.

  2. Taylor's integral remainder controls boundary limits uniformly over nondecreasing heights. Waves.maintains.

  3. Continue rotation identities, then integrate boost derivatives to obtain covariance. C09.boost_invariant.

  4. The Laplace identity forces nonnegative suffix energies; Lorentz covariance upgrades this to cone support. tents.

  5. Fourier–Laplace damping extends \(F\) to the tube. Exponential decay gives complex differentiability in Schwartz space. C13.analytic_interior.

  6. Imaginary boosts construct two germs; equal Wick-slice jets identify them, yielding boundary exchange. C20.complex_germ.

  7. Gram limits give an isometric embedding into the OS space; left multiplication defines fields, and the embedding transfers positive energy. universal_embed.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Boost derivatives require finite-dimensional label levels and Schwartz estimates. Locality requires complete jets at equal pair times. Independent heating scales require unrestricted boundary limits.

6. Notation

\(\mathcal{H}, \Omega, H \geqslant 0, U\): OS space, vacuum, Hamiltonian and translations. \(\mathcal{F}= \bigcup_d F_d\): quotient labels; \(J\): reflection adjoint; \(b^{\ast}\): reversed starred word. \(E_n = \{y_1 < \cdots < y_n \}\); \(F_n^y (x) = F_n (x^0 + iy ; \vec{x})\); \(\underline{y} = ((\vec{0}, y_k))_k\). \(\mathcal{T}_n = \{z : \text{Im} (z_{k + 1} - z_k) \in V_+ \}\), with unrestricted first imaginary point. \(V_+ = \{x : | \vec{x} | < x^0 \}\). Pairings are Euclidean; \(\tilde{\Lambda} = \textsf{r} \Lambda \textsf{r}\), with spatial reversal \(\textsf{r}\). \(\mathcal{L}^{\uparrow}_+\) is the proper orthochronous Lorentz group; \(K\) is its label action. The closed imaginary cone replaces \(V_+\) by \(\bar{V}_+\). \(\mathcal{F}W (f) = W (\hat{f})\); Fourier phases are \(e^{- i \langle x, p \rangle}\), inverse normalization \((2 \pi)^{- 4 n}\). \(g_{\ast} f = f \circ g^{- 1}\) slotwise; \(\rho\) is the rotation Lie action.

7. What remains unexplained

The upstream slab laws, compact Borel functional calculus and some Fourier–Laplace differentiation estimates remain outside the detailed checks. Vacuum uniqueness applies to the selected phase; it is not supplied for the mixed table.
