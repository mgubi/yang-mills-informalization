1. What this unit proves

Fix a compact matrix gauge group \(G\) with real simple nonabelian Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}\), invariant inner product and orthonormal colour basis. Let \(V_d\) be complex \(\text{Ad} (G)\)-invariant radial-jet polynomials of engineering weight at most \(d\), \(F_d\) their free-null quotient, \(\rho\) restriction to Maxwell jets, \(\mathcal{I}_d = V_d \cap \ker \rho\), and \(\omega_d = \rho \circ \text{sec}_d\) for the VII.3 representatives.

Proposition VII.1.1 (regulator removal). For every integer \(L \geqslant 2\), \(d, r \in \mathbb{N}\), label frames, \(p \in F_d^r\) and continuous \(f\) with compact closed support in \(\text{Conf}_r\), set \(h_m = L^{- m}\), \(N_m = 4 L^{m^2 + m + 1}\). Then

\(\displaystyle \Pi_m (f ; p) \longrightarrow \int f (x) \mathcal{W}_x (\omega_d p_1, \ldots, \omega_d p_r) \hspace{0.17em} dx.\)

For any position board with smooth compactly supported profiles in \(\text{Conf}\), order-zero readings converge slotwise for every basis-label tuple to free readings. Status: theorem. Lean: Rig.zerothProfile, Position.finite_leading.

Proposition VII.1.2 (pointwise limit). For every itinerary \((N_i, h_i)\) with \(4 \mid N_i\), \(d, r \in \mathbb{N}\), \(z \in \text{Conf}_r\), sites \(o_{i, j}\) homing to \(z_j\), and \(p \in F_d^r\),

\(\displaystyle \left\langle \prod_j R_{h_i} (\text{sec}_d p_j, o_{i, j}) \right\rangle^{(0)}_{N_i} \longrightarrow \mathcal{W}_z (\omega_d p_1, \ldots, \omega_d p_r) .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: Warp.row_limit.

Proposition VII.1.3 (corrected covariance). For every such itinerary, any index set \(\iota\), injective \(z : \iota \to \mathbb{R}^4\), homing sites \(o_{i, j}\), indices \(j, l\) and keys \(a, b\),

\(\displaystyle \text{Cov}_{\mu_{N_i}} (\ell^{h_i}_{o_{i, j}, a}, \ell^{h_i}_{o_{i, l}, b}) - \delta_{jl} S_{h_i} (a, b) \longrightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} K_{ab} (z_j - z_l), & j \ne l,\\ 0, & j = l. \end{array} \right.\)

Status: theorem. Lean: Align.corrected_limit.

Proposition VII.1.4 (detection). For every \(v \in V_d \setminus \mathcal{I}_d\),

\(\displaystyle \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} \mathcal{W}_{(e_{\text{t}}, 0)} (\rho v, \rho (\sigma \bar{v})) > 0.\)

More generally, for the diagonalizing and reflected probe substitutions \(\Phi, \Psi\), every nonzero shell polynomial \(q\) satisfies \(\text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} \mathcal{W}_{(e_{\text{t}}, 0)} (\Phi q, \Psi \bar{q}) > 0\). Here \(\Phi\) is onto and \(\rho v = \Phi q\) implies \(\rho (\sigma \bar{v}) = \Psi \bar{q}\). Status: theorem. Lean: Free.two_detect, Free.positive.

Proposition VII.1.5 (Kelvin formulas). For every real symmetric \(j\)-tensor \(A\) with harmonic diagonal, and \(x \ne 0\),

\(\displaystyle \frac{A (\partial, \ldots, \partial) \Gamma (x)}{j!} = \frac{(- 2)^j}{4 \pi^2} |x|^{- 2 (j + 1)} A (x, \ldots, x) .\)

For every \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued Maxwell shell tensor \(Y \in \text{Sh}_j\), contracting the curvature kernel's first rank-\(j\) Taylor leg with \(Y\), summing colours, ordered form indices and jet indices without \(1 / 2\), gives \(\gamma_j \iota^{\ast} Y^{\natural} (x)\) for \(x \ne 0\), where \(\iota (x) = - x / |x|^2\), \(Y^{\natural} (x) = Y (x, \ldots, x)\) and \(\gamma_j = 2^j (j + 1) (j + 2) / \pi^2\). Status: theorem. Lean: Kelvin.harmonic_Green, Kelvin.response_eq.

Proposition VII.1.6 (corrected charts). Fix a realized Wilson family, construction record with limit table \(S\) and extraction order \(f\), VII.4 OPE data, \(D \in \mathbb{N}\), and a nontrivial filter \(u\) on \(\mathbb{N}^2\) finer than \(f\)'s extraction filter. Assume a weak measuring Portfolio through \(D\): observable/Haar bounds, Haar chart ratio, three price panels and a dominating label-cost constant; \(g (k) \to \infty\) with eventually \(g (k) \leqslant k\) and

\(\displaystyle \text{scale} (k) \leqslant \text{scale} (g (k)) + \log \hat{\beta}_k ;\)

finite-regulator complex linear label maps eventually satisfying Poised simultaneously through \(D\) at step \(g (k)\) (degree availability, monitor bound, tested equivariant maps and all panel estimates); and rescaled read-outs converging along \(u\) to \(\Phi_{k, d}\) for every \(d \leqslant D\) and reference label. Set \(D_{k, d} = \pi_d^S \Phi_{k, d} \lambda_d\), where \(\pi_d^S\) projects to \(S_d\) and \(\lambda_d\) is the fixed equivariant representative of the free quotient in reference label coordinates, and let \(\chi_k (d) = D_{k, d}\) when bijective, a fixed isomorphism otherwise. There exist complex linear isomorphisms \(p_k (d) : F_d \to S_d\) for all \(k, d\), eventually in \(k\), simultaneously for \(d \leqslant D\), unit-preserving, commuting with signed permutations and conjugation, and satisfying

\(\displaystyle p_k (e) \iota^F_{d, e} = \iota^S_{d, e} p_k (d) \quad (d \leqslant e \leqslant D) ;\)

and \(\chi_k (d)^{- 1} p_k (d) v \to v\) for \(d \leqslant D\), \(v \in F_d\). Status: conditional theorem. Lean: Weak.Portfolio.tune_grades.

Proposition VII.1.7 (windows and expansions).

(a) Fix III.2 RG constants, Haar bounds, a Haar chart ratio satisfying the local ratio bounds, and admissible price-panel relay with activation bounds \(u_j \leqslant L_j\), eventual activation of every degree, and all comparison/analytic rank bounds. For any wait \(U\) with \(w_j (t) = \lfloor \log (\bar{\beta} + c_G t) / \log L_j \rfloor\), \(c_G > 0\), there is a wait \(W\) with no smaller stage-length/entry bounds and the same window, and panels and dominating costs at every degree. Every realized Wilson family satisfying \(W\), prescribed observable bounds and polynomial-representative conditions then, for every \(D\), has simultaneous finite-regulator comparison data through \(D\) at all sufficiently late steps \(k\) whose scale exceeds the stage entry threshold and whose backward window has length at most \(k\) and lies in one stage; returned panels satisfy all three comparison inequalities. Lean: Dense.slim_wait_for.

(b) If \(U\) has this window and \(V\) has no smaller stage-length/entry/window bounds, each \(V\) entry at stage \(j\) exceeds or equals \(U\)'s entry at \(j + 1\), and \(\bar{\beta} + c_G t \geqslant c_G\) above that \(V\) entry, every trajectory satisfying \(V\) admits \(g (k) \to \infty\) eventually satisfying the scale inequality in VII.1.6, \(g (k) \leqslant k\), and \(g (k)\) above its \(U\) entry threshold with backward \(U\) window of length at most \(g (k)\) contained in one stage. Lean: Dense.nearby.

(c) Fix finite \(I\), \(T \subset I\), \(p \in T\), \(D, k, E, q \in \mathbb{N}\), \(R \in \mathbb{R}\), \(v > 0\), \(q \leqslant k + E\). There exist \(0 < w \leqslant v\), \(C_0 \geqslant 0\), independent of layout and bound \(B\), such that every layout satisfying the prescribed tensor symmetry, rank and analytic realization conditions (Sense), depending only on occupied markers, zero on the empty set, with weights \(a_i \in \mathbb{N}\) summing to \(k\) on \(T\) and anchored \(v\)-weighted absolute-sum coefficient bounds \(B\) through order \(E\) at every leg rank, has this expansion: re-root at \(x_p\), shift order by \(q\), zero terms \(q + n < k\); through leg rank \(k + E - q\) the coefficients are Taylor coefficients of a finite sum of curvature expressions of ranks \(\max (k - q, 0), \ldots, k + E - q\). These depend only on \(T\), retain symmetry and analytic realization, and have anchored absolute-sum bound \(BC_0\) with weight \(\exp (w \hspace{0.17em} \text{girth})\), including relative marker positions. Lean: Dart.coin_pre. Status (a–c): theorems, modulo imported station and curvature-lifting estimates whose full proofs are outside this verification (probable; see §7).

3. Strategy

At order zero the normalized expectation is Gaussian. Independent imaginary Gaussians subtract the infinite-lattice self-contractions at each insertion. Fourier analysis gives Maxwell pairings between distinct insertions and zero limits for corrected self-pairings. The common Wick deletion recursion then gives polynomial moments; compactness and dominated convergence give tested limits.

Translated inversion produces a triangular change of probe variables with diagonal positive reflected pairings. Wick matchings give strict positivity on nonzero shell polynomials. Interacting rank estimates transfer detection to raw charts; fixed equivariant retractions remove their filtration defects recursively.

4. Steps

  1. Invert the gauge-fixed form: covariance is direct torus Green function minus its reflected image. Orb.covar_link.

  2. Fourier annuli give separated limits; fixed-offset quadrature controls self-contractions. Align.chart_limit, Align.chart_return.

  3. Induct on distinguishable legs using the shared deletion recursion; compactness and dominated convergence give profiles. Warp.earth, Rig.grid_limit.

  4. Kelvin translation makes probes triangular; diagonal positive pairings give positive Wick monomial norms and finite detection. Free.pairing_var, free_injective.

  5. Rank estimates give moving-detector convergence; free detection and recursive retractions yield compatible charts. Portfolio.twoC, tune_grades.

  6. Raise entry thresholds so all active estimates fit the logarithmic window; near a stage boundary choose the preceding stage's last usable step. slim_wait_for, nearby.

  7. Re-root and reduce exponential weights, then lift and subtract successive lowest coefficients to obtain local curvature expressions with controlled bounds. coin_pre.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Oscillatory Fourier cancellation must survive differentiation. The chosen volume growth controls the amplified same-insertion errors; quarter-period centring removes reflected images. Detection needs strict positivity, including constants. No optimal volume growth rate is asserted.

6. Notation

\(\text{Conf}_r\): distinct \(r\)-tuples in \(\mathbb{R}^4\); \(\Gamma (x) = (4 \pi^2 |x|^2)^{- 1}\); \(K\): Maxwell jet kernel; \(\mathcal{W}\): Wick moments without same-insertion pairings. \(\mu_N\): Gaussian on reflection-odd link fields; \(\ell^h\): linear radial jet; \(S_h\): infinite-lattice self-covariance; \(R_h (p, o) =\mathbb{E}_{\xi} p (\ell_o^h + i \xi)\) with independent \(\xi \sim \mathcal{N} (0, S_h)\) (column); \(\langle \cdot \rangle^{(0)}\): coupling-series constant coefficient. \(\Pi_m\): centred \(h_m^{4 r}\)-weighted sum of \(f\) times the order-zero product correlation. An itinerary has even \(N_i\), \(h_i > 0\), positive integer \(N_i h_i \to \infty\), \(h_i \to 0\), \(N_i h_i^n \to \infty\) for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\); homing means \(h_i (o_i - c_{N_i}) \to z\), \(c_N = (N / 4, \ldots, N / 4)\), using centred lifts. \(\sigma\): time reflection; \(e_{\text{t}}\): time unit vector; \(\text{Sh}_j\): symmetric Taylor/antisymmetric form tensors with homogeneous fields solving \(dF = d \ast F = 0\); Taylor coefficients include \(1 / j!\). A key \(a = (j, \alpha)\) is a coordinate on \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued radial-gauge homogeneous potentials of degree \(j + 1\), with engineering weight \(j + 2\); \(K_{ab}\) uses these coordinates after inverse curl from the shell. A bar denotes complex conjugation; \(\iota^{\ast}\): 2-form pullback; \(\iota^{F, S}\): filtration inclusions.

  • K-VII.1.1 covar_link (key result) — The free orbital covariance of two link form elements is the color inner product times a difference of toroidal kernel values if the directions agree, else zero.

  • K-VII.1.2 harmonic_Green (key result) — Differentiating the 4D Green kernel j times against a symmetric harmonic tensor gives a constant times R^{-(j+1)}… times the diagonal harmonic polynomial, a Kelvin-type formula.

  • K-VII.1.3 response_eq (key result) — The colored response of a shell element equals gain times the inverted actual field, extending the scalar computation to Lie-algebra colors.

  • K-VII.1.4 pairing_var (key result) — The bilinear pairing between eigen variables and reflected variables is diagonal with squared-length entries.

  • K-VII.1.5 nearby (numbered result) — There is an unbounded predecessor-marking map g with g(k)⩽k, g(k) interior, and S(k)⩽S(g(k))+log P(k) eventually.

  • K-VII.1.6 orbit_limit (key result) — For separated endpoints, the averaged orbital pairing of dual Taylor test slots converges to the Taylor-form continuum pairing.

  • K-VII.1.7 finite_leading (key result) — For clean profiles, the finite-regulator Wilson reading at order zero converges to the continuum white-noise reading as the lattice is refined.

  • K-VII.1.8 tune_grades (key result) — There exist corrections of the measuring charts that are eventually graded-exact through level D and converge to the identity on every field.

  • K-VII.1.9 row_limit (key result) — The order-zero lattice rows of any tuple of fields converge to the Gaussian moment of their reduced sections.

  • K-VII.1.10 earth (key result) — After deleting bare-linear legs, the equation price converges to the cut value at a separated configuration.

  • K-VII.1.11 zerothProfile (main result) — For compactly supported continuous test functions away from coincident points, the order-zero coefficient of the lattice profile converges to the integral against the free discrete correlation.

  • K-VII.1.12 linear_substitution (numbered result) — The Wick moment of products of linear combinations of variables equals the sum over matchings of products of pairwise Wick tensor entries.

  • K-VII.1.13 corrected_limit (key result) — The corrected (subtracted) covariance of lattice sites at separated points converges to the indirect continuum covariance for all key labels.

  • K-VII.1.14 coin_pre (numbered result) — Finite demands on layouts admit an exact tally with constants such that suitable bounds on inputs yield an ample armor bound for a given order.

  • K-VII.1.15 two_detect (main result) — For a label outside the equation ideal, the two-point moment of the observable with its reflected conjugate has strictly positive real part.

  • K-VII.1.16 slim_wait_for (key result) — For a simple gauge group and a relay with a slim window, there is a thicker wait with the same window admitting a certified station port.
