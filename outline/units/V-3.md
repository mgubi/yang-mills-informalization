## 1. What this unit proves

Fix a Wilson family $C$, an extraction $f$ and the limit table $S$ of IV.1, and suppose the record `Final C f S` is given (§2). Its relevant content is as follows.

- The table $S$ is the barycenter of a probability measure $\mu$ on a compact metric space $X$ of "phases" (V.1).
- For each $i\in X$ there is a Euclidean table $T_i$ (written `ensemble i` in the Lean) satisfying the full Euclidean hierarchy.
- A Wightman theory $\mathcal W_{i_0}$ is given at one distinguished phase $i_0$ (V.2).

Throughout, $\mathcal H_i$ is the Hilbert space of the reconstructed theory $\mathcal W_i$. The Euclidean chamber of $T_i$ is the Hilbert space of the Osterwalder–Schrader (OS) transfer semigroup in the time direction. $e^{-sH}$ is that semigroup, and $\Omega_i$ is the vacuum of $\mathcal W_i$.

**Proposition V.3.1 (reconstruction in every phase; main result).** There is a family $(\mathcal W_i)_{i\in X}$ of Wightman theories, together with isometries $J_i:\mathcal H_i\to\mathcal H^{\rm OS}_i$ into the OS chamber of $T_i$ ("boundary isometries"), such that for every $i\in X$ (not merely $\mu$-almost every $i$):
1. $\mathcal W_i$ is a Wightman theory on the common field algebra, with the same field labels, the same rotation action and the same star operation. Its Poincaré covariance, spectral condition, locality, and unique translation-invariant vector are proved, and its Wightman functions are the boundary values of the Schwinger functions of $T_i$.
2. $J_i$ is onto.
3. The OS chamber of $T_i$ has a unique zero-energy ("heat-fixed") vector, up to scalars.
4. The zero-energy spectral projection of $\mathcal W_i$ is the rank-one projection onto $\Omega_i$.
5. *Low-energy bound.* For every finite sum of words $A$ there is $M$ such that $\nu_{i,A}\{p:\,0<p^0\le u\}\le M u^2$ for all $i\in X$ and $u\in(0,1]$. Here $\nu_{i,A}$ is the spectral measure of the vector $A\Omega_i$ in the energy-momentum variable.
6. *Decay.* For every finite sum of words $A$ there is $M$ such that for all $i$ and $s\ge1$,
 $$0\le\langle A\Omega_i,(e^{-sH_i}-P^{\rm vac}_i)A\Omega_i\rangle\le M s^{-2},$$
 where the subtracted projection is the full zero-energy projection.
7. *Irreducibility.* A bounded operator is weakly central, in the sense that it commutes weakly with every smeared field on the common domain of words, if and only if it is a scalar multiple of the identity.
8. *The stress tensor.* The continued stress tensor of $\mathcal W_i$ is a symmetric, Hermitian and conserved Lorentz tensor of operator-valued distributions. Its time-smeared charge acts as a commutator on local fields, and it is the derivative of the field, rotation and translation actions as prescribed by the data $F$. This is the structure `Stress.Force`.
9. *Integration over phases.* For every $n$, labels $v$ and joint test function $g$, the map $i\mapsto W^{(i)}_n(v;g)$ is continuous, and
 $$\int_X W^{(i)}_n(v;g)\,d\mu(i)=W^{\rm unsel}_n(v;g),$$
 where $W^{\rm unsel}$ is the Wightman function of the unselected limit. On words of pairwise distinct points, $\int_X T_i\text{-correlation}\,d\mu=S$.

The pieces are: the existence of the family (`almanac`), the properties 1–9 (`Completed`), and the theorem that they all hold together (`all_characters`).

## 3. Strategy

The Wightman theory of V.2 is constructed at a single phase $i_0$, using the unique vacuum of that phase. To obtain the reconstructed theory of the *whole* limit, one needs the same construction at all phases, and the Euclidean uniqueness of the vacuum is known a priori only at $i_0$ and for $\mu$-almost every $i$ (through a cluster argument that is available for almost every extremal state). The unit is arranged so that this is enough.

*Step 1: reconstruct without uniqueness.* For every $i$ the unit builds a Euclidean chamber and slab representation of $T_i$ (the structure `Almanac`), a contour, and a Wightman theory $\mathcal W_i$ that satisfies all requirements of `Realizes` except uniqueness of the vacuum (the structure `FieldCore`). The transfer operators of $T_i$ are controlled uniformly by those of the unselected chamber: for each word the character gives a bound by the operator norm in the unselected chamber (`sameBound`). At $i_0$ the construction coincides with that of V.2.

*Step 2: uniform spectral estimates.* The substitute for vacuum uniqueness is a low-energy bound uniform in $i$. By locality, the time-antisymmetrized commutator of $A$ with its translates vanishes at large spatial distance; averaging over a unit time interval turns this into a positive spatial Fourier transform of the spectral measures. This gives $\nu_{i,A}\{0<p^0\le u\}\le Mu^2$ (a "shell estimate"), uniformly in $i$ because the total masses $\|A\Omega_i\|^2$ are continuous in $i$ on a compact space, hence bounded. Together with the cone support of the spectral measure, this gives the inverse-square rate for $\langle A\Omega_i,(e^{-sH_i}-P_i)A\Omega_i\rangle$.

*Step 3: from almost every phase to every phase.* For $\mu$-almost every $i$ the zero-energy projection is the vacuum projection (unique heat-fixed vector in the OS chamber). For such $i$, the quantity $r_i(s)=\langle A\Omega_i,e^{-sH_i}A\Omega_i\rangle-|\langle\Omega_i,A\Omega_i\rangle|^2$ lies in $[0,Ms^{-2}]$. Since $i\mapsto r_i(s)$ is continuous on $X$, and $\mu$ has full support, the closed set $\{i: r_i(s)\in[0,Ms^{-2}]\}$ has full measure and so is all of $X$. Letting $s\to\infty$ gives $\langle A\Omega_i,P_iA\Omega_i\rangle=|\langle\Omega_i,A\Omega_i\rangle|^2$ for all $i$, hence $P_i=|\Omega_i\rangle\langle\Omega_i|$ on the word span, which is dense (`all_plant`). This is the replacement of "almost every" by "every", and the key mechanism of the unit.

*Step 4: finishing the properties.* With unique vacuum for every $i$: the heat-fixed vector in the OS chamber is unique (`every`), the Wightman theory is a Wightman theory in the full sense (`boundary_realizes`), the boundary isometry is onto (`onto`, using the explicit time-ordered vectors), the commutant is trivial (`irreducible`), and the stress tensor satisfies its force relations (`force`). Finally continuity of the Wightman functions in $i$ and the barycenter formula follow from the Taylor formula for boundary values with a common dominator (`eager`, `word_continuity`, `barycenter`).

*What differs from the usual route.* In Glimm–Jaffe the decomposition into pure phases and the reconstruction are done for a pure state, where clustering gives the unique vacuum. Here the decomposition is done on the Euclidean side (V.1) and the unit transports every statement across the decomposition uniformly in $i$, with continuity in $i$ replacing the usual measure-theoretic bookkeeping.

### 4.3 Low-energy estimate, decay and unique vacuum

**Lemma V.3.h (time-averaged commutator).** For a positive finite measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb R^4$ supported in the forward cone, set $a(p)=\hat\mu(p)-\hat\nu(-p)$, and $D(x)=\int_0^1\frac{a(x,t)-a(x,-t)}{2i}\,dt$ for $x\in\mathbb R^3$. Then $D$ is bounded by the total masses, and, if $D$ vanishes for $|x|>R$, then
$$\mu\{p:\ u/2<p^0\le u\}\le c(R,M)\,u^2,\qquad 0<u\le1,$$
with $c$ depending only on $R$, the total mass bound $M$, and a universal constant.
*Sketch.* The one-sided time average of the odd part is a positive spatial Fourier transform of $\mu$ weighted by $\int_0^1\sin(p^0t)\,dt\ge0$ (non-negative even at high energy, which is the point of the device; it avoids differentiating spectral integrals and requires no domain bounds on the generators). Test against a bump function of scale $u$ in space, supported in a ball of radius $R$ where $D$ may be non-zero. The positivity of the weight on the shell $\{u/2<p^0\le u\}$, which in the cone also confines the spatial momentum to a ball of radius $u$, gives $\mu(\text{shell})\lesssim(\text{volume of ball of radius }R)\cdot M\cdot u^2$ by the 3-dimensional scaling of the bump.

### 4.4 Surjectivity and irreducibility

**Proposition V.3.p (the boundary isometry is onto).** $J_i$ has dense range, and the range is closed (it is the image of an isometry), hence $J_i$ is surjective.
*Sketch.* It suffices to show that the vectors generated by ordered Euclidean words with strictly positive times are in the range, since they span a dense subspace of the OS Hilbert space. Such a vector is first reordered by sorting the times, then moved to the boundary by pushing the heights $g(u)=k+u\,\mathrm{rate}$, $u\downarrow0$ along a path of strictly increasing times; for $u>0$ the vector lies in the range by the "ordered covered" lemma (an analytic-continuation statement of the attachment), and the range is closed, so the limit $u\to0$ also lies in it.

**Proposition V.3.q (irreducibility).** $B$ is weakly central for $\mathcal W_i$ if and only if $B=a\cdot\mathrm{id}$.
*Sketch.* "Weakly central" means $\langle T^*u,Bv\rangle=\langle u,BTv\rangle$ for every smeared field $T$ and $u,v$ in the domain (the Lean first rewrites it in terms of the actual adjoint in `precise`). ## 5. Where the difficulty lies

*Mathematically new or delicate.*
1. The passage from "almost every phase" to "every phase" in V.3.n and V.3.o. The structure of the argument (uniform bound, continuity in $i$, closed set of full measure, full support of $\mu$) is simple, but it needs everything in V.3.d to V.3.m to hold uniformly in $i$, which is why most of the 5,000 lines are bookkeeping of constants that do not depend on $i$.
2. The low-energy bound V.3.h–V.3.k is a replacement for a mass-gap or cluster argument. It uses locality only, and the constant is uniform because it depends on the word only through the support radius $R$ and the total mass. The bound $O(u^2)$ in dimension four comes from the volume of a three-dimensional ball of radius $\sim u$ in momentum space times the unit time window; whether the exponent two is optimal is not established here, and nothing downstream appears to need it, beyond summability in $s$ of the heat form. (tentative)
3. Surjectivity of $J_i$ (V.3.p) is a statement about the analytic continuation in complex time, and uses the ordering of times and a closed range; it is the step that makes the Wightman theory and the OS Hilbert space the same.

*Routine but long.*
- Continuity in $i$ of Wightman functions (V.3.d–V.3.g), by dominated convergence with an explicit common dominator.
- The stress-tensor continuation (V.3.r–V.3.v): about 40% of the folder (the files `Pure`, `Through`, `Docks`, `Decree`, `Court`, `Charge`, `Implement`, `Tensor`, `Killing`, `Frost`, `Slots`, `Strip`, `Cross`, `Seeds`, `Shell`, `Frequency`, `Vitali`, `Polynomials`).

*What looks unusual.*
- The statement quantifies over *all* characters, but the Euclidean input (`Selection.Certificate`) already contains a unique vacuum at the distinguished point and the full Euclidean hierarchy at every point. The unit proves the vacuum uniqueness at all other points, which is what makes the decomposition meaningful as a decomposition into Wightman theories.
- `Completed` is a structure with 11 fields, bundling statements that a paper would split into three theorems (reconstruction, vacuum and decay, stress tensor).
- The docstring of `Pack.lean` says that the construction is chosen so that at the selected point it coincides with the older one, so that the new construction does not alter the earlier results. This is a bookkeeping device.
- `Compatibility.lean` says its lemmas are "restored from the accepted original proof" with bodies unchanged. This is an indication of the proof's history, not of mathematical content.
- The theory is *irreducible* (V.3.q) in each phase, but the unselected limit $S$ is a mixture over phases. Whether $X$ is a single point is not decided in this unit; the main theorem of the whole proof says (VIII.1) that the limit is a family of theories, one for each phase.
