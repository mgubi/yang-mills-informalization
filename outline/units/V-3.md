## 1. What this unit proves

**Hypotheses throughout.** Fix a Wilson family, extraction and limit table $S^{\rm mix}$ satisfying [`Final`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Spec/Closed.lean#L64-L73), constructed in VIII.1. This includes the Euclidean axioms; a compact metric phase space $X$ with full-support Borel probability $\nu$; continuous characters $\chi_\xi$, indexed bijectively by $X$, of the commutative C$^*$ ground-space slab algebra $\mathfrak A$, with $\xi\mapsto\chi_\xi(M)$ continuous and $\int\chi_\xi(M)d\nu=\langle\omega,M\omega\rangle$ for every $M\in\mathfrak A$; spatial translations trivial on the mixed ground space; phase tables satisfying the Euclidean axioms and $S^\xi(w)=\chi_\xi(M_w)$ for distinct-point words, where $M_w$ is their time-translated compressed slab operator; the selected reconstruction at $\xi_*$ and continuations of $S^{\rm mix},S^{\xi_*}$; and the VI.1 field algebra, derivatives, rotations, Euclidean stress tensor and phase Ward identities.

**Proposition V.3.1 (every phase).** There exist OS spaces of $S^\xi$ with slab operators, continuations $\mathsf F^\xi$ of $S^\xi$ with boundary distributions $W^\xi$, and theories $\mathfrak W_\xi=(\mathcal H_\xi,\mathfrak D_\xi,\Omega_\xi,\phi_\xi,U_\xi,P_\xi)$ for every $\xi\in X$, agreeing with V.1–V.2 at $\xi_*$. The common label space $\mathbb F$, generated by the $F_d$, has the selected star operation and Poincaré action; $\mathfrak D_\xi$ is the dense invariant word domain, $\Omega_\xi$ a unit vacuum, and $U_\xi$ a strongly continuous unitary Poincaré representation on the Hilbert space $\mathcal H_\xi$. Fields $\phi_\xi(a,f)$ use $a\in\mathbb F$, $f\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R^4)$. For every phase:

1. Vacuum field products equal $W^\xi$; $\phi_\xi(a,f)^*$ extends $\phi_\xi(a^*,\bar f)$; spacelike separated fields commute on $\mathfrak D_\xi$; $U_\xi$ implements Poincaré covariance; the translation spectrum lies in $\overline V_+$. Every nonzero $a\in\mathbb F$ has some $f\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R^4)$ with $\phi_\xi(a,f)\ne0$ on $\mathfrak D_\xi$; translation-invariant vectors are multiples of $\Omega_\xi$.
2. A unitary $J_\xi:\mathcal H_\xi\to\mathcal H^{\rm OS}_\xi$ sends $\Omega_\xi$ to $\omega_\xi$, intertwines translations and spectral projections, and sends word vectors to boundary values of the corresponding Euclidean vectors at imaginary times.
3. The OS ground space is $\mathcal D_\xi=\mathbb C\omega_\xi$.
4. For every $x\in\mathcal H_\xi$, $P^0_\xi x=x$ iff $x\in\mathbb C\Omega_\xi$.
5. For every compactly supported finite sum of words $A$, some $M\ge0$ satisfies $\mu_A^\xi(\{0<p^4\le u\})\le Mu^2$ for all $\xi\in X$, $0<u\le1$.
6. For every such $A$, some $M\ge0$ satisfies, for all $\xi\in X$, $s\ge1$,

   $$0\le\langle A\Omega_\xi,(e^{-sH_\xi}-P^0_\xi)A\Omega_\xi\rangle\le Ms^{-2}.$$

7. A bounded complex-linear $B$ is scalar iff, for every $a,f$ and $u,v\in\mathfrak D_\xi$, $u\in\mathrm{dom}\,\phi_\xi(a,f)^*$ and $\langle\phi_\xi(a,f)^*u,Bv\rangle=\langle u,B\phi_\xi(a,f)v\rangle$.
8. Proposition V.3.y holds.
9. For every $n$, $a\in\mathbb F^n$, $g\in\mathcal S((\mathbb R^4)^n)$, $\xi\mapsto W_n^\xi(a;g)$ is continuous and $\int_XW_n^\xi(a;g)d\nu=W_n^{\rm mix}(a;g)$. For every distinct-point word $w$, $\int_XS^\xi(w)d\nu=S^{\rm mix}(w)$.

**Proposition V.3.y (stress tensor).** For every $\xi\in X$:

- **(T0)** $\mathsf F^\xi$ extends holomorphically to the full forward tube (consecutive imaginary differences in the open forward cone), with continuous tested partial boundaries on the closed cone, equal to $W^\xi$ at zero. Each adjacent pair has label-independent tied heights tending to zero with equal exchange germs at spacelike separation.
- **(T1)–(T4)** For every proper orthochronous Poincaré transformation $g$, Schwartz $h$, indices $a,b\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ and $u,v,x\in\mathfrak D_\xi$, $U_\xi(g)\mathfrak D_\xi\subset\mathfrak D_\xi$ and

  $$U_\xi(g)T_{ab}(h)x=\sum_{i,j}\Lambda(g)_{ia}\Lambda(g)_{jb}T_{ij}(h\circ g^{-1})U_\xi(g)x,$$

  $$T_{ab}=T_{ba},\qquad \langle T_{ab}(h)u,v\rangle=\langle u,T_{ab}(\bar h)v\rangle,\qquad \sum_a\eta_{aa}T_{ab}(\partial_ah)=0.$$

- **(T5)** Fix $B\in F_d$, a Killing field $K$, compact $\mathcal O\subset\mathbb R^4$, $m\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^4)$ supported in $\mathcal O$, real $f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$ with $\int f=1$, and complex $c\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3)$ equal to $1$ on an open ball. Suppose every $(\vec y,s)$ over its complement, for $s\in\mathrm{supp}\,f$, is strictly spacelike to every point of $\mathcal O$. On $\mathfrak D_\xi$,

  $$i[Q_K(f,c),\phi_\xi(B,m)]=\phi_\xi(\Sigma_KB,m)-\sum_a\phi_\xi(B,\partial_a(K^am)).$$

- **(T6)** Under these conditions, $i[Q_K(f,c),A]$, for every finite sum of words $A$ supported in $\mathcal O$, is the sum replacing one factor at a time by (T5), preserving order, for any representatives in the $F_d$.

## 3. Strategy

Uniform slab domination transfers reconstruction to every phase. Character continuity passes through holomorphic continuation to boundary distributions. Locality supplies uniform low-energy and heat decay; full support and continuity extend vacuum-subtracted decay from almost every phase to every phase. Its large-time limit identifies the vacuum projection. Separately, Euclidean vector recovery proves surjectivity; continued Ward identities give stress charges.

<a id="unit-difficulty"></a>

## 5. Where the difficulty lies

Uniform analytic boundary bounds, Euclidean vector recovery and joint current limits require care. Exchange germs agree only at spacelike separation; current limits are spatially integrated. The $u^2$ estimate does not imply a mass gap. Uniqueness of the phase itself remains undecided.

<a id="unit-notation"></a>

## 6. Notation

$\mathcal D_\xi$ is the fixed space of the OS heat semigroup; $\mathfrak D_\xi$ is the Wightman word domain. Time is coordinate $4$; $P^0_\xi=P_\xi(\{p^4=0\})$, $\mu_A^\xi(B)=\langle A\Omega_\xi,P_\xi(B)A\Omega_\xi\rangle$, and $e^{-sH_\xi}=J_\xi^*e^{-sH^{\rm OS}_\xi}J_\xi$. A compactly supported word sum has all tests in $C_c^\infty$. Write $\eta=\mathrm{diag}(-1,-1,-1,1)$, $\zeta_4=-i$, $\zeta_j=1$, $T_{ab}(h)=\phi_\xi(-\zeta_a\zeta_bT^{\rm E}_{ab},h)$, and $Q_K(f,c)=\sum_bT_{4b}(K^b(c\otimes f))$. Here $K(x)=k+Vx$, $V^{\mathsf T}\eta+\eta V=0$, and $\Sigma_K$ is its infinitesimal label action.

The reconstruction family is [`Almanac`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/EveryPhase/Pack.lean#L117-L132) (`L.chambers`, `L.contours`, `L.actors`, `L.J`); word sums are [`SumOfWords`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/EveryPhase/Polynomials.lean#L14); the charge is [`Stress.Charge`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/EveryPhase/Charge.lean#L80-L81).
