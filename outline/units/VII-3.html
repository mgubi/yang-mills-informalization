1. What this unit proves

All points lie in \(\mathbb{R}^4\). Let \(\mathfrak{g}\) have its invariant inner product; \(\mathfrak{g}= 0\) is allowed in VII.3.1–5. Free moments are Wick-ordered Maxwell correlations with covariance built from \(\Gamma (z) = (4 \pi^2 |z|^2)^{- 1}\). Conjugation bars conjugate polynomial coefficients, and orthogonal transformations act on every tensor index. For \(x \ne y\), set \(\langle P, Q \rangle_{x, y} = \langle P (x) (\mathcal{R}_{x - y} \bar{Q}) (y) \rangle_0\).

Proposition VII.3.1 (strict positivity). For every \(x \ne y\), this pairing on \(\mathcal{W}\) is Hermitian, with real self-pairings positive exactly for \(P \ne 0\). For every finite complex-linearly independent family \((V_i)\), \((\langle V_j, V_i \rangle_{x, y})_{ij}\) is positive definite, without degree restriction. Status: theorem; only strict positivity is used downstream. Lean: reflected_positive, reflected_symm, reflected_pd.

Proposition VII.3.2 (tower Gram form). For \(n \geqslant 0\), \(z = x - y \ne 0\), \(B_z (p, q) = \langle \ell_p, \ell_q \rangle_{x, y}\) is symmetric positive definite on \(\text{Tow}_n\). A block-unitriangular automorphism \(\sigma_z\) satisfies, for all \(p, q\),

\(\displaystyle B_z (\sigma_z p, \sigma_z q) = \sum_{j < n} \frac{(j + 1) (j + 2) 2^j}{\pi^2 |z|^{2 j + 4}} \langle p_j, q_j \rangle .\)

For any \(b_1, \ldots, b_N \in \text{Tow}_n\) with \(B_z (b_i, b_k) = d_i \delta_{ik}\), \(d_i \in \mathbb{R}\), let \(\Phi_b : \mathbb{C} [Y_1, \ldots, Y_N] \to \mathcal{W}\) be the algebra homomorphism with \(\Phi_b (Y_i) = \ell_{b_i}\). For all complex polynomials \(p, q\),

\(\displaystyle \langle \Phi_b p, \Phi_b q \rangle_{x, y} = \sum_{\alpha} p_{\alpha} \overline{q_{\alpha}} \prod_i \alpha_i !d_i^{\alpha_i} .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: shiftAll_gram, towerB_pos, duplex_script.

Proposition VII.3.3 (Wick recursion). For every \(n \geqslant 1\), \(x \in \text{Conf}_n\), \(W \in \mathcal{W}^n\), insertion \(i\) and slot \(s\),

\(\displaystyle \langle \cdots (W_i X_s) (x_i) \cdots \rangle_0 = \sum_{k \ne i} \langle \cdots W_i (x_i) \cdots (\partial_s^{x_i, x_k} W_k) (x_k) \cdots \rangle_0,\)

where \(\partial_s^{y, y'}\) is the complex-linear derivation with \(\partial_s^{y, y'} X_t = C_{st} (y - y')\). For every \(n \geqslant 0\) and \(x \in \text{Conf}_n\), this recursion and \(T (1, \ldots, 1) = 1\) uniquely determine the complex-multilinear free moment \(T\). Moments are \(O (4)\)-covariant; conjugating all polynomials conjugates their moment. Status: theorem. Lean: Pair.remove, byAnnihilators.

Proposition VII.3.4 (faithfulness). For every \(d \geqslant 0\), \(P \in \text{sh}_d\) vanishes if all its correlations with arbitrary finite families \(Q_i \in \text{sh}_{d_i}\), \(d_i \geqslant 0\), at distinct points vanish. Thus null labels are exactly \(\ker \omega_d\), and \(\omega_d\) induces \(E_d \cong \text{sh}_d\), with injection \(\varpi_d : E_d \to \mathcal{W}\). For every basis \((b_i)\) of \(E_d\) and \(x \ne y\), \(v \mapsto (\langle \varpi_d v (x) \varpi_d b_i (y) \rangle_0)_i\) is injective. For any Wilson family, any table on its register, and any pair with \(x_{\mu} \ne y_{\mu}\) in every coordinate, these readings give a column system: a conjugation-fixed complex basis with unit first, from frame labels with recorded weight and length. Status: theorem. Lean: no_residual, exactField, scanner_inj, vane.

Proposition VII.3.5 (covariance). For every gauge group above, free-table correlations respect signed coordinate permutations and conjugation. For every \(d\), \(O (4)\) acts complex-linearly on \(E_d\), with \(\varpi_d (Rv) = R \varpi_d (v)\). Status: theorem. Lean: table_spin, rideO.

Proposition VII.3.6 (rigidity and position equations). A buffer \(B \in \text{Buf}_d\) is complex-linear on \(E_d\), kills \(1\), preserves every lower-degree subspace, and commutes with conjugation and signed permutations. Buffers form a finite-dimensional real space. A buffer is stationary when inserting \(B\) successively in every slot gives total free correlation zero for every field tuple and distinct-point configuration.

(a) If \(\mathfrak{g}\) is simple nonabelian, every stationary degree-four buffer vanishes. Indeed \(B = 0\) follows from \(\langle Bv \rangle_0 = 0\) and \(\langle (Bv) (e) w (0) \rangle_0 + \langle v (e) (Bw) (0) \rangle_0 = 0\) for all \(v, w \in E_4\), where \(e\) is axis \(0\)'s unit vector. Also \(\text{Buf}_d = 0\) for \(d < 4\).

(b) For any gauge group, if stationary degree-\(d\) buffers vanish, finitely many sections \((r_i, p_i, f_i)\), \(r_i \geqslant 0\), \(p_i \in E_d^{r_i}\), \(f_i \in C_c^{\infty} (\text{Conf}_{r_i} ; \mathbb{C})\), and real-linear \(g : \mathbb{R}^k \to \mathbb{R}^{\dim_{\mathbb{R}} \text{Buf}_d}\) give the position chart \(\text{chart}_Q (w, B) = g (\text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} w_i ((1 + B) p_i))_i\), where \(w_i\) are complex-multilinear forms. At the free tested data \(w_Q\) and \(B = 0\), its buffer derivative is the isomorphism

\(\displaystyle B \longmapsto g \left( \text{Re} \int f_i (x) \sum_j \langle p_{i 1} (x_1) \cdots (Bp_{ij}) (x_j) \cdots \rangle_0 \hspace{0.17em} dx \right)_i .\)

Such systems are called clean and square.

(c) Assume simple nonabelian \(\mathfrak{g}\), a final Wilson record with table \(S\), and a scaling sequence \(X = (r_k, U_{k, d}, \xi_k)\): \(r_k \downarrow 0\) strictly, \(\xi_k > 0\), and complex-linear isomorphisms \(U_{k, d} : E_d \to F_d (S)\). Assume VI.2's leading comparison and eventual frame compatibility with unit, conjugation, signed permutations and inclusions. Fix a clean square degree-four system \(Q\) and \(\varphi\) satisfying: a local real-analytic solution of \(\text{chart}_Q (w, \varphi (w)) = \text{chart}_Q (w_Q, 0)\), with \(\varphi (w_Q) = 0\) and local uniqueness; \(\pi_Q^{(0)} (L, k) \to w_Q\) for every integer \(L \geqslant 2\); for every \(L \geqslant 2\) and \(p, j \geqslant 0\), eventually all order-\(j\) coupling derivatives at zero of the position equations on the order-\(p\) lattice truncation and its image under \(\varphi\) equal those of the constant free value; and buffers \(A_k \to 0\) with invertible \(1 + A_k\), eventually solving the equations and satisfying the displayed comparison below with \(B_k\) replaced by \(A_k\), for every clean degree-four \(R\) and integer \(L \geqslant 2\).

Then \(B_k = \varphi (m_Q (k)) \to 0\); eventually \(1 + B_k\) is invertible and \(U_{k, 4} (1 + B_k)\) solves \(Q\) exactly, respects unit, conjugation, signed permutations and degree-\(\leqslant 3\) inclusions, and

\(\displaystyle m_R^{B_k} (k) - \text{chart}_R (\pi_R^{(0)} (L, k), \varphi (\pi_Q^{(0)} (L, k))) \to 0.\)

Status: (a,b) theorems; (c) conditional theorem, hypotheses discharged in VI.2 and VIII.1. Lean: Rigid.frozen_four, square_of_frozen, residency.

Proposition VII.3.7 (finite Wick profiles). A mode has mass \(\textsf{m} \geqslant 0\), integers \(j, q \geqslant 0\), continuous \(h : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(h (tp) = t^j h (p)\) for every \(t > 0\) and \(p\). Its kernel, for \(r > 0\), is

\(\displaystyle K_{\textsf{m}, q, h} (r) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{E^q h (p) e^{- rE}}{2 E} \hspace{0.17em} dp, \qquad E = \sqrt{\textsf{m}^2 + |p|^2} .\)

A Wick profile is a finite complex linear combination of finite products of these kernels, including empty products. If \(r^8 \log^2 (1 / r) G_{\Theta} (r) \to A > 0\) as \(r \to 0^+\), every profile \(W\) differs from \(G_{\Theta}\) somewhere in every \((0, \delta)\), \(\delta > 0\). In the selected reconstructed theory, every corresponding finite sum of mass-shell contractions differs from the Wightman two-point function on some Schwartz product \(\bar{f} \otimes g\) with compactly supported \(\overline{\hat{f}} \hat{g}\), using positive-sign Fourier transforms. Contractions test this product at sums of positive-energy on-shell momenta, with densities \(E^q h / (2 E)\). Status: theorem, assigned to VII.2 and VIII.1; VII.2 supplies \(A = 3 \dim \mathfrak{g}/ \pi^4\). Lean: Wick.euclidean_excludes, Wick.polynomial_excludes.

3. Strategy

Kelvin inversion and translation diagonalize the reflected jet covariance. Wick recursion extends positivity to polynomials, detecting null labels. Degree-four rigidity then supplies finite position equations. Independently, finite Wick profiles have power-log expansions incompatible with the trace's inverse-logarithmic leading term.

4. Steps

  1. Hobson's formula identifies multipole fields with Kelvin transforms. Translating at the inverted separation cancels lower Taylor coefficients and gives positive Gram pivots. reply_kelvin, shiftAll_gram.

  2. Wick recursion gives factorial monomial norms; every polynomial lies in a finite tower. Reflected self-tests then prove faithfulness and injectivity. duplex_script, no_residual.

  3. In the 22-dimensional degree-four basis, stationarity forces weighted antisymmetry; signed permutations force symmetry or zero. Finite test functionals detect all buffers, giving invertible linearization. coeff_barren, square_of_frozen.

  4. Local implicit uniqueness identifies \(B_k\) with \(A_k\) eventually, transferring exact position equations and leading comparison. residency.

  5. At finite regulator, normalized Gaussian perturbative expectations define lattice profiles; separate imaginary Gaussian averages within insertions implement Wick subtraction. Their tested Riemann sums give the lattice readings. Full.profile, Full.column.

  6. Mode expansions have integer powers and nonnegative logarithmic powers. Dominance along \(r = 2^{- n}\) excludes the trace law; spectral damping transfers tested Wightman equality to Euclidean equality. Wick.no_powerlog, Wick.polynomial_excludes.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Simultaneous diagonalization depends on Taylor normalization and ordered-index contractions. The position comparison is leading-order only. Non-freeness excludes finite profiles, leaving continuous-mass generalized free fields and infinite Wick expansions outside its scope.

6. Notation

\(\text{Conf}_n\): distinct-point configurations; \(\text{Sol}_j\): real \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued homogeneous degree-\(j\) 2-forms satisfying \(dS = \delta S = 0\); \(\text{Tow}_n = \bigoplus_{j < n} \text{Sol}_j\); \(\mathcal{W}=\mathbb{C} [X_s]\): polynomials in basis coordinates \(s = (j, a)\) of on-shell Taylor coefficients. \(\ell_p (A) = \sum_j \langle p_j, A_j \rangle\), using Taylor coefficients \((j!)^{- 1} D^j\) and summing over all ordered form and derivative indices, without a factor \(1 / 2\); \(\mathcal{R}_z\): reflection in \(z^{\perp}\); \(C_{st}\): jet covariance. \(\omega_d\): on-shell evaluation of adjoint-invariant radial-potential labels of weight \(\leqslant d\), with order-\(j\) slots weighted \(j + 2\); \(\text{sh}_d = \text{im} \omega_d\); \(E_d\): free quotient. \(m_Q\), \(w_Q\), \(\pi_Q^{(0)}\): tested rescaled correlations in \(U_{k, 4}\), free readings, and order-zero lattice readings at spacing \(L^{- k}\), respectively; \(m_R^B\): measured readings in frames \(U_{k, 4} (1 + B)\); \(\text{chart}_Q\): tested readings after \(1 + B\). \(G_{\Theta} (r)\): centered stress-tensor trace two-point function in the selected phase at \(\mp (r / 2) e_{\text{t}}\).

7. What remains unexplained

An intrinsic characterization of \(\text{sh}_d\) needs surjectivity. Interacting rank estimates belong to III.11 and III.12; continuum Wick subtraction to VII.1. Further Maxwell-Fock embedding exclusions are outside VII.3.7.
