## 1. What this unit proves

$S$ is the limit table; words are separated unless stated otherwise. “Hierarchy” means permutation symmetry, normalization and unit labels, signed-axis symmetries and reality, translation invariance, point and smeared reflection positivity for every coordinate axis, smoothness on separated configurations, and caps on all derivatives (IV.1). A cap bounds each fixed correlation derivative by $Cr^{-A}$ for $0<r\le1$ and mutual sup-distance $\ge r$, with $C\ge0$, $A\in\mathbb N$ depending on arity, degrees, labels and derivative order. The OS inner product is $\langle b,b'\rangle=S(\Theta b\cdot b')$, and $\Omega=|\emptyset\rangle$.

**Proposition V.1.1 (chambers and ground space).** For every $S$ satisfying the hierarchy and coordinate axis $a$, its OS space carries a strongly continuous self-adjoint contraction semigroup and a commuting strongly continuous spatial unitary group, $E_t|b\rangle=|b+te_a\rangle$, $U_y|b\rangle=|b+y\rangle$ ($t\ge0$, $y\perp e_a$). These identities hold for every $a$-positive $b$. This is a chamber. The fixed space $\mathcal H_0$ of all $E_t$ contains $\Omega$, and $E_t\psi\to P_0\psi$ for every $\psi$, where $P_0$ projects onto $\mathcal H_0$.
*Status:* theorem. *Lean:* [`OS.implement`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/OS/Dynamics.lean#L63-L104), [`OS.Semigroup.emerges`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/OS/Core.lean#L38-L78).

**Proposition V.1.2 (spectral calculus).** In every chamber, writing $E_t=e^{-tH}$, there is a strongly continuous unitary group determined by $e^{isH}E_1\psi=E_1^{1-is}\psi$ through continuous functional calculus. The strongly continuous unitary representation $V(x)=e^{ix^aH}U_{x-x^ae_a}$ has a projection-valued measure $P$; for every $\psi$, $\mu_\psi(B)=\langle\psi,P(B)\psi\rangle$ is supported in $p^a\ge0$, and, for every $x$ and $\mathrm{Im}\,z\ge0$,
$$\langle\psi,V(x)e^{i(\mathrm{Re}\,z)H}E_{\mathrm{Im}\,z}\psi\rangle=\int e^{ip\cdot x}e^{ip^az}\,d\mu_\psi(p).$$
*Status:* theorem. *Lean:* [`Semigroup.unit`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Heat/Lift.lean#L50-L58), [`Spectral.Orbit.total`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/SNAG/Escape.lean#L52-L119), [`Semigroup.heat`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Heat/Lift.lean#L108-L114).

**Proposition V.1.3 (slab operators).** Under the hierarchy and bench V.1.7, every time-axis chamber has unique bounded $T_d(w)$ for $d>0$, $w$ in $(0,d)$, satisfying $T_d(w)|b\rangle=|w\cdot\tau_db\rangle$ for every positive $b$. For every $D\in\mathbb N$, $\rho>0$, some $C>0$, $\kappa\in\mathbb N$ give
$$\|T_d(w)\|\le C^{|w|}(|w|/r)^{\kappa|w|} \tag{B}$$
for all $d>0$, $0<r\le1$, and $r$-buffered $w$ with degrees $\le D$, label norms $\le\rho$; the empty-word bound is $1$. With the plan's derivative identities, for fixed $d$ and labels, $T_d(w(x))$ is norm-$C^\infty$ on the open slab configuration set, and coordinate differentiation replaces the corresponding label by $\partial_jv$. More generally this smoothness holds for any slab family satisfying (B) on a time-axis chamber of $S'$, provided $S,S'$ satisfy the hierarchy, $S'$ respects $S$, and $S'$ satisfies the derivative identities of the plan on $S$.
*Status:* theorem conditional on the bench. *Lean:* [`Work.B01.answer`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/B01.lean#L50-L71), [`Work.B02.answer`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/B02.lean#L17-L30).

**Proposition V.1.4 (compressed algebra).** Assume the hierarchy, $O(4)$-covariance, a time-axis chamber and a slab family satisfying V.1.3's insertion identity. Then spatial translations fix $\mathcal H_0$ pointwise. For every separated $w$, $M(w)=P_0T_d(w+s)|_{\mathcal H_0}$ is independent of admissible $s,d$; $M(b)\Omega=P_0|b\rangle$ for positive $b$, and $\langle\Omega,M(w)\Omega\rangle=S(w)$. For any finite collection of separated words $q_i$ and shifts $a_i(n)$ with $t(a_j(n)-a_i(n))\to+\infty$ for $i<j$, the concatenations are eventually separated and their correlations tend to $\langle\Omega,\prod_iM(q_i)\Omega\rangle$, regardless of spatial shifts. The generated norm-closed $*$-algebra $\mathcal A$ is commutative; $\Omega$ is cyclic and separating. With smooth slab laws, $M$ is permutation/translation invariant, satisfies $M(\emptyset)=1$, permits dropping unit labels together with their points, satisfies $M(w)^*=M(\Theta w)$, is multilinear through products of $F_d(S)$ and norm-smooth in positions; $\mathcal A$ is separable.
*Status:* theorem. *Lean:* [`Ground.adjunction`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Ground/Permute.lean#L149-L161), [`Work.B03.answer`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Work/B03.lean#L405-L415).

**Proposition V.1.5 (decomposition).** Under V.1.4 including smoothness, $X=\mathrm{Spec}(\mathcal A)$ is compact metrizable, with full-support Borel probability $\nu$. Its points bijectively parametrize characters $\chi_\xi$; every evaluation is continuous and $\int_X\chi_\xi(p)d\nu=\langle\Omega,p\Omega\rangle$ for all $p\in\mathcal A$. Each $S^\xi(w)=\chi_\xi(M(w))$, extended by zero off separated configurations, is a table; evaluations are continuous in $\xi$, and $S(w)=\int_XS^\xi(w)d\nu$ for every separated $w$.
*Status:* theorem. *Lean:* [`Ground.resolution`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Ground/Spectrum.lean#L126-L140), [`Resolution.barycenter`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Phase/Mixture.lean#L113-L117).

<a id="unit-strategy"></a>

## 3. Strategy

Compress bounded slab insertions to the ground space. Their commutative algebra describes infinite-time-separated observables; characters yield phase tables and the vacuum state supplies their mixing measure. Operator identities give laws in every phase; an integrated variance identity selects a unique-vacuum phase almost everywhere.

<a id="unit-difficulty"></a>

## 5. Where the difficulty lies

Phase reflection needs lattice variance positivity; null-space retention needs quantitative corrected-field estimates. Neither follows merely from the abstract character decomposition. No clustering, unique vacuum or spectral gap is assumed.

<a id="unit-notation"></a>

## 6. Notation

$t(x)$: last coordinate; $e_a$: coordinate unit vector; $v\in V_d$: degree-$d$ label. Separated means distinct points. $|w|$: word length; $w\cdot w'$: concatenation; $\tau_s$: time translation; $\Theta$: antilinear OS reflection with reversal; $\vartheta$: linear time reflection. Positive words have positive times; $r$-buffered means mutual sup-distance $\ge r$ and times in $[r,d-r]$. $N_d(S)$ comprises labels vanishing against every separated spectator family; $F_d(S)=V_d/N_d(S)$. A table $S'$ respects $S$ when $N_d(S)\subset N_d(S')$ for every $d$.

<a id="unit-limitations"></a>

## 7. What remains unexplained

Ultraviolet packing, bounded approximants and corrected-field estimates remain external inputs. With copy number and approximation index fixed, the variance argument takes cutoff limits before large translations, then sends copy number to infinity and approximation error to zero. Vacuum uniqueness for every phase belongs to V.3; selection here is noncanonical.
