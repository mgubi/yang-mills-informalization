1. What this unit proves

Setting. \(G\) is the gauge group and \(\mathfrak{g}\) its Lie algebra. In dimension four, \(f = Lc\), with odd \(L \geqslant 3\) and patch order \(J \geqslant 4\). Sums use counting measure and physical colour coordinates. A step \(S\) has kernel choices \(K\), penalty \(\kappa > 0\), tagged predictors \(Q_k\), and radius \(R_S = (J + 7) L\). “Larger” means unique selected minimizing frames and predictors, nonempty fallback axial frames, \(100 R_S \leqslant f\), and \(c > 1\). A forest \(H\) has one block-centred rooted tree per block, height at most \(4 L\); \(H^{\text{fr}}\) denotes free bonds.

Here \(x\) is a coarse \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued bond field and \(a\) a free-bond field. The axis field is \(U^H (x, a) = e^a \mathcal{R} (e^x)\) on free bonds and \(1\) on tree bonds; \(\mathcal{R}\) copies coarse links onto crossing fine bonds. With the symmetrized Wilson, row-square, cubic and quartic main term \(\mathcal{M}_p\),

\(\displaystyle \mathcal{T}^H_k (x, a) =\mathcal{M}_p (W ; U^H (x, a)) + \kappa \sum_b \text{price} (e^{x_b} Q_k (U^H (x, a))_b^{- 1}), \qquad \text{price} (U) = \|U - 1\|^2 / (2 c_G^2) .\)

A critical branch has \(\rho (0) = 0\), is analytic at \(0\), and satisfies \(D_a \mathcal{T}^H_k (x, \rho (x)) = 0\) near \(0\) for every tag, with every tested action jointly analytic there. For a local exponential-chart Haar density \(J_{\mathscr{J}}\), its thermal function is

\(\displaystyle \Theta^H_k (x) = \text{loop}_W (U^H (x, \rho (x))) + \tfrac{1}{2} \log \det \text{Hess}_a \mathcal{T}^H_k (x, \rho (x)) - \sum_{e \in H^{\text{fr}}} \log J_{\mathscr{J}} (\rho (x)_e) .\)

Proposition III.6.1 (tested second jet; F03). Let \(S\) be larger, \(H\) a forest, \(B\) a bed with floor \((a, A)\) for clip \(p\) on \(\Lambda_f\), and \(W\) have bounds \((p, \sigma, C, \Lambda)\), be attached to \(B\), with \(B\) sworn. Assume main-term analyticity along analytic families through the identity. For every tag \(k\), \(\mathcal{T}^H_k\) is analytic at \(0\), its value and gradient vanish, and for all \((x, a), (x', a')\),

\(\displaystyle D^2 \mathcal{T}^H_k (0) [(x, a), (x', a')] = \langle ds, M_B ds' \rangle + \kappa \langle x - Ps, x' - Ps' \rangle, \qquad s = \iota x + a, \quad s' = \iota x' + a' .\)

Free-bond fields are extended by zero on tree bonds. Status: theorem. Lean: Bid.F03.

Proposition III.6.2 (main-term analyticity; F02). If \(W\) has bounds \((p, \sigma, C, \Lambda)\), \(X\) is a finite-dimensional real normed space, \(x_0 \in X\), and \(A (y)\) is a configuration family with \(A (x_0) = 1\) and link matrices analytic at \(x_0\), then \(\mathcal{M}_p (W ; A (y))\) is analytic at \(x_0\). Status: theorem; supplies III.6.1's analyticity assumption. Lean: Bid.F02.

Proposition III.6.3 (vertical gap; F01). Assume shell comparison E02. For every \((G, L, J, K, a)\) with \(a > 0\) there is \(\eta > 0\) such that, for all sides \(c, f\), larger \(S\) with these parameters, forests \(H\), clips \(p\), beds \(B\) with floor \((a, A)\) for clip \(p\) on \(\Lambda_f\) for some \(A\), and path data \(W\) whose tested actions have the analyticity and jets of III.6.1,

\(\displaystyle D_a^2 \mathcal{T}^H_k (0, 0) [v, v] \geqslant \eta \sum_e |v_e |^2\)

for every tag \(k\) and free-bond field \(v\). One may take \(\eta = \min (a, \kappa) / (1 + \beta + \gamma)\), where \(\beta = 2 C_{\text{E02}}^2 L^4 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 |X|^2\), \(\gamma = 2 L^4\), and \(X = \{- R_S, \ldots, R_S \}^4\). Thus \(\eta\) is independent of volume, forest, \(B\) beyond its floor, and \(W\). Status: theorem conditional on E02, proved in III.10. Lean: Bid.F01.

Proposition III.6.4 (branch, tangent and value; F04). Let \(S\) be larger, \(H\) a forest, \(B, C\) beds, \(p\) a clip, \(W\) a path datum, and \(T\) towers for \((S, B, C)\) satisfying diagonal identities. Assume every tested action has the analyticity and jets of III.6.1, a vertical gap \(a > 0\), and a floor \((v, u)\) for \(C\) on \(\Lambda_c\) for some clip \(q\) and real \(v, u\). There is a critical branch with

\(\displaystyle D \rho (0) = \text{rel}_H, \qquad D^2 [\mathcal{T}^H_k (\cdot, \rho (\cdot))] (0) [x, x'] = \langle dx, M_C dx' \rangle .\)

For every tag the stationary value is analytic with zero value and gradient. Status: theorem giving a germ. Lean: Bid.F04.

Proposition III.6.5 (forest independence; F05). Let \(S\) be larger, \(H, H'\) forests, \(W\) have bounds \((p, \sigma, C, \Lambda)\), and \(\rho, \rho'\) be critical branches with positive gaps \(a, b\), respectively. Given a local chart ratio \(\mathscr{J}\), for every tag and \(x\) near \(0\) the stationary values and thermal functions agree between forests. Status: theorem; thermal equality has additive constant zero. Lean: Bid.F05, Work.F05.same_thermal.

Proposition III.6.6 (assembled branch). For every \((G, L, J, K, a)\) with \(a > 0\) there is \(\eta > 0\): for every larger \(S\) with these parameters, forest \(H\), beds \(B, C\), clips \(p, q\), path datum \(W\) and towers \(T\), assume diagonal identities for \((S, B, C)\), floors \((a, A)\) for \((B, p)\) on \(\Lambda_f\) and \((v, u)\) for \((C, q)\) on \(\Lambda_c\) for some real \(A, v, u\), bounds \((p, \sigma, C_0, \Lambda)\) for \(W\), attachment to \(B\), and \(B\) sworn. Then III.6.3 holds with \(\eta\) and there is a branch satisfying III.6.4. Status: theorem with no work-item assumptions. Lean: Bid.Scene, Integrated.stationary_scene.

Proposition III.6.7 (volume-independent ball). (a) Assign finite horizontal and vertical coordinate sets \(\iota, \tau\) to torus sites by \(\ell\), with multiplicity at most \(K_0\), using sup norms and periodic max-distance. Fix \(C_0, a_0, r, m > 0\), integer \(n \geqslant 3\). Suppose \(f : \mathbb{R}^{\iota \sqcup \tau} \to \mathbb{R}\) is analytic on \(\|z\|_{\infty} < r\) and, for every such \(z\), \(1 \leqslant k \leqslant n\), coordinate tuple and \(1 \leqslant j \leqslant k\),

\(\displaystyle | \partial_{q_1} \cdots \partial_{q_k} f (z) | \leqslant C_0 e^{- a_0 \sum_i \text{dist} (\ell (q_j), \ell (q_i))} .\)

If \(\nabla_{\tau} f (0) = 0\) and \(D^2_{\tau \tau} f (0) \geqslant mI\), there are \(0 < r_{\text{hor}} \leqslant r_{\text{zone}} \leqslant r_{\text{safe}} < r\), depending only on \((K_0, C_0, a_0, r, m)\), and a map \(g : \mathbb{R}^{\iota} \to \mathbb{R}^{\tau}\) with \(g (0) = 0\) such that for \(\|x\|_{\infty} < r_{\text{hor}}\), \(g\) is analytic at \(x\), has norm at most \(r_{\text{zone}} / 2\), and is the unique vertical critical point in the closed \(r_{\text{zone}}\)-ball.

(b) Fix \(G\), real \(\mu, C\), order \(J\), choices \(K\), and integer \(n \geqslant 3\). Auxiliary constants can be chosen before \(L\) so that (a) applies to every clip \(p\), real \(\Lambda\), larger \(S\) of order \(J\) with choices \(K\), forest \(H\), and \(W\) with bounds \((p, \mu, C, \Lambda)\). One branch serves all tags; radii depend on the auxiliary constants, \(p, L\), but not \(c, f, H, \Lambda\) or \(W\) beyond its bounds. Coarse coordinates are located at fine block roots. Status: theorem. Lean: Receipt.throughout, Before.graph.

Proposition III.6.8 (localized derivatives). For every integer \(N \geqslant 0\), assume III.6.7(b), choose auxiliary constants at order \(N + 3\), and take any branch satisfying its analytic, size and uniqueness conclusions. For every tag, on the closed joint \(r_{\text{safe}}\)-ball the vertical Hessian is analytic, \(\geqslant m / 2\), with localized coefficient derivatives through \(N + 1\). Every principal sub-block shifted by \(tI\), \(t \geqslant 0\), is invertible: its inverse is exponentially localized with constant \(O ((1 + t)^{- 1})\), has localized derivatives through \(N + 1\) and \(\ell^2\) norm at most \((m / 2 + t)^{- 1}\); its log determinant is analytic with localized derivatives of orders \(1, \ldots, N + 1\), bounded proportionally to \((1 + t)^{- 1}\). On the horizontal ball, \(g\) and its graph have localized derivatives of orders \(1, \ldots, N + 2\), the classical action of orders \(1, \ldots, N + 3\), and the Hessian along the branch has the same block package. Constants depend only on the corresponding bounds and radii. No volume-independent value bound for the action or log determinant is asserted. Status: theorem. Lean: Counter.service, BlockPack.

Proposition III.6.9 (one iteration level). Assume III.2's pre-\(L\) constants, warm-up and paid schedule; a horizon of beds \(M_{N, m}\) and towers with prescribed strip/derivative bounds, floors on every torus, diagonal/stack identities and identity diagonal at cutoff (Suffices); sworn beds at the periods used; harmonic towers (zero-momentum two-form coefficient independent of fine block residue); a levelwise gap quote supplied by Iteration.post; a local chart ratio; and the fresh-data hypotheses. These supply bounded fresh cubic, quartic and loop atoms for every admissible datum and family of branches satisfying (i) below, classical/thermal matchings on chosen forests/tags per period (possibly different choices), and cutoff cubic/quartic atoms.

For every \(m < N\) and path datum \(T\) funded at \(m + 1\), there are \(R\) funded at \(m\), branches \(\rho_{p, F}\) for every period and forest, and \(\alpha \in \mathbb{R}\), such that:

  • (i) Each branch satisfies III.6.6 with the level-\((m + 1)\) gap, tower map and bed \(M_{N, m}\).

  • (ii) \(| \alpha + \kappa_G \log L_m | + 1 \leqslant \text{drift}_{m + 1}\).

  • (iii) For every period, forest and tag, the stationary tested action for \(T\) with the level-\((m + 1)\) clip has the same jets through order four as \(\mathcal{M}_q (R ; e^x)\), with level-\(m\) clip \(q\).

  • (iv) Every thermal function is analytic with positive Hessian determinant near zero. For all tags and real parts \(w_1, w_2\) of complex plane waves with every axis/colour coefficient of modulus at most one,

\(\displaystyle |D^2 \Theta^F_k (0) [w_1, w_2] - D^2 (\alpha \mathcal{M}_q^{(2)} (R) + \text{loop}_R) (0) [w_1, w_2] | \leqslant | \Lambda_c | \Gamma \lambda_{m + 1} e^{- \gamma c} .\)

Here \(\Gamma, \gamma > 0\) are chosen after \(L\); \(R, \alpha\) serve all periods and forests. \(\mathcal{M}^{(2)}\) is the full symmetrized Wilson-plus-row-square term, including higher-order dependence. There is also a funded datum at cutoff \(N\) whose main term has Wilson-energy jets through order four. Status: theorem under these hypotheses. Lean: Iteration.extend, Iteration.peak.

3. Strategy

Forest gauge removes blockwise gauge directions; curvature and the predictor penalty control the remaining links. The vertical gap gives a stationary germ, with tangent and quadratic value identified by the towers. Under forest changes, the Hessian's squared Jacobian cancels the Haar-density ratio in the thermal function.

Exponential inverse-Hessian decay supplies a volume-independent sup-norm bound for Newton contraction. Differentiated stationarity and resolvent identities control localized derivatives. The iteration joins transported atoms to supplied fresh classical and one-loop data.

4. Steps

  1. Analyticity and jets. Exponential summability gives normally convergent holomorphic row/atom series; quadratic limits and the floor identity give III.6.1 after chart composition. Work.F02.answer, Work.F03.answer. (proof read; metric and cubic/quartic limits: statement only)

  2. Gap. Linearize E02, square and sum link bounds, then use the floor and penalty to control vertical directions. Work.F01.answer.

  3. Branch and tangent. Tags agree near identity; apply the analytic implicit function theorem, then differentiate stationarity and use \(D^2 \mathcal{T} (0) [(y, b), (x, \text{rel}_H x)] = \langle dy, M_C dx \rangle\) to identify the tangent and stationary quadratic value. Work.F04.answer.

  4. Forest comparison. Transport critical graphs by root-trivial gauge transformations; Haar change of variables cancels the determinant Jacobian. Work.F05.answer.

  5. Uniform radius. Combes–Thomas decay gives summable inverse rows; Newton contraction yields the ball, using a direct shell estimate and Wilson positivity without beds or attachment. inverse_sealed, Receipt.throughout, Before.graph. (proof read; direct shell/action-derivative estimates: statement only)

  6. Localized jets. Differentiate \(D (M^{- 1}) = - M^{- 1} (DM) M^{- 1}\) and \(D \log \det M = \text{tr} (M^{- 1} DM)\), control the index sums by exponential convolution, and compose with the stationary graph. inverse_lines, Counter.service. (proof read for inverse induction and assembly; convolution and composition: statement only)

  7. Iteration. Join fresh atoms to inherited atoms, whose cubic and quartic charges carry factors \(L^{- 2}\) and \(L^{- 4}\); use the schedule for the drift bound. Match jets on reference forests and transfer the classical and thermal matchings to every forest and tag. Iteration.extend. (proof read for assembly; inherited-atom bounds and matching: statement only)

5. Where the difficulty lies

Curvature alone leaves flat and gauge directions. The gap is uniform in volume; no lower bound uniform in \(L\) is asserted. A spectral gap alone gives no uniform sup-norm radius: exponential summability is also needed. The conclusions concern local stationarity and ball uniqueness, without global constrained minimization.

6. Notation

  • \(P\): predictor derivative; \(\iota\): linearized replica; \(d\): lattice exterior derivative.

  • A bed \(B\) consists of translation-invariant plaquette kernels, with metric \(M_B\), roots \(R_B\) and diagonal kernel \(D_B\) (Bed). A floor \((a, A)\) includes \(M_B = c_p I + R_B^{\top} R_B\), metric/diagonal inverse identities, \(\hat{M}_B (0) = I\), \(M_B d = D_B d\), symmetry, and quadratic bounds \(aI \leqslant M_B, D_B \leqslant AI\) (Floor). Attachment identifies row coefficients, summed by offset and plaquette type, with \(R_B\); swornness means signed-coordinate-permutation invariance of \(\langle dA, M_B dA \rangle\) (Attached, Sworn).

  • Towers satisfy Towers.Diagonal. With \(K_B = d^{\top} M_B d\) and linearized block average \(\bar{Q}\), their map obeys \(K_B \mathcal{W}= \bar{Q}^{\top} K_C\), \(\bar{Q} \mathcal{W}= 1 - \kappa^{- 1} K_C\); \(\text{rel}_H = (\hat{\mathcal{W}}_H - \iota) |_{H^{\text{fr}}}\), where \(\hat{\mathcal{W}}_H\) is its forest-gauge transform.

  • A clip \(p\) carries \(c_p > 0\). Path data \(W\) contain row series of transported plaquette variables and invariant loop, cubic and quartic atoms. Bounds \((p, \sigma, C, \Lambda)\) require exponential-length summability, row/cubic/quartic bounds \(C\), loop bound \(\Lambda\), row normalization \(c_p \delta_{ij} + \sum_k r_{ki} r_{kj} = \delta_{ij}\), and summable vanishing loop zeroth and first offset moments in every offset component, for every pair of plaquette types and colours (Bounds). Here \(r_{ij}\) sums row-\(i\) coefficients of plaquette type \(j\). “Funded” further specifies root-kernel rows on fixed coordinate paths, level bounds and weighted cubic/quartic caps (Work item).

  • A local chart ratio has positive measurable analytic density \(J_{\mathscr{J}}\), \(J_{\mathscr{J}} (0) = 1\), on a positive-radius ball where \(\exp\) is injective with open image; a positive scale times this density pushes forward to probability Haar measure restricted to that image (LocalRatio). The thermal Hessian uses the orthonormal colour basis.

  • Localization means \(| \partial_{q_1} \cdots \partial_{q_k} A_{ij} | \leqslant C_k e^{- a_k (\text{dist} (i, j) + \sum_{\nu} \text{dist} (i, q_{\nu}))}\) using assigned sites; omit \(j\) for maps, and use III.6.7(a) for scalars.
