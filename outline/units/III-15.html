1. What this unit proves

Proposition III.15.1 (pair-step rates). Fix \(\rho > 0\), \(C \geqslant 0\), \(r > \rho\), \(d \in \mathbb{N}\), \(e \in \mathbb{R}\), \(q > 0\). Put

\(\displaystyle s = p (y) \sqrt{\beta / y}, \quad a = Ce^{- rs^2}, \quad b = C (1 +\mathcal{B}(y))^d p (y)^e g (y)^q, \quad \kappa = \rho s^2, \quad \gamma = e^{\kappa} a + 2 b.\)

For all sufficiently large \(y > 1\), uniformly for \(\beta \in [y / 4, 4 y]\) and integers \(0 \leqslant B \leqslant \mathcal{B} (y)\),

\(\displaystyle \kappa, a \geqslant 0, \qquad 0 \leqslant b \leqslant 1, \qquad (2 B + 1)^4 \gamma \leqslant 1, \qquad \text{quote} (\kappa, \gamma) \leqslant e^{- \kappa} .\)

The threshold depends only on the fixed parameters. Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Progress.DirectedRecipe.Payments.Pairing.currency.

Proposition III.15.2 (hard–soft merging). Fix a parameter point with \(K \geqslant 6\), direction power \(P_0 \geqslant 2\), \(B_P \geqslant 1\), \(\sigma > 0\), \(0 < t_P \leqslant 1\). Assume countably indexed regular terms \(E_i\), forced activities \(F_i\) on \(X_i\), and real corrections \(\ell_i\) on nonempty \(X'_i\). Require support regularity, flat-field vanishing and vanishing affine Hessian on nonwrapping supports for \(E_i\); gauge invariance, measurability and dependence on supported links and final flags for \(F_i\), their eligibility tests and \(\ell_i\); eligibility whenever \(F_i \ne 0\); translation/reflection covariance for all families, with prescribed permutations, support and inventory transformations for regular and forced families; and \(\ell_i = 0\) on its small-field region. Suppose the regular family's nonnegative derivative bounds through \(K\) on small-field supports, in prescribed directions, have weighted anchored sum \(\leqslant \delta\); \(|F_i (U) | \leqslant p_i\), \(| \ell_i (U) | \leqslant q_i\) for all \(i, U\), with \(p_i, q_i \geqslant 0\); and

\(\displaystyle \text{Hit}_{B_P} (p ; \sigma + 1) \leqslant a, \qquad \text{Hit}_{B_P} (q ; \sigma + 1) \leqslant b.\)

Assume III.15.1's inequalities with \(B = B_P\), replacing its last bound by \(\text{quote} (\kappa, \gamma) \leqslant w\). Then the fusion input condition holds with \((\delta, w)\): original bounds \(\delta, a\) persist, the soft family has a summable uniform majorant, and \((F_i) \sqcup (e^{\ell_i} - 1)\) has weights \((e^{\kappa} p_i) \sqcup (2 q_i)\) and hit bound \(\gamma\), with \(|F_i | \leqslant e^{- \kappa} (e^{\kappa} p_i)\) and \(|e^{\ell_i} - 1| \leqslant 2 q_i\). III.10's fusion gives norm \(\leqslant w\). Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Progress.DirectedRecipe.Payments.tender.

For III.15.3–4 fix \(K \geqslant 6\), \(P \geqslant 2\), \(0 < t \leqslant 1\). Directions satisfy \(\|A_e \| \leqslant t (1 + \text{dist} (e, o))^P\).

Proposition III.15.3 (slots). For every \(\ell \in \mathbb{N}\), slot \(s\) with \(|s| \leqslant \ell\), finite \(S \subset \mathbb{Z}^4\) containing its stencil, and site \(o\), the slot is \(K\) times left-differentiable. If \(U\) is small-field on wrapped \(o + S\), every derivative of order \(m \leqslant K\) in admissible directions has norm \(\leqslant C_s (P, K, \ell) t\), where

\(\displaystyle C_s (P, K, \ell) = (1 + \kappa_G^{- 1} \|1\|) [2 (\kappa_G + 1) 2^P (\ell + 1)^{P + 1}]^K .\)

At order zero the bound is \(t\); constants are independent of volume and \(t\). Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Next.Scheduling.Wages.slotPays.

Proposition III.15.4 (atoms). For every \(k \in \mathbb{N}\), \(k\)-leg atom \(a\), site \(o\), and \(U\) small-field on its footprint, \(a (\cdot, o)\) is \(K\) times left-differentiable, with every admissible derivative through \(K\) bounded by

\(\displaystyle |a| 2^K [2^K C_s (P, K, \text{len} (a)) t]^k .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Next.Scheduling.Wages.fin.

Proposition III.15.5 (launch data). Let \(G\) be simple. Fix a sheet of profiles \(P_j\), kernel, compatible price table, targets \(R_{ji}\) with \(\rho_{R_{ji}} < - 3 / 8\) for all \(j, i\), tolerances \(h_{ji} > 0\), libraries \(C_{ji}\) of order \(P_i\), and marks \(r_{ji}\). There exist \(f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) and \(C'_{ji} \geqslant 1\) giving quotation bounds with fine/coarse exponents \((\sigma_j, \sigma_i + 2)\) and factor \(C'_{ji} L^{- 5}\). These require support enlargement by \(r_{ji} \mathcal{B} (x)\), coarse power equal to that of \(P_i\), fine exponent \(\sigma_j\), \(B_P =\mathcal{B} (x)\), \(B_B \leqslant 2 B_P\), \(t_P \leqslant 2 t_B\), and acceptance. For every \(u \geqslant 0\) satisfying local analytic smallness and every \(\tau \geqslant 0\), the bound holds. The quotation inequality is \(e^{(\sigma_i + 2) N_{B_P} (Y)} \tau \hspace{0.17em} \text{returnFee} \leqslant C'_{ji} L^{- 5} e^{\sigma_j N_{B_B} (X)} \tau\). For each stage pair \((j, i)\) and every block side \(L \geqslant f_j\), III.15.6 holds and

\(\displaystyle (\text{rel}_{R_{ji}} + 1) 10 c_{h_{R_{ji}} + 5} / L + 2 \varepsilon_{R_{ji}} \leqslant \sigma_j, \quad 20 (\sigma_i + 2) \text{toll} (r_{ji}) \leqslant L \sigma_j, \quad C'_{ji} / L \leqslant h_{ji} .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: YM.RemainderComparison.Chron.sails.

Proposition III.15.6 (large-side comparison). For simple \(G\), kernel \(K\) and target \(Q\) with \(\rho_Q < - 3 / 8\), there exists \(n\) such that for every \(L \geqslant n\) and branching patch, every \(W, \mu > 0\), order datum at \(W\), songbook and scrollwork at side \(L\), \(\chi\), stages \(E, E'\), rays satisfying the heart condition for \((E', \text{e}_Q)\), library of order \(E'\), record with parameter \(\leqslant 1 / 4\), \(j \in \mathbb{N}\), reserve \(f\), and \(\eta, \delta > 0\), there exist a financed quarry \(D\) at rate \(\rho_Q\), with \(d (D) > 0\) and paint supply, and \(P_1\). For all \(x \geqslant P_1\), poster comparison, chart conditions and instructions hold; active, resonance and tolerance conditions hold for every \(\beta \in [\beta_F / 4, 4 \beta_F]\) and every point tempered to \(E'\). The threshold \(n\) is independent of auxiliary data; \(D, P_1\) may depend on them. Status: theorem. Lean: YM.RemainderComparison.Cadenza.buoys.

Proposition III.15.7 (delay enlargement). Fix a sheet, compatible target/expense tables \(R, X\), sides \(L_j\), protection, register, firmament, tolerances, libraries and marks as in III.15.5, and records \(u_{ji}\). The register supplies positive \(W_{ji}, \mu_{ji}\) and orders on stage pairs; each finite request family supplies scrollwork, songbook, grade/orders, reserve, positive constants, and an extra condition eventually true for every financed quarry. Assume launch thresholds \(k_j \leqslant L_j\), \(\varepsilon (u_{ji}) \leqslant 1 / 4\) on stage pairs with \(i\) at least the first stage, and \(\sigma_i + 3 < \text{e}_{R_{ji}}\) for all \(j, i\). For every initial delay \(T_0\), there exist \(W \geqslant T_0\) and quarries \(V_{jig}\) for every request slot at every \((j, i)\), chosen before any \(U \geqslant W\). For every such \(U\), launch data persist; on branching stage pairs with \(j \geqslant 4\) and \(i\) at least the first stage, every request has the heart property, positive \(d\), paint supply, III.15.6's allotment at threshold \(\beta_j^{\ast} (U)\), and its extra condition above that threshold. On all stage pairs with \(i\) at least the first stage, requests are tiled whenever \(x \geqslant \beta_j^{\ast} (U)\) and

\(\displaystyle c \geqslant \text{shells}_j (U) (1 +\mathcal{B}(x))^{\text{growth}_j (U)} .\)

Tiling means \(2 z_g (x) < c\) and \(4 \text{halo}_g (x) + 16 \leqslant c\). Delay ordering increases entry couplings, scale-entry functions, shell counts and growth exponents componentwise. Status: theorem. Lean: YM.RemainderComparison.Chron.grows.

3. Strategy

Retain \(e^{- \rho s^2}\) from the hard bound and use the remaining Gaussian decay to absorb support counting; soft bounds decay by their negative coupling power. Express slots as single group words: unitarity controls derivatives, and Leibniz gives \(t^k\) for atoms.

Choose the block-side threshold before auxiliary data, then comparison functions and entry couplings. Negative activity rate survives the tree weight \(\beta_F^{3 / 8}\); finitely many requests per stage permit common delay enlargement.

4. Steps

  1. Fusion. Rescaled hard weights and doubled soft weights add to \(\gamma\); the exponential inequality bounds the Mayer terms. YM.Progress.DirectedRecipe.Payments.tender.

  2. Observable bounds. Differentiate the detour word, use unitarity, then multiply slot estimates. YM.Next.Scheduling.Wages.slotPays; YM.Next.Scheduling.Wages.fin.

  3. Comparison functions. Choose reserve functions conditional on local derivative estimates, charts and coefficient matching. YM.RemainderComparison.Herbal.compile (proof read; analytic inputs not expanded).

  4. Marked outputs. For the conditional marked-output estimate, separate single remaining occurrences from clusters with further occurrences and from large supports. The \(L^{- 5}\) quotation summed over \(L^4\) sites supplies the \(C_0 / L\) term; the other contributions have negative rate. Occurrences are counted outside inserted leaves, with at least one marked. YM.RemainderComparison.Solo.atlas (proof read; singleton input not expanded).

  5. Launch and enlargement. Combine comparison and tail bounds with quotation thresholds, then take finite stagewise maxima. YM.RemainderComparison.Chron.sails; YM.RemainderComparison.Chron.grows.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The bounds must be uniform in coupling bands and volume; the block-side threshold precedes all auxiliary choices, while comparison functions precede the entry coupling. Part C's conditional comparison conclusions reach the selected run but are discarded by the final construction-run theorem.

6. Notation

\(g (\beta) = \beta^{- 1 / 2}\), \(p (x) = e^{(\log \log x)^2}\), \(t (x) = g (x) p (x)\), \(\mathcal{B} (x) = \lceil (\log x)^2 \rceil\), \(\beta_F = x + b_G \log L\); \(x\) is coarse inverse coupling. \(\text{quote} (\kappa, \gamma) = e^{- \kappa} (e^{\gamma} - 1)\). Hit means \(\sum_{i : z \in X_i} f_i e^{aN_M (X_i)} \leqslant \gamma\) at every site \(z\), for weights \(f_i \geqslant 0\); \(N_M\) counts a minimal connected covering by \(M\)-balls. Stage pairs have \(i \in \{j, j - 1\}\); \(\sigma_j\) is the profile's support exponent. A quarry is a record of bounding functions; financing specifies reserve/rate bounds. Rate \(\leqslant a\) means \(|w| \leqslant \beta_F^v\) eventually for every \(v > a\); a reserve satisfies \(|w| \leqslant p (\beta_F)^k\) eventually for some \(k \in \mathbb{N}\).

A slot is curvature transported along a walk \(\omega\), with length \(| \omega | + 4\); atoms are coefficient-weighted products of slots, with coefficient norm \(|a| = \sum_b |c_b |\) and total length \(\text{len} (a)\). Small-field means every touching plaquette has chord distance \(\leqslant t\) from identity; \(\kappa_G\) is the colour scale and \(\|1\|\) the Frobenius norm of identity.

For a target \(Q\), \(\text{rel}_Q = \text{e}_Q + 2 \varepsilon_Q + \delta_Q\); \(h_Q\) is its halo and \(c_{h_Q + 5}\) its pre-cost. Targets carry their chart/local-ratio conditions, \(\rho_Q \in (- 1 / 2, 0)\), \(\text{e}_Q \geqslant 0\), \(\varepsilon_Q, \delta_Q > 0\), \(h_Q \geqslant 500\), \(4 \varepsilon_Q\) below the top support exponent and \(8 \varepsilon_Q\) below both chart widths. The heart condition is

\(\displaystyle 0 \leqslant \sigma_{\ast}, \quad \sigma_{E'} \leqslant \sigma_{\ast}, \quad \sigma_{\ast} + 1 \leqslant a_{\ast}, \quad \varepsilon_{\ast} > 0, \quad a_{\ast} + 3 \varepsilon_{\ast} \leqslant \text{e}_Q,\)
\(\displaystyle \text{ord} (E') \leqslant J_{\ast}, \quad \text{ord} (E') + (p_{\ast} + 1) + p_{\ast} + 2 \leqslant n_{\ast}, \quad p_{\ast} + 1 \leqslant s_{\ast}, \quad k_{\ast} + 1 \leqslant s_{\ast},\)

with starred ray parameters. Tempered points have radius \(\mathcal{B} (x)\), threshold \(t (x) \leqslant 2 t (\beta_F)\), and the stage's order, power and support exponent.
