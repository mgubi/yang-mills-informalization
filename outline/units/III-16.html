1. What this unit proves

Proposition III.16.1 (integrated positivity). Let \(X\) be compact metric with finite Borel measure \(\mu\), \(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}\) sets, \(K : \mathcal{A}^2 \to \mathbb{C}\) positive semidefinite, and \(g : \mathcal{B} \times X \to \mathcal{A}\). If \((x, y) \mapsto K (g (b, x), g (c, y))\) is continuous for every \(b, c\), then \(\tilde{K} (b, c) = \iint K (g (b, x), g (c, y)) \hspace{0.17em} d \mu (y) d \mu (x)\) is positive semidefinite. Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Analysis.NNKernel.accumulate.

Proposition III.16.2 (trace Hölder). For finite complex square matrices of the same size \(A \succeq 0, b_1, \ldots, b_n\), integers \(q, M \geqslant 1\), \(Z_A = \text{tr} A^M > 0\), and \(v \in \mathbb{C}^n\) with \(\text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} v_k \geqslant 0\) and \(n / (2 q) + \sum_k \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} v_k / M = 1\),

\(\displaystyle \left| \text{tr} \prod_k b_k A^{v_k} \right| \leqslant C_A, \qquad C_A = \prod_k \|b_k \|_{2 q} Z_A^{1 - n / (2 q)} .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Holder.estimation.

Proposition III.16.3 (moving matrix gaps). For such matrices, integers \(q, w \geqslant 1\), \(M = 2 qw\), \(Z_A > 0\), and \(Y, Y' \in \mathbb{N}^n\), suppose \(nw + \sum_k Y_k = M\) and \(Y' = Y + \ell (e_i - e_j)\) for \(i \ne j\), \(\ell \in \mathbb{N}\), with \(e_i\) the coordinate vectors. If \(r > 0\), \(Y_k > 2 r\) for every \(k\), and \(\ell \leqslant r\), then

\(\displaystyle \left| \text{tr} \prod_k b_k A^{Y'_k} - \text{tr} \prod_k b_k A^{Y_k} \right| \leqslant C_A \ell / r.\)

Status: theorem. Lean: YM.Holder.finite_lip.

Proposition III.16.4 (moving kernel gaps). On \(X, \mu\) as in III.16.1, let \(K\) be continuous positive semidefinite and \(B_1, \ldots, B_n\) continuous complex kernels. For integers \(q, w \geqslant 1\), \(M = 2 qw\), real \(Z = \text{Tr} (K^M) > 0\), and \(Y, Y', \ell, r\) satisfying all gap conditions of III.16.3, assume real \(c_k\) satisfy \(| \text{Tr} ((B_k^{\ast} B_k)^q) |^{1 / (2 q)} \leqslant c_k Z^{1 / (2 q)}\). Then

\(\displaystyle | \text{Tr} (B_1 K^{Y'_1} \cdots B_n K^{Y'_n}) - \text{Tr} (B_1 K^{Y_1} \cdots B_n K^{Y_n}) | \leqslant Z \prod_k c_k \hspace{0.17em} \ell / r.\)

Status: theorem. Lean: YM.TimeKernel.passage.

Proposition III.16.5 (Laurent estimates). Let \(\alpha\) be finite and \(\eta > 0\). For holomorphic, \(2 \pi\)-periodic \(u : S_{\eta} \to \mathbb{C}\), define \(\hat{u}_p (x) = \int_{[0, 1]^{\alpha}} u (p + 2 \pi \theta) e^{- 2 \pi i \theta \cdot x} d \theta\), \(\hat{u} = \hat{u}_0\). The torus mean is independent of \(p \in S_{\eta}\), and \(\hat{u}_p (x) = e^{ip \cdot x} \hat{u} (x)\). If \(|u| \leqslant K\), then \(| \hat{u}_p (x) | \leqslant K\) and, for \(H \geqslant 0\), \(b > 0\), \(H + b < \eta\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_x e^{H |x|_1} | \hat{u} (x) | \leqslant K (2 (1 + b) / b)^{\text{card} \hspace{0.17em} \alpha} .\)

If \(|u| \leqslant K\), the Laurent series reconstructs \(u\) absolutely throughout \(S_{\eta}\): \(u (p) = \sum_x \hat{u} (x) e^{ip \cdot x}\).

Conversely, for \(f : \mathbb{Z}^{\alpha} \to \mathbb{C}\), if \(\sum_x e^{\eta |x|_1} |f (x) | \leqslant W\), then \(| \hat{f} | \leqslant W\) on \(| \text{Im} \hspace{0.17em} p_a | \leqslant \eta\). For every \(0 < \sigma < \eta\), \(\hat{f}\) is holomorphic on \(S_{\sigma}\), with derivative norm and Lipschitz constant at most \(W / (\eta - \sigma)\) for the sup norm. For real \(p\), \(\widehat{\text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} f} (p) = (\hat{f} (p) + \overline{\hat{f} (- p)}) / 2\). Status: theorems. Lean: full_turn, Vane.issues, Vane.paints, Width.estimate, readDeriv, readDiff, readRealPart.

Proposition III.16.6 (Fourier and Bloch identities). For positive integers \(n, c, L\), finite internal index sets \(\alpha, \beta\), and real entrywise summable \(R : \mathbb{Z}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^{\alpha \times \beta}\), put \(R_n ((y, a), (y', b)) = \sum_{x \in \mathbb{Z}^4 : y + x \equiv y' \pmod{n}} R_{ab} (x)\). Then

\(\displaystyle F_n F_n^{\ast} = F_n^{\ast} F_n = n^4 I, \qquad R_n (F_n \otimes I_{\beta}) = (F_n \otimes I_{\alpha}) \text{diag}_p \hat{R} (2 \pi p / n) .\)

This includes \(p = 0\). With \(f = cL\), reindexing fine momenta as \(k_{p, l} = 2 \pi (p + cl) / f\), \(p \in \Lambda_c, l \in \Lambda_L\), gives \(\Phi \Phi^{\ast} = f^4 I\) and the same intertwining identity with \(\Phi\), \(R_f\), and blocks \(\text{diag}_{p, l} \hat{R} (k_{p, l})\). For every \(p \in \mathbb{C}^4\), \(E_L (\bar{p})^{\ast} E_L (p) = L^4 I\). Status: theorems. Lean: circulant, gram, coGram, readingsGram, aligns, straightens.

Proposition III.16.7 (matrix root). For \(m > 0\), nonempty finite matrix indices and complex square \(X\),

\(\displaystyle \Phi_m (X) = \sqrt{m} \sum_{k \geqslant 0} \binom{1 / 2}{k} (X / m - I)^k\)

is holomorphic on \(\|X / m - I\| < 1\), bounded on every smaller closed ball, and commutes with entrywise conjugation. If \(v > 0\) and \(X\) is Hermitian with \(vI \leqslant X \leqslant mI\) as quadratic forms, its value is Hermitian, squares to \(X\), and lies between \(\sqrt{v} I\) and \(\sqrt{m} I\). Status: theorem. Lean: binRoot_on_band, binRoot_deriv, binSum_bound, binRoot_conj.

Proposition III.16.8 (Maxwell jets). Fix the compact matrix gauge group and orthonormal real colour coordinates. For every integer \(j \geqslant 0\), curl is an isomorphism \(\mathcal{A}_j \to \mathcal{C}_j\), with inverse \(A_i = (j + 2)^{- 1} \sum_k x_k F_{ki}\). For every smooth antisymmetric colour-valued field satisfying Bianchi and Maxwell on an open \(U \subset \mathbb{R}^4\), its \(j\)-th Taylor coefficient lies in \(\mathcal{S}_j\) at every \(x \in U\).

Off zero, \(G_0 = (4 \pi^2 |x|^2)^{- 1}\) is harmonic; set \(\psi_{b, km} = \delta_{kb} \partial_m G_0 - \delta_{mb} \partial_k G_0\), \(C_{ab, km} = \partial_a \psi_{b, km} - \partial_b \psi_{a, km}\). In either index pair, \(C\) is smooth, antisymmetric and solves both equations; \(C_{ab, km} (z) = C_{km, ab} (z) = C_{ab, km} (- z)\).

For all \(j, l \geqslant 0\), real linear probes \(p, q\) on \(\mathcal{S}_j, \mathcal{S}_l\) have a bilinear contraction \(\kappa (p, q) (z)\), independent of extensions to unrestricted tensors, smooth for \(z \ne 0\), with \(\kappa (p, q) (z) = \kappa (q, p) (- z)\). For coordinate probes \(p (F) = F_{ab} [u]_c\), \(q (F) = F_{km} [v]_d\), \(u \in (\mathbb{R}^4)^j, v \in (\mathbb{R}^4)^l\),

\(\displaystyle \kappa (p, q) (z) = \delta_{cd} (- 1)^l c_l (t \mapsto c_j C_{ab, km} (t) [u]) (z) [v] .\)

Status: theorems; contraction definition. Lean: potential, WaveOn.jets, Bare.equation, legKernel, reads_mixed.

Proposition III.16.9 (labels). Fix real bases of \(\mathcal{A}_j\); coordinate weight is \(j + 2\). For each integer \(d \geqslant 0\), \(\text{Label}_d\) consists of complex polynomials of weight \(\leqslant d\) invariant under simultaneous adjoint action. Real jet evaluation determines polynomials. There are finitely many weight-\(\leqslant d\) monomials, so \(\text{Label}_d\) is finite-dimensional. Every label decomposes into invariant bihomogeneous components (weight and degree both \(\leqslant d\)), commuting with conjugation and \(O (4)\). For \(s \in O (4)\), the action is \((s \cdot P) (A) = P (T_{s^{- 1}} A)\), where \((T_s A_j)_i (x) = \sum_k s_{ik} (A_j)_k (s^{- 1} x)\), and satisfies the group law. Simultaneously for all \(d\), there exist complex bases of real bihomogeneous labels containing \(e_0 = 1\), nested as sets of polynomials with no further prescribed ordering. Status: theorems. Lean: eval_ext, labels_finite, decompose, rotation_law, frames.

Proposition III.16.10 (Wick hierarchy). For every \(n \geqslant 0\), integer cutoffs \(d_i \geqslant 0\), distinct \(x_i \in \mathbb{R}^4\), and \(P_i \in \text{Label}_{d_i}\), restrict labels by \(A_j = \text{curl}^{- 1} F_j\), \(F_j \in \mathcal{S}_j\). Sum products of \(\kappa (\cdot, \cdot) (x_i - x_j)\) over pairings of monomial occurrences at different points, extending multilinearly. This correlation is smooth, kills any insertion vanishing on all shell jets, respects simultaneous permutations and insertion of unit labels at additional distinct points, and equals \(\prod_i a_i\) for constant insertions \(a_i \in \mathbb{C}\), including the empty product \(1\). Status: definitions and theorems. Lean: Wick.correlation, Wick.smooth, Wick.kills_equation, Wick.exchangeSites, Wick.adjoin, Wick.constants.

3. Strategy

Moving an insertion transfers units between two gaps. Continue these gaps holomorphically while preserving the Hölder balance; Cauchy's derivative estimate controls the change by \(\ell / r\). Sampling all kernels and adjoints with one partition passes this dimension-free estimate to cyclic integrals.

Shift Fourier contours to exchange strip holomorphy for exponential decay; diagonalize finite tori and construct matrix roots by a binomial series on a thinner strip. Radial gauge identifies potentials with closed jets; the free kernel descends to shell probes and supplies Wick correlations.

4. Steps

  1. Factor matrices into normalized entire paths with operator norm \(\leqslant 1\) on \(0 \leqslant \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} z \leqslant 1\) and trace norm \(\leqslant 1\) on its right boundary. At exponents with nonnegative real parts summing to \(1\), three-lines induction bounds the product trace by \(1\), giving dimension-free Hölder. Lean.

  2. Vary gaps along \(Y + z (e_i - e_j)\); Cauchy's estimate on radius-\(r\) circles gives III.16.3. Lean.

  3. Positive quadratic forms pass through quadrature, proving integrated positivity. Sample kernels with square-root cell masses; positivity, adjoints and cyclic traces survive, and quadrature passes the trace estimate to kernels. accumulate, Lean.

  4. Shift integration contours and sum geometric tails; Fourier inversion reconstructs symbols; termwise differentiation gives the reverse derivative estimate. Vane.paints, readDeriv.

  5. Character orthogonality diagonalizes periodized kernels and their alias blocks, retaining zero momentum. circulant, straightens.

  6. Diagonalization reduces the binomial matrix series to scalar square roots; Banach-algebra convergence gives holomorphy. binRoot_on_band.

  7. Euler's identity and Bianchi invert curl; shell restriction makes contractions independent of extensions. Lean, Lean.

  8. Finite Wick sums give smoothness; real bihomogeneous decomposition gives nested frames. Lean, Lean.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The trace bound must remain independent of matrix dimension as gaps vary and quadrature converges at fixed exponents and period. Strict gap inequalities avoid the exceptional zeroth-power convention. Laurent estimates lose strip width; complex alias adjoints require \(\bar{p}\). Maxwell contractions retain factorials, the colour normalization and the second-endpoint sign.

6. Notation

\(\| \cdot \|\): operator norm; \(\|b\|_{2 q} = (\text{tr} ((b^{\ast} b)^q))^{1 / (2 q)}\). Products are ordered. \(A^z = \sum_{\lambda > 0} e^{z \log \lambda} P_{\lambda}\) for spectral projections \(P_{\lambda}\); \(A^0\) projects onto the range. For \(L \geqslant 1\), \(\text{Tr} (k_1 \cdots k_L) = \int_{X^L} \prod_{l = 1}^L k_l (x_l, x_{l + 1}) \hspace{0.17em} d \mu^{\otimes L}\), \(x_{L + 1} = x_1\); powers repeat kernels; \(B^{\ast} (x, y) = \overline{B (y, x)}\). Positive semidefinite means every finite Gram matrix, allowing repeated points, is Hermitian positive semidefinite.

\(|x|_1 = \sum_a |x_a |\), \(S_{\eta} = \{p \in \mathbb{C}^{\alpha} : | \text{Im} \hspace{0.17em} p_a | < \eta \forall a\}\), \(\hat{f} (p) = \sum_x f (x) e^{ip \cdot x}\). \(\Lambda_n = (\mathbb{Z}/ n\mathbb{Z})^4\); \(F_n = (e^{2 \pi ip \cdot x / n})\), \(\Phi = (e^{ik_{p, l} \cdot y})_{y \in \Lambda_f, (p, l) \in \Lambda_c \times \Lambda_L}\), \(E_L (p) = (e^{i (p + 2 \pi l) \cdot x / L})_{x, l \in \Lambda_L}\). Residues use representatives \(0, \ldots, n - 1\).

Colour coordinates are orthonormal for \(- (\text{dim} \hspace{0.17em} \rho)^{- 1} \text{Re} \hspace{0.17em} \text{tr} (XY)\) in the fixed defining representation. \(\mathcal{A}_j\): colour-valued \(4\)-tuples of homogeneous degree-\((j + 1)\) potentials with \(x \cdot A = 0\); \(\mathcal{C}_j\): antisymmetric homogeneous degree-\(j\) curvature jets satisfying \(\partial_a F_{bc} + \partial_b F_{ca} + \partial_c F_{ab} = 0\); \(\mathcal{S}_j\): additionally divergence-free. \(c_j = D^j / j!\).
