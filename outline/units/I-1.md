# I.1. Lattice, symmetries, gauge groups and the Wilson measure

*Part I — Setting.* 3,701 lines of Lean in 595 declarations used by the main theorem.

**What this unit contains** (automatic summary from the Lean statements and documentation; the outline of its proof is in preparation).

Hyperoctahedral (signed permutation) symmetries of the lattice, skew generators, configurations and Lorentz/Poincare boosts (`Signed`, `Mink.Lorentz`). Pure support for every later symmetry argument.

Discrete torus, separation and wrap-around distance, paths, subdivision of the torus, and the schedule of spacings and block sizes (`Lattice.Site`, `Lattice.separation`, `Schedule`).

The gauge group as a closed matrix group, invariant forms on simple Lie algebras (`Gauge.invariant_scalar`), Haar measure of shells, adjoint Casimir constant, logarithm/exponential charts near the identity.

Space of lattice gauge configurations and the Wilson law on the torus; invariance of the physical measure under lattice symmetries.
