1. What this unit proves

Let \(G \subset U (n)\) be closed, with Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}\).

Proposition III.9.1 (slope). For simple nonabelian \(\mathfrak{g}\) with orthonormal basis \((b_i)\) in \(\langle X, Y \rangle = - \text{Retr} (XY) / n\), define \(C_G = - (\dim \mathfrak{g})^{- 1} \sum_i \text{tr}_{\mathfrak{g}} ((\text{ad} b_i)^2)\) and \(\kappa = 11 C_G / (24 \pi^2)\). Then \(\kappa > 0\). Status: definition and positivity theorem. Lean: slopeConstant_pos.

Proposition III.9.2 (reference scales). Fix \(\kappa > 0\), \(\bar{\beta} > 1\), an infinite sequence of odd \(L_m \geqslant 3\). Set

\(\displaystyle s_m = \sum_{i < m} \log L_i = \log (1 / a_m), \quad \hat{\beta}_m = \bar{\beta} + \kappa s_m, \quad M_m = e^{(\log \log \hat{\beta}_m)^2},\)
\(\displaystyle t_m = \hat{\beta}_m^{- 1 / 2} M_m, \quad \varepsilon_m = \hat{\beta}_m^{- 11 / 10}, \quad \tilde{\varepsilon}_m = e^{- M_m^{1 / 5}}, \quad B_m = \lceil (\log \hat{\beta}_m)^2 \rceil, \quad \mathfrak{B}_m = [\hat{\beta}_m / 2, 2 \hat{\beta}_m] .\)

Then \(\hat{\beta}_{m + 1} = \hat{\beta}_m + \kappa \log L_m\), \(\hat{\beta}\) is nondecreasing and diverges. Additional scale hypotheses are: \(t_m \leqslant 1\); sides at least \(100 (B_m + 1)\) and \(100 (R_m + 1)\) at levels \(m, m + 1\), for every volume; \(J_m \geqslant 4\) nondecreasing, divergent, increasing by at most one; nondecreasing derivative orders, spatial powers, support exponents and stage factors \(\ell_j\), with \(L_m = \ell_{J_{m + 1}}\); antitone \(0 < \theta_{J_m} \leqslant 1 / J_m\); \(\log L_m / s_m \to 0\); \(\omega_{J_{m + 1}} / \log L_m \leqslant J_{m + 1}^{- 2}\) and \(\log (1 + \text{paths} + \text{width}^{- 1} + \text{columns} + \text{relaxation} + \text{support exponent})_{J_{m + 1}} / \log L_m \leqslant J_{m + 1}^{- 2}\). Status: definitions and elementary theorems; scale hypotheses supplied by III.2. Lean: Calendar.Fits.

Proposition III.9.3 (selected histories). A selected history assigns representations \(\rho_{N, p, m}\) for all \(N, p\) and \(m \leqslant N\), with volume-independent paths \(P_{N, m}\), level norms and clip, stage path bounds and loop cap \(2 M_m^{\theta_{J_m}}\), and \(\beta_{N, p, m} \in \mathfrak{B}_m\). Regular activities are local, gauge invariant, vanish at the flat field, with zero affine Hessian on nonwrapping supports and bound \(\varepsilon_m\); forced activities obey II.2 with bound \(\tilde{\varepsilon}_m\); the family respects translations and signed permutations. At the top, \(v_N = 0\), \(\rho_N = e^{- \beta_N S_W}\); at the bottom, \(\beta_0 = \bar{\beta}\). For every \(m < N\),

\(\displaystyle \rho_m = T^{(m)}_{\beta_{m + 1}} \rho_{m + 1}, \qquad | \beta_{m + 1} - \beta_m - \kappa \log L_m | \leqslant \omega_{J_{m + 1}} .\)

For every \(\epsilon > 0\), eventually \(| \beta_{N, p, m} - \hat{\beta}_m | \leqslant \epsilon s_m\) uniformly in \(N \geqslant m, p\). Consequently \(\beta_{N, p, m} / s_m \to \kappa\) uniformly, including \(m = N\), and \(\int \rho_{N, p, m} \hspace{0.17em} d \text{Haar} = Z_W (\Lambda_{N, p} ; \beta_{N, p, N})\) for every \(m \leqslant N\). Status: specification; realized in III.13 using III.9.5 and III.10. Lean: History.Selected.

Proposition III.9.4 (reset along a curve). Fix scales meeting entry thresholds and kernel conditions, compatible horizon, Haar chart, positive post-block constants, planned kinetic data and recipe. For \(N, p, m < N\), let \(\rho'_x\), \(x \in I = [l, u]\), be continuous admissible fine representations, with \(l \leqslant u\); write \(\beta'_x = \beta (\rho'_x)\). Fix stage-pair reset constants \(c > 0\), \(0 < r \leqslant 1\), prescribed fine-stage \(g > 0\), and coarse-stage sensor \(\eta > 0\). Assume:

  • \(2 (c + 1) \varepsilon_m \leqslant \eta t_m^2 r\); a small-field probe satisfies \(\mathcal{K} (V) \geqslant | \Lambda_m | \eta t_m^2\).

  • Trial activities \(F (x, d)\), \(|d| \leqslant r\), have fixed addresses, valid allowances, coefficient continuity, and common summable norm majorants along subsequences of every convergent parameter sequence. For \(H_x = T^{(m)}_{\beta'_x} \rho'_x\), \(H_x (1), H_x (V) > 0\) and

\(\displaystyle | (\beta'_x + \alpha)\mathcal{K}(V) +\mathcal{L}(V) - \log (H_x (1) / H_x (V)) | \leqslant | \Lambda_m | c \varepsilon_m,\)
\(\displaystyle |\mathcal{E}_{F (x, b)} (V) -\mathcal{E}_{F (x, a)} (V) | \leqslant | \Lambda_m | c \varepsilon_m |b - a| .\)

  • The trial shape \(e^{- (\beta'_x + \alpha + d) \mathcal{K}-\mathcal{L}-\mathcal{E}_{F (x, d)}} \mathcal{G}_{F (x, d)}\) differs from \(H_x (U) / H_x (1)\) by at most \(C_{x, U} |f (x, d) |\), with finite \(C_{x, U} \geqslant 0\) independent of \(d\); forced activities have bound \(e^{- gM_{m + 1}^2}\); every exact trial passes the recipe.

There is continuous \(d : I \to [- r, r]\) giving continuous admissible \(\rho_x = H_x\), coupling \(\beta'_x + \alpha + d (x)\) and \(v_x = | \Lambda_m |^{- 1} \log H_x (1)\), retaining the stronger forced bound. Without the last bullet, the unique residual root still matches \(1, V\). Status: theorem. Lean: advance.

Proposition III.9.5 (explicit reset; program). Under III.9.4's datum, curve and constants, assume simplicity, \(\beta'_x \in \mathfrak{B}_{m + 1}\), and the early presentation

\(\displaystyle H_x = e^{\tau_x | \Lambda_m | - \beta'_x \mathcal{K}-\mathcal{L}- \sum_i \chi_{X_i} E_{x, i}} \mathcal{G}_x e^{- \alpha \mathcal{S}} .\)

Assume local gauge-invariant smooth regular functions with nonnegative sup-jet bounds \(h_{x, i}\) satisfying, at every anchor, \(\sum_{i : o_i = o} e^{(a + 3) \text{cover}_{B_m} (X_i)} h_{x, i} \leqslant H_x^E\), forced-family conditions and site-pinned bound \(w_x\) at exponent \(a + 1\), translation and point-group symmetry, fixed addresses and III.9.4's continuity/tails. Require priced reset, compatible clip, width and cubic conditions, \(H_x^E \leqslant c \varepsilon_m / 4\), regular/forced reorganization estimates fitting numerical bounds \(e_x, k_x\) that imply both allowances and \(e^{- gM_{m + 1}^2}\), and recipe acceptance and continuation. Then

\(\displaystyle d (x) = \delta_x, \quad t_m^2 | \delta_x | \leqslant H_x^E, \quad v_x = \tau_x - | \Lambda_m |^{- 1} \sum_i E_{x, i} (1) .\)

All III.9.4 conclusions hold. A Gaussian-sector formulation additionally requires integration licenses and equality of that integral with the push. If these inputs hold at every level along every continuous admissible curve with couplings in \(\mathfrak{B}_{m + 1}\), and start-up curves pass initial and retention tests, they give, for every \(N, p\), an admissible continuous Wilson curve on \(\mathfrak{B}_N\) with coupling parameter \(\beta\) and \(v = 0\), and reset outputs along every continuous admissible input curve with couplings in \(\mathfrak{B}_{m + 1}\) on every closed interval, for all \(m < N\). Output couplings need not remain in the coarse band before shooting. Status: conditional theorems; physical inputs supplied by III.10. Lean: Return.reset, program.

Proposition III.9.6 (Wilson start-up). For compatible clip/norm pairs (\(0 < t \leqslant 1\), \(c_{\text{dir}} \leqslant 1\), \(B \geqslant 2\), \(te^{(B - 2) / c_{\text{rel}}} \geqslant 2\)), Wilson top paths, point-group-averaged regular activities \(\beta (1 - c_{\text{dir}}) R_p\) on small-field plaquettes and forced activities \(e^{- \beta \hspace{0.17em} \text{charge} (D, U)}\) on padded large-field components give \(\rho^{\text{start}}_{\beta} (U) = e^{- \beta S_W (U)}\) for every real \(\beta\) and configuration \(U\), with \(v = 0\). For every \(G, k, q\), some \(C > 0\), \(0 < \delta \leqslant 1\) give residual sup-jet bound \(Ct^5\) for every norm choice of order \(k\) and power \(q\), every torus, plaquette and site of that plaquette when \(t \leqslant \delta\). At each fixed stage with \(c_{\text{dir}} \leqslant 1\), some \(\beta^{\ast}\) ensures compatibility, small threshold and both norm allowances for every realized choice of levels and every \(N\) at that stage with \(\hat{\beta}_N \geqslant \beta^{\ast}\). The band curve is continuous; admissibility additionally requires Wilson top paths, their stage bounds and recipe tests. Given old planned data and a revised horizon with Wilson top quadratic datum, unchanged lower data and initial guesses, bounded Wilson top paths can replace the old top while preserving \(\alpha\) and planned conditions. Status: theorem. Lean: Start.evaluates.

Proposition III.9.7 (thresholds). For a fixed pre-block profile and simple \(G\), sensors \(\eta_j > 0\) precede block sizes. Fix block factors, \(0 < \theta_j \leqslant 1\), reset constants, decay forecasts and post-block costs. Assume, for each \(i \leqslant j \leqslant i + 1\), \(8 f_{ji} \leqslant q_i \ell_j^{\min}\) and \(u_{ji} (x) \to 0\), where \(f_{ji}\) is the inherited coefficient, \(\ell_j^{\min}\) the preassigned block-size floor, and \(q_i = 1 / [64 (1 + A_i)]\) for the regular reorganization coefficient \(A_i \geqslant 0\). Every threshold record \(V\) admits \(U \geqslant V\) such that every \(W \geqslant U\) ensures pricing, cubic, width, reorganization and inherited inequalities beyond thresholds; clip compatibility requires \(c_{\text{dir}} \leqslant 1\). Realized levels must share these data and satisfy \(W^{\text{entry}}_{J_{m + 1}} \leqslant \hat{\beta}_m\). Physical bounds also require \(4 q_i \leqslant c_{ji}\) and \(4 g_j \leqslant \text{rate}_{ji}\). Start-up thresholds are likewise monotone when every used clip has \(c_{\text{dir}} \leqslant 1\). Status: conditional numerical theorem. Lean: Time.beforeWait.

Proposition III.9.8 (marker sums). Fix \(M, \mu \in \mathbb{N}\), \(a \in \mathbb{R}\), \(c \geqslant 0\). On a four-torus let arbitrarily indexed polymers \(X_i\) have \(f_i \geqslant 0\) and subsets of finitely many marker names, at most \(\mu\) markers per site. Every positive-weight polymer contains one of its markers' sites. If every marker-rooted sum \(\sum_{i : v \in \text{markers} (i)} e^{(a + 1) \text{cover}_M (X_i)} f_i \leqslant c\), then every site-pinned sum \(\sum_{i : x \in X_i} e^{a \text{cover}_M (X_i)} f_i \leqslant \mu e (2 (16 M + 9))^4 c\), uniformly in volume. All nonnegative sums must be summable. Status: theorem. Lean: root_hit.

3. Strategy

Extract the isotropic quadratic coefficient of the early regular activities into \(\beta\). Subtract flat values and quadratic projections, compensate path terms, and absorb failed-mask discrepancies into the forced gas. A scalar equation identifies this exact reorganization with the continuous reset; shooting chooses the bare coupling with endpoint \(\bar{\beta}\).

4. Steps

Under the corresponding hypotheses in §1:

  1. Taylor expansion through order four gives the Wilson residual; connected large-field components give the exact gas. Start.gas_raw.

  2. A small-field stripe gives \(\mathcal{K} (V) \geqslant | \Lambda | \eta t^2\). Pricing and the activity-motion bound make \(f (x, d) = (\beta'_x + \alpha + d) \mathcal{K} (V) +\mathcal{L} (V) +\mathcal{E}_{F (x, d)} (V) - \log (H_x (1) / H_x (V))\) increasing with slope at least \(\mathcal{K} (V) / 2\), yielding a unique continuous root. paidResponse.

  3. Simplicity and lattice symmetry make the projected Hessian isotropic; exact masked splitting preserves every density value and gives \(d = \delta\). Local.exponent.

  4. Fixed-stage asymptotics make the reset inequalities eventual; prefix maxima combine their thresholds monotonically. collect_upper.

  5. Shooting restricts intervals to keep couplings in their bands and reach \(\beta_0 = \bar{\beta}\); summing drift errors gives uniform running. Shoot.trajectory.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Two-point matching requires full masked reorganization to imply exact transfer. Activity-norm continuity must survive hard configuration masks. The running estimate gives an \(o (s_m)\) remainder, without a bounded remainder or two-loop expansion.

6. Notation

\(N, p, m\): cutoff, volume, level; \(| \Lambda_m |\): site count; \(\alpha = \alpha_{N, m}\): one-loop shift; \(a\): support exponent; \(\text{cover}_M\): polymer cover size; \(J_m\): stage; \(\omega_j\): drift allowance; \(R_m\): patch radius. \(\mathcal{K}, \mathcal{L}, \mathcal{E}, \mathcal{G}\): kinetic, loop, regular and forced-gas terms; \(\mathcal{S}\): masked quadratic receipt; \(\delta\): isotropic Hessian coefficient; \(\chi_X\): small-field indicator. Admissible means prescribed paths/norms/clip, lawfulness and recipe acceptance. Curve continuity means continuous coupling and vacuum constant, fixed supports, inventories and eligibility tests, and continuity in weighted regular sup-jet/forced uniform norms; regular jets are measured on small-field supports at the prescribed order and power.
