1. What this unit proves

Throughout, \(N \geqslant 1\), \(G \subset U (N)\) is closed, \(c \geqslant 1\), \(f = cL\), \(L \geqslant 3\) is odd, \(J \geqslant 4\), \(R = (J + 3) L\), and \(K = (\kappa, \delta, \varepsilon, a) > 0\). Branch conditions mean uniqueness of the centre-normalized minimizing frame when every route holonomy in every centre axial gauge has chord \(< \delta\) (B1), uniqueness of the minimizing predictor when its minimum price is \(< \varepsilon\) (B2), and nonempty eligible axial tags for every configuration (B3).

Proposition III.1.1 (Markov kernel). Under (B1)–(B3), for every real \(\beta\), \(\rho_{\beta}\) is nonnegative, Borel, bounded and satisfies \(\int \rho_{\beta} (U, V) d \mu_c (V) = 1\) for every \(U\). For every Banach-valued \(F \in L^1 (\mu_f)\), \(\mathcal{P}_{\beta} F (V) = \int \rho_{\beta} (U, V) F (U) d \mu_f (U)\) exists for every \(V\), is integrable, depends linearly on \(F\), and preserves its integral. Positivity and integrability in \(V\) need no branch conditions. Status: theorem. Lean: isKernel.

Proposition III.1.2 (covariance). Under (B1)–(B3), for every \(\beta, U, V\), fine gauge \(k\), signed axis permutation \(s\) and coarse site \(b\),

\(\displaystyle \rho_{\beta} (k \cdot U, (k \circ \text{embed}) \cdot V) = \rho_{\beta} (U, V) = \rho_{\beta} (s_{Lb} U, s_b V), \qquad \text{embed} (x) = Lx.\)

A calendar satisfying branch conditions on every patch has block control and Markov kernels at every step. Status: theorem. Lean: covary; channel.

Proposition III.1.3 (uniform branch constants). Let \(d\) be as in III.1.5 and \(r_G > 0\) a unique-nearest-point tubular radius of \(G\). For every \(\kappa > 0\), \(0 < \delta \leqslant d / \sqrt{N}\), \(0 < \varepsilon \leqslant r_G^2 / (2 N)\) and \(a \geqslant 16\), (B1)–(B3) hold for every patch. Every configuration and axis ordering admit an eligible root. Status: theorem. Lean: Axial.viable; RG.frame_unique; RG.predictor_unique.

Proposition III.1.4 (axial gauge). For every list \(l\) of distinct axes, \(r \in \mathbb{N}\), \(o \in B_r\) and configuration \(U\) on \(\mathbb{Z}^4\), put \(S = \{y \in B_r : y_j = o_j (j \not\in l)\}\). Some \(x \in S\) gives an axial gauge \(g_y = T_U (x \to y ; l)\) with \(E_S (g \cdot U) \leqslant 4 |l| (2 r + 1) F_S (U)\). Status: theorem. Lean: Axial.existence.

Proposition III.1.5 (synchronization uniqueness). There exists \(d (G) \in (0, 1 / 4]\) such that, for all finite nonempty \(n, \Lambda\), data \(W : n \times n \times \Lambda \to G\) with \(W_{xxk} = 1\), \(W_{yx \sigma (k)} = W_{xyk}^{- 1}\) for a permutation \(\sigma\), and \(\|W_{xyk} - 1\| \leqslant d\) have this property: any two global minimizers over \(G^n\) of \(\text{avg}_{x, y, k} \text{pr} (h_x W_{xyk} h_y^{- 1})\) differ by one global left multiple. Status: theorem. Lean: Branch.align.

Proposition III.1.6 (frame comparison). There exists \(A (G) > 0\) such that, for every patch, \(K\), configuration \(W\), positive-frame-mass tag, realized frame \(h\), and gauge \(H : \mathbb{Z}^4 \to G\), some \(q \in G\) satisfies

\(\displaystyle \sum_{y \in B_R} \text{ch} (q^{- 1} h_y H_y^{- 1}) \leqslant A (2 R + 1) [E_{B_R} (H \cdot W) + a (2 R + 1) F_{B_R} (W)] .\)

No branch conditions are needed. Status: theorem. Lean: Pressure.synchronized.

Proposition III.1.7 (common gauge). Fix \(G, K\) and \(j \geqslant 4\); set \(\ell = 2 j + 15\), \(D = 16 \ell\), \(C = A \ell (D + a \ell)\) and \(R' = (j + 7) L\). For every step with \(J = j\), fine \(U\), coarse \(x\), and tag usable at \(x\), there exist \(H : \mathbb{Z}^4 \to G\) and \(p_y \in G\) for \(y \in B_2\) such that, for every such \(y\),

\(\displaystyle E_{B_{R'}} (H \cdot U^{(x)}) \leqslant DL \hspace{0.17em} \text{port} (x, U), \qquad \sum_{i \in B_R} \text{ch} (p_y^{- 1} h_i^{(x + y)} H_{i + Ly}^{- 1}) \leqslant CL^2 \text{port} (x, U) .\)

Every positive-tag-mass tag is usable everywhere. Status: theorem. Lean: Pressure.planting.

Proposition III.1.8 (coarse curvature). With III.1.7's constants and \(C_8 = 8 \max (1, \varepsilon^{- 1}) (2 C + D)\), for every step with \(J = j\), configurations \(U, V\), coarse site \(x\), tag \(t\) usable at \(x\), and plane \(p\),

\(\displaystyle \text{ch} (Q (U, t)_{(x, p)}) \leqslant C_8 L^{- 2} \text{port} (x, U), \qquad \text{ch} (V_{(x, p)}) \leqslant \text{fuel} (Q, V)_{(x, p)} + C_8 L^{- 2} \text{port} (x, U) .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: Pressure.qdc.

Proposition III.1.9 (densities and histories). Representations evaluate to an exponential of vacuum minus action and masked regular activities, times the gas. Flags mark sites touched by plaquettes of chord above threshold. Reading links and flags in \(X\) requires only links in \(X\) and corners of plaquettes meeting \(X\). The gas equals \(1\) without flags. Every lawful representation (path bounds and valid activities) has bounded Borel evaluation. Selected histories start at the bare Wilson density and obey exact pushes; every selected history satisfies \(\int \text{eval}_{N, p, m} d \mu = Z_{N, p} (\beta_{N, p, N})\) for all \(m \leqslant N\). Status: definitions, with four consequences proved; quantitative construction belongs to III.3–III.10. Lean: UsesFinal.allReads; gas_one; Rep.envelope; History.partition_eq.

Proposition III.1.10 (tube identity). For every tube, path cutoff, real thresholds and \(U, Q, V\), the six works satisfy

\(\displaystyle W_F + W_I + W_C + W_N + W_L + W_D = C_f + P - C_c,\)

where \(C_f, C_c\) are truncated crown sums and \(P = \sum_b \kappa \hspace{0.17em} \text{ch} (V_b Q_b^{- 1})^2 / 2\). Status: theorem. Lean: Tube.locates.

Proposition III.1.11 (energy per seed). Estimator data require: for every sufficiently large \(L\) and then sufficiently large \(W\), some \(\kappa_0 > 0\) gives \(\kappa_0 t^2 |A| \leqslant \text{Work} (A)\) at all sufficiently large reference couplings, uniformly over compatible tubes, forests and plots with trivial trunks and \(c \geqslant 100 (1 + \varrho)\). For a directed history satisfying ringing, each \(J_0 \leqslant i \leqslant j \leqslant i + 1\) admits \(\mu > 0\) such that \(\mu t_{m + 1}^2 |A| \leqslant \text{Work} (A)\) for all \(N, m < N, p\) with \((J_{m + 1}, J_m) = (j, i)\) and every plot at \((\hat{\beta}_m, F (j, i))\) with trivial trunks. Status: requirement, proved in III.10; transfer theorem here. Lean: Stores.Plot; Teller.Grants; Testing.notation_ticket.

Proposition III.1.12 (kinetic specification). Fix levels, a quadratic horizon, Haar-chart density and positive stage constants. Kinetic data assign shifts \(\alpha\), path data and free-link maps \(\psi\). Stages and factors agree with the fixed choices; rows attach to the horizon and satisfy stage bounds. At level \(N\), the unmasked, unclipped action is analytic, matches Wilson derivatives through order four, and has zero loop coefficients. For every \(m < N\), volume, forest and tag, let \(\mathcal{I}_t\) be the fine unmasked, unclipped unit-coupling action without loops plus predictor penalty, on fields with trunks \(1\) and free links \(\exp (b_e) (\iota \exp u)_e\), where \(\iota\) copies coarse links across block boundaries. Requirements, for every free field \(v\), are

\(\displaystyle D_b^2 \mathcal{I}_t (0, 0) [v, v] \geqslant \eta \sum_{e \text{free}} |v_e |^2, \qquad D_b \mathcal{I}_t (u, \psi (u)) = 0\)

near zero: \(\psi (0) = 0\), both maps are analytic, \(D \psi (0)\) is the prescribed background adjusted to tree gauge minus the linearized block copy, and the analytic coarse action matches \(\mathcal{I}_t (u, \psi (u))\) through order four. Writing \(H_t (u) = D_b^2 \mathcal{I}_t (u, \psi (u))\) in physical coordinates,

\(\displaystyle T_t =\mathcal{L}_f^{\circ} (U_F (u, \psi (u))) + \tfrac{1}{2} \log \det H_t - \log \prod_{e \text{free}} J (\psi (u)_e), \qquad D = \alpha \mathcal{Q}_c^{\circ} (\exp u) +\mathcal{L}_c^{\circ} (\exp u) .\)

\(\mathcal{L}^{\circ}, \mathcal{Q}^{\circ}\) denote loop and quadratic actions; \(J\) is the Haar-chart density. Both \(T_t, D\) are analytic, \(\det H_t > 0\) near zero, and

\(\displaystyle |D^2 T_t (0) [a, b] - D^2 D (0) [a, b] | \leqslant | \Lambda_c | \Theta \Lambda e^{- \gamma c}\)

for real plane waves with direction-colour amplitudes bounded by \(1\); \(\Lambda\) is the fine loop bound. Also \(| \alpha + \kappa_{\text{slope}} \log L_m | + 1 \leqslant \text{drift}_{m + 1}\). Directed histories require paid levels, a sufficient horizon, matching recipe and local Haar-chart formula. Moves require exact push, continuation, retention, lawful coarse data, forced-activity bound \(\exp [- g_j \text{monitor}_{m + 1}^2]\), and \(| \beta_m - \beta_{m + 1} - \alpha | \leqslant 1\). Status: definitions; conditional constructors for simple nonabelian \(\mathfrak{g}\) belong to III.2. Lean: Kin.Planned; Directed.Move.

Proposition III.1.13 (tree gauge). For odd \(L\), block forests exist. Inverse tree transport gives a gauge equal to \(1\) at roots, depending only on trunks, and trivializing them. Every real-Banach-valued integrable \(F\) invariant under gauges trivial at roots satisfies \(\int Fd \mu_f = \int F (\text{drape} \hspace{0.17em} s) d \mu_{\text{free}} (s)\). For a kernel satisfying III.1.2(a) and gauge-invariant integrable \(F\), the same reduction applies to \(\mathcal{P}_{\beta} F (V)\). Status: theorem. Lean: Forest.pack; Forest.pushing.

Proposition III.1.14 (linearization). Under (B1)–(B3), for every tag \(t\) and coarse link \(b\), \(v \mapsto Q_b ((\exp v_e)_e, t)\) on fine \(\mathfrak{g}\)-valued \(1\)-forms equals \(1\) at \(v = 0\) and is real analytic nearby, using minimizing branches. Its tag-independent derivative is \(\mathcal{Q}v =\mathcal{M}v + d_0 \mathcal{F}v\), and \(\mathcal{Q}d_0 \varphi = d_0 (\varphi \circ \text{embed})\) for every fine \(0\)-form. Frames have derivative \(h_y^{(x) \prime} = \tau_x (y) - \tau_x (0)\). The kernel is analytic near \((1, 1)\) for fixed \(\beta\); neighbourhoods may depend on the step. Status: theorem. Lean: Push.derivative; Push.prices.

3. Strategy

Minimize the average route price to fix centre-normalized frames, transport across blocks, and average the connectors. The predictor \(Q\) is the nearest group point to their weighted matrix mean on its minimizing branch. The kernel averages \(\prod_b Z_{\beta}^{- 1} e^{- \beta \kappa \text{pr} (V_b Q_b^{- 1})}\) over tags, with \(Z_{\beta} = \int_G e^{- \beta \kappa \text{pr} (g)} dg\). Unique minimizers handle small fields; symmetric sampling of curvature-controlled axial frames and connectors handles the remaining configurations. A common macro-box gauge compares neighbouring frames.

4. Steps

  1. Uniform cutoffs. Normalize minimizers by nearest centroid points; tangential cancellation makes feedback contract. Slab induction gives eligible axial roots. align; existence.

  2. Kernel. Tag probabilities and Haar noise each have mass one; uniqueness and tag permutations give covariance. density_total; channel.

  3. Curvature. Synchronization costs \(L^2\), while \(w \leqslant L^{- 4}\); four padded links give the plaquette bound. indicated; qdc.

  4. Local analysis. The implicit function theorem gives derivatives; Haar invariance removes trunks. Adjointness cancels cross terms and squares in the tube identity. scheme; pack; Tube.locates.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Uniqueness must remain uniform in patch size. Both branches must preserve area scaling. Global regularity is Borel; analyticity is local.

6. Notation

\(B_r = \{- r, \ldots, r\}^4\); \(\text{ch} (u) = \|u - 1\| / \sqrt{N}\), \(\text{pr} (u) = 1 - \text{Re tr} (u) / N\); \(\| \cdot \|\) is Frobenius, \(|v| = \|v\| / \sqrt{N}\). \(\mu\) denotes product probability Haar measure; \(E_X, F_X\) sum link and plaquette chords. \(U^{(x)}\) is the root-centred periodic lift; \(\text{port} = F_{B_{R'}} (U^{(x)})\). Usable means positive frame masses on \(x + B_2\); fuel sums four link discrepancies with multiplicity. \(w\) is the \(J\)-fold block-uniform convolution. \(\tau\) averages negative route integrals, \(\mathcal{M}\) averages straight length-\(L\) integrals, \(\mathcal{F}v (x) = \tau_x (0) - \sum_y w_y \tau_x (y)\), and \(d_0\) is forward difference. Here \(\tau_x (y) = - \text{avg}_{z \in B_R, o \in S_4} \int_{Lx + y \to Lx + z ; o} v\).

\(\text{monitor}_m = e^{(\log \log \hat{\beta}_m)^2}\); \(t_m = \text{temp} (\hat{\beta}_m)\), \(\text{temp} (z) = z^{- 1 / 2} e^{(\log \log z)^2}\); \(t = \text{temp} (\hat{\beta} + \kappa_{\text{slope}} \log L)\), \(\varrho = \lceil (\log \hat{\beta})^2 \rceil\), \(\kappa_{\text{slope}} = 11 C_A / (24 \pi^2)\).

A store \(A\) comprises fine plaquettes, coarse links and coarse sites; \(|A|\) sums their cardinalities. \(\text{Work} (A)\) sums local works whose fine bases’ parents or coarse bases lie within distance \(2 \varrho\) of its locations, with cutoff \(\lfloor \varrho / 10^5 \rfloor\). \(F (j, i) > 0\) is the assigned wire parameter.

7. What remains unexplained

Energy lower bounds and kinetic construction belong to later units. The uses of \(B_2\) rather than \(B_1\), and \(J \geqslant 4\) rather than \(J \geqslant 1\), remain unspecified here.
