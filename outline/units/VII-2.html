1. What this unit proves

Unless stated otherwise, fix a final record over a Wilson family \(C\), extraction order \(f\) and limit table \(S\) (VIII.1), with selected phase \(\xi_{\ast}\) and Ward-normalized stress tensor \(T\) (VI.1). The compact matrix group \(G\) has simple nonabelian Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}\). Set

\(\displaystyle \kappa_G = \frac{11 C_A}{24 \pi^2}, \qquad d_2 = - \frac{\kappa_G}{4}, \qquad A_G = \frac{3 \dim \mathfrak{g}}{\pi^4} .\)

Here \(\mathcal{O}= \tfrac{1}{4} \sum_{a, \mu, \nu} : F^a_{\mu \nu} F^a_{\mu \nu} :\) belongs to the free Maxwell table \(\textsf{F}\) with \(\dim \mathfrak{g}\) species and propagator \(1 / (4 \pi^2 |x|^2)\).

Proposition VII.2.1 (centered trace). Define \(\Theta = \sum_{\mu} T_{\mu \mu} - \langle \sum_{\mu} T_{\mu \mu} \rangle_{\xi_{\ast}} \boldsymbol{1} \in F_4\). It has zero expectation, is real, \(O (4)\)-invariant and fixed by the time-reflection adjoint; these conclusions also hold when centering in any phase table respecting \(S\). Its reconstructed label \(\iota (\Theta)\) is fixed by conjugation \(J\) and every Lorentz transformation. For every Schwartz test \(h\), its vacuum expectation vanishes and, on the common invariant field domain,

\(\displaystyle \Phi_{\Theta} (h) = \Phi_{\sum_{\mu} T_{\mu \mu}} (h) - \left\langle \sum_{\mu} T_{\mu \mu} \right\rangle_{\xi_{\ast}} \left( \int h \right) \text{id} .\)

If \(r^8 \log^2 (1 / r) G_{\Theta} (r) \to A > 0\), then \(\iota (\Theta) \ne 0\). Status: theorem. Lean: pinned_scalar, center_field, nonzero_strength.

Proposition VII.2.2 (spectral representation). There is a unique positive Lorentz-invariant measure \(\mu_{\Theta}\) supported in \(\bar{V}_+\) such that:

  • Some \(C_{\mu} \geqslant 0\), \(k \in \mathbb{N}_0\) satisfy \(\mu_{\Theta} \{p_0 \leqslant R\} \leqslant C_{\mu} R^k\) for every \(R \geqslant 1\).

  • For every \(s > 0\), \(e^{- sp_0} \mu_{\Theta}\) is finite and \(G_{\Theta} (s) = \int e^{- sp_0} d \mu_{\Theta}\).

  • For every Schwartz \(\varphi\), the translation spectral measure of \(\Phi_{\Theta} (\varphi) \Omega\) is \(| \hat{\varphi} |^2 \mu_{\Theta}\); for every Schwartz \(\phi\) on \((\mathbb{R}^4)^2\), \(W_2 (\Theta, \Theta) (\phi) = \int \hat{\phi}^{\text{wave}} d \mu_{\Theta}\).

Thus \(G_{\Theta}\) is real, nonnegative and nonincreasing on \((0, \infty)\). Status: theorem. Lean: traceSpectrum_exists.

Proposition VII.2.3 (one-loop certificates). Let \(C\) issue from run data satisfying the RG construction hypotheses in §2, including a chosen bounded block-spin run. For every filter \(\mathcal{L}\) on \(\mathbb{N}^2\) along which both spacing and volume indices \(N, p \to \infty\), there are level-5 lattice work items \(t_{k, N, p}\) satisfying the lattice certificate for all large \(k\), \(\mathcal{L}\)-eventually in \((N, p)\). In particular, their Ward darts satisfy, for every color \(q\),

\(\displaystyle | \text{midPay}_q (J) + 12 \kappa_G | \leqslant \hat{\beta}_k^{- 100} .\)

Status: theorem. Lean: agenda, certificate WorthB.

Proposition VII.2.4 (field anomaly; gallery property). Assume II.1 Wilson consistency, the quantitative RG and VII.6 scaling hypotheses in §2, auxiliary depths \(n_k \geqslant 2\) eventually, and degree bounds \(z_d\) covering every generator budget of degree at most \(d\) and satisfying the degree-\(d\) scaling estimates. Let \(\mathcal{L}\) be an ultrafilter finer than the fuse filter of \(f\), with work items satisfying VII.2.3's certificate.

For every limit table \(S\) along \(f\) with ordered Wilson convergence, Euclidean hierarchy, point limits, cell hypothesis and corrected rank estimates, and every field algebra on \(S\) satisfying derivative, rotation, conservation and Ward laws, there exist coupling and label-transfer limits \(Z\) along \(\mathcal{L}\) and a free Maxwell hierarchy \(\textsf{a}\), with \(Z. \beta (k) = \lim_{\mathcal{L}} \beta_{N, p} (k)\) and \(\hat{\beta}_k / 2 \leqslant Z. \beta (k) \leqslant 2 \hat{\beta}_k\) for every level \(k\).

(i) For every scaling sequence \((r_i, U_i)\) approaching \(\textsf{a}\) with VII.6 RG lifts and pointwise lattice limits, base levels \(k_i \to \infty\) and selected readout levels \(\ell_i\), put \(w_i = Z. \beta (\ell_i)\). Here \(r_i \downarrow 0\); the complex-linear identifications \(U_i : \textsf{F}_d \to F_d\) eventually preserve the unit, reality and signed coordinate actions degreewise, with rescaled correlations converging to \(\textsf{a}\). Then

\(\displaystyle w_i \to \infty, \qquad \frac{\log (1 / r_i)}{w_i} \to \kappa_G^{- 1}, \qquad r_i^4 U_i^{- 1} \mathring{T}_{\mu \nu} \to T^{\text{free}}_{\mu \nu} .\)

For every phase hierarchy \(T'\) respecting \(S\), real sequences \(e_i, d_i\) satisfy, eventually,

\(\displaystyle r_i^4 U_i^{- 1} \Theta_{T'} = e_i \boldsymbol{1}+ 4 d_i \mathcal{O}, \qquad w_i e_i \to 0, \quad w_i d_i \to d_2 .\)

Thus \(w_i r_i^4 U_i^{- 1} \Theta_{T'} \to - \kappa_G \mathcal{O}\).

(ii) Every sequence \(s_j > 0\) tending to zero has a subsequence that is the scale sequence of a scaling sequence satisfying (i). Status: theorem; hypotheses discharged in III.2 for the constructed family, recorded in VIII.1. Lean: gang, Store_l.

Proposition VII.2.5 (short-distance and spectral laws). If the gallery property holds for \((C, f)\), then

\(\displaystyle \lim_{r \downarrow 0} r^8 \log^2 (1 / r) G_{\Theta} (r) = A_G > 0\)

in \(\mathbb{C}\). For every continuous compactly supported or Schwartz \(\varphi\), as \(\rho \to \infty\),

\(\displaystyle \rho^{- 8} \log^2 \rho \int \varphi (p / \rho) d \mu_{\Theta} (p) \to \frac{A_G}{1536 \pi} \int_{V_+} (p^2)^2 \varphi (p) d^4 p.\)

The limiting measure has Laplace transform \(A_G s^{- 8}\). For every Schwartz \(\phi\) on \((\mathbb{R}^4)^2\), setting \(\phi_{\rho} (x, y) = \rho^8 \phi (\rho x, \rho y)\), the same cone integral with \(\varphi = \hat{\phi}^{\text{wave}}\) is the limit of \(\rho^{- 8} \log^2 \rho \hspace{0.17em} W_2 (\Theta, \Theta) (\phi_{\rho})\). Status: theorem under the gallery property. Lean: outside, joint_limit.

Proposition VII.2.6 (uniqueness of stress). Let \(\mathcal{K} (S)\) consist of real, symmetric, \(S\)-centered degree-4 tensors covariant under signed coordinate permutations; let \(\mathcal{V} (S)\) consist of real degree-5 vectors with that covariance. Fix a derivative plan satisfying derivative laws and the orientation condition. The free Maxwell divergence \(\mathcal{K} (\textsf{F}) \to \mathcal{V} (\textsf{F})\) is onto with one-dimensional kernel. In VII.2.4(i)'s setting, \(\dim \mathcal{K} (S) = 3\), \(\dim \mathcal{V} (S) = 2\), and \(\dim \ker \text{div} = 1\). Status: theorem. Lean: free_rank, ranking.

Proposition VII.2.7 (no finite Wick profile). Suppose \(r^8 \log^2 (1 / r) G_{\Theta} (r) \to A > 0\). For every finite \(W (r) = \sum_{\alpha} c_{\alpha} \prod_{\beta} K_{m_{\alpha \beta}, q_{\alpha \beta}, h_{\alpha \beta}} (r)\), with complex coefficients and

\(\displaystyle K_{m, q, h} (r) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \frac{E_m (p)^q h (p)}{2 E_m (p)} e^{- rE_m (p)} dp, \qquad E_m (p) = \sqrt{m^2 + |p|^2},\)

where \(m \geqslant 0\), \(q \in \mathbb{N}_0\) and continuous \(h : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\) is positively homogeneous of nonnegative integer degree, every \(\delta > 0\) admits \(r \in (0, \delta)\) with \(G_{\Theta} (r) \ne W (r)\). No such collection reproduces the Wightman function by its mass-shell expression on all Schwartz product tests \(\varphi \otimes \psi\) with compactly supported wave transform. This also excludes Wick contractions of two finite polynomial fields from any free-mode palette. Status: theorem. Lean: polynomial_excludes.

Proposition VII.2.8 (free-photon Fock exclusion). Under VII.2.7's positive law, for every \(m \in \mathbb{N}_0\), let \(\mathcal{F}_m\) be the Fock space of \(m\) free-photon species. For every complex-linear isometry \(U : \mathcal{H} \to \mathcal{F}_m\) preserving the vacuum, taking values in physical transverse waves and intertwining translations with \(e^{ib \cdot p_{\text{tot}}}\), there exist a compactly supported smooth scalar test \(\varphi\) and compactly supported smooth real three-vector tests \(h_c\), \(1 \leqslant c \leqslant m\), each causally separated from \(\varphi\), such that

\(\displaystyle \langle W (- h) \Omega_{\mathcal{F}}, U \Phi_{\Theta} (\varphi) \Omega \rangle \ne \langle U \Phi_{\Theta} (\bar{\varphi}) \Omega, W (h) \Omega_{\mathcal{F}} \rangle .\)

Equality for all such tests would follow from relative locality with the free Weyl operators. The conclusion persists after applying any bounded self-adjoint operator fixing \(U\mathcal{H}\) to both coherent vectors, or averaging over any probability family of inner-product-preserving maps on physical waves fixing \(U\mathcal{H}\). In the averaged version, for every \(r > 0\), tests can be supported in open \(Q, W\) with \(\bar{Q} \subset B (0, r)\) and \(\bar{Q} - \bar{W}\) spacelike, with \(\varphi\) supported in \(Q\) and every component of \(h\) in \(W\). Status: theorem. Lean: excludes, regions_excludes.

Proposition VII.2.9 (position-space coefficient). For every simple \(G\), kernel parameter \(b > 0\) and extraction order \(p\), there exist a consistent Wilson family \(C\) with parameter \(b\) and refinement \(f\) of \(p\), admitting final records. For every such final record, there exist a free hierarchy, coupling limits \(Z\) and an ultrafilter admitting degree-4 position-space frames. For every admissible pair \(P (s), P' (s) : \textsf{F}_4 \to F_4\) with window cutoff \(\chi\), real \(\kappa, d, e\) satisfy, for sufficiently small \(s > 0\),

\(\displaystyle P' (s)^{- 1} (s^4 T_{\mu \nu}) = \kappa (s) T^{\text{free}}_{\mu \nu} + d (s) \delta_{\mu \nu} \mathcal{O}+ e (s) \delta_{\mu \nu} \boldsymbol{1}, \qquad \kappa (s) \to 1.\)

Admissibility requires position-space Gram normalization, branch/angular alignment and convergence of prescribed finite off-diagonal jets for \(P\); \(P'\) positively rescales its centered scalar sector and complement, fixes the unit, and normalizes two shell tests to free values. For every eventually positive \(g\) with \(\log (1 / s) g (s)^2 \to \kappa_G^{- 1}\), \(d (s) / g (s)^2 \to d_2\). The test-defined coupling satisfies \(g_X / g_W \to 1\) and \(d - d_2 g_X^2 = O (g_X^4)\), where \(g_W\) is the Wilson coupling from \(Z\). Eventually \(s^4 \Theta = 4 d (s) \text{center}_{\xi_{\ast}} (P' (s)\mathcal{O})\) exactly. Status: theorem for the supplementary construction's family and refinement. Lean: meterQ, FieldComparison.

3. Strategy

The classical stress tensor is traceless; the anomaly comes from the running coupling. In Balaban's RG framework, transport a lattice Ward identity through block-spin steps and extract its coefficient from the background-field one-loop logarithmic pole. The canonical tensor has a divergence diagnostic tending to \(- \kappa_G\). Conservation forces the pure-trace improvement coefficient to \(d_2\); rank-one uniqueness and Ward normalization identify the conserved candidate with \(T\).

Free-field convergence then gives the two-point law. The factor \((\log (1 / r_i) / w_i)^2 \to \kappa_G^{- 2}\) cancels the squared anomaly coefficient, leaving \(A_G\). Monotonicity removes lattice rounding; subsequences give the full limit. Laplace convergence gives the spectral law. Finite Wick expansions and relatively local photon Fock realizations each force incompatible short-distance growth.

4. Steps

  1. A sound Ward dart represents the action variation along a displacement field through fourth order. Under the criticality, branch, bound, positive price-gap and classical-matching hypotheses, block-spin transport preserves soundness; repeated transport contracts precision errors by \(1 / 2\) per step. Soundness.

  2. Under uniform covariance, resolvent and action bounds, the one-loop action regulated at infrared mass \(\mu > 0\) has logarithmic coefficient \(- 12 \kappa_G\). A mean-value argument in \(\log \mu\), followed by regulated Ward comparison, gives the payoff limit. awake.

  3. Under VII.2.4's hypotheses, lattice-to-continuum transfer preserves the divergence diagnostic. Solving the two-dimensional improvement equation forces its scalar coefficient to \(d_2\). quill.

  4. Rank-one conservation identifies the candidate up to a multiple; comparison of Ward fluxes makes that multiple tend to one. year_spec.

  5. Free Wick normalization gives \(A_G\) along scaling sequences; monotonicity removes lattice rounding and the subsequence principle gives the full limit. outside.

  6. Lorentz invariance converts the law to cone Laplace convergence; uniform energy bounds extend compact-test convergence to Schwartz tests. joint_limit.

  7. Under the positive trace law, finite Wick power–log expansions cannot produce an inverse logarithm squared. Relative locality in photon Fock space forces point-supported Cauchy data and then momentum-doubling eigencomponents, yielding energy growth at most \(R^7\), incompatible with the spectral law. no_powerlog, saturate.

  8. For the supplementary position-space frames, shell normalization gives \(d = d_2 g_X^2 \kappa\) and \(\kappa - 1 = - dQ / B_0\), where \(B_0 \ne 0\) and \(Q\) has a finite limit. Hence \(\kappa - 1 = O (g_X^2)\) and \(d - d_2 g_X^2 = O (g_X^4)\). meterQ.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The Ward transport, exact one-loop coefficient and remainders \(o (\hat{\beta}^{- p})\) for every \(p \in \mathbb{N}_0\) carry the anomaly proof. Slower coefficient errors must also defeat growing improvement coefficients. The amplitude uses equality of the independently entering schedule and one-loop slopes. The Wick exclusion concerns finite profiles; continuous-mass generalized free fields are outside its scope. Fock exclusion additionally needs point-supported Cauchy data to force dilation identities.
