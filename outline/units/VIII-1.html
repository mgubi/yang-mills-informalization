1. What this unit proves

A gauge group \((m, G, \mathfrak{g})\) has \(m \geqslant 1\), \(G \subset U (m)\) closed, and \(\mathfrak{g}= \{X : \exp (tX) \in G \text{ for all } t \in \mathbb{R}\}\). Simplicity includes nonabelianness; Lie-algebra dimensions are real. Clause labels refer to statement.md.

Proposition VIII.1.1 (public theorem). For every gauge group with simple \(\mathfrak{g}\), there exists a Wilson family \(\mathcal{W}\) satisfying (Reg1)–(Reg4). For every strictly increasing \(p : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\), there exist a strictly increasing \(q\) and a hierarchy satisfying (P1)–(P4) along \(p \circ q\):

  • For every \(n\), token list \(v \in \mathcal{T}^n\) and \(f \in C_c^{\infty} (\text{Conf}_n)\), admissibility holds eventually along \(p \circ q\). At every admissible volume \(p'\), \(C_{N, p'} (v ; f) \to R_{p'}\) along all \(N\), and \(R_{p (q (k))} \to \int fS^{\text{mix}} (\pi (v) ; \cdot)\).

  • The selected theory satisfies (W0)–(W3), has faithful fields, and satisfies (R1)–(R15). The mixed correlations are averages of continuous phase correlations against a full-support probability measure \(\nu\); the selected continuation is holomorphic in complex times at fixed real spatial positions, with selected Wightman boundary values on product Schwartz tests.

  • (O1)–(O2) use common coefficients uniformly over all phases and the mixed state.

  • \(\Theta\) is centered and \(r^8 (\log 1 / r)^2 G_{\Theta} (r) \to A_{\mathfrak{g}} = 3 \dim_{\mathbb{R}} \mathfrak{g}/ \pi^4 > 0\) as \(r \downarrow 0\). Every finite Wick profile differs from \(G_{\Theta}\) somewhere in every \((0, \delta)\), \(\delta > 0\).

Status: theorem. Lean: YangMills.jointExistence.

Proposition VIII.1.2 (detailed construction). For every such group and \(b > 0\), there exists an internal family \(\mathcal{C}\), depending only on \((G, b)\), satisfying (D3), penalty \(b\) (D16), and Wilson regularity. For every extraction order \(\sigma\), there exist \(\tau, S\), a final object \(\mathfrak{F}\) on \((\mathcal{C}, \sigma \circ \tau, S)\), every-phase reconstruction \(\mathfrak{L}\), reader \(Q\), contact devices \(\textsf{M}\) and scaling data \(\Sigma = (r_k, \xi_k, U_k)\) with \(r_k \downarrow 0\), \(\xi_k > 0\), satisfying (D1)–(D15) and (Core). These include (P1)–(P4) along \((\sigma \circ \tau)^{\text{v}}\), distribution extensions, full-mesh ordered and positional limits, exact trajectory witnesses, every-phase Wightman axioms and barycentre identity, separated and nested OPE, trace and spectral laws, contact resolution with real bases and thresholds, Maxwell comparison and degree-four normalization. (Core) also includes integrated OPE remainder bounds for integrable complex densities against positive measures with bounded \(L^1\) mass.

Status: theorem. Lean: YangMills.constructed.

Proposition VIII.1.3 (chosen run). For every such group and \(b > 0\), the chosen funded run \(\textsf{R}_{G, b}\) has penalty \(b\). For every \(\sigma\), its family admits \(\tau, S, \mathfrak{F}, \mathfrak{L}, Q, \textsf{M}, \Sigma\) on \(\sigma \circ \tau\) satisfying VIII.1.2's full-mesh ordered limit, positional convergence, (Core), contact conclusions, uniform nested OPE over admissible finite trees, and (D14)–(D15).

Status: theorem. Lean: continuum_with_position_tests.

Proposition VIII.1.4 (funded runs). Let \(\mathfrak{g}\) be simple, \(\textsf{R}\) a run with campaign \(T\) and pillars \(W\), and \(\Delta\) local step estimates. If Funded (Fd) holds, then (Goal) holds: funding, (UV\(_{\infty}\)), logarithmic OPE, dense sharp-Gram coverage, and the trace result for every final object on every \((\mathcal{C}_{\textsf{R}}, \sigma, S)\). For every \(\sigma\), one refinement carries ordered coupling limits \(B_m\) at every level, \(S, \mathfrak{F}, \Sigma, \mathfrak{L}, Q\), full-mesh ordered and positional convergence, (D14), every-phase reconstruction, trace results, OPE facilities, foundation and uniform nested OPE. All-shapes OPE holds for every coherent prescription; every ultrafilter finer than the refined extraction filter has a compatible sharp-Gram allocation.

Status: theorem. Lean: R2IGoals.fromFunded.

Proposition VIII.1.5 (ultraviolet endpoint). For simple \(\mathfrak{g}\) and a run with campaign \(T\) and pillars \(W\), assume (Fd)'s stationary-point/amplification condition, delay condition and weight-and-tableau data \((\vartheta, v)\). Then (UV\(_{\infty}\)) holds: (D3), weights, refinement to final objects with full-mesh ordered limits, trajectories competing in and issued by the fixed tuning recursion, successful tuning to level-zero target \(\bar{\beta} + ws\) for every \((p, N, s)\) with \(s \in [- 1, 1]\) and fixed \(w > 0\), uniqueness of bare couplings within that recursion, analytic interpolation and coupling/activity convergence as \(N \to \infty\) at every fixed \((m, p)\) whenever level-zero targets converge. Complex-parameter convergence, with all parameter derivatives, is uniform on compact subsets of the unit disc in the fixed weighted activity norm. For every fixed \((m, p)\) there is \(c_{m, p} > 0\) with \(e^{- \text{scalar}_{N, p, m}} Z_{N, p} \geqslant c_{m, p}\) for \(N \geqslant m\); scalar density tends to \(- \infty\). Volume-uniform bounds and (D5) hold. For every fixed \(p\), a successful parameter family with targets \(\bar{\beta} + \lambda ws\), some \(\lambda > 0\), has the chosen family as central member and the same step relations and insertion normalization. For every token list, compact \(K \subset \text{NoHit}_p\) and finite derivative order \(h\), there are a neighbourhood \(\Omega\) of \(K\) and a smaller positive parameter width independent of \(N\), giving a limiting correlation holomorphic in the parameter and \(C^h\) in position, with convergence of coordinate differences of order at most \(h\), their interpolants and admissible smearings, uniformly on compact parameter/position sets with all parameter derivatives.

Status: theorem. Lean: FullVolume.Final.jewelOf17.

Proposition VIII.1.6 (full spacing sequence). Under VIII.1.5's hypotheses, for every \(p, n\) and \(v \in \mathcal{T}^n\) there is smooth \(f_{p, v}\) on \(\text{NoHit}_p\) with \(C_{N, p} (v ; t) \to \int tf_{p, v}\) for every \(p\)-admissible \(t\). Every fixed finite list of normalized forward, backward or centered coordinate differences with fixed integer widths converges, after interpolation, locally uniformly to the corresponding derivative; this also holds along approaching lattice configurations and arbitrary cutoff indices tending to infinity. Smeared differences converge to the integrable derivative pairing. Thresholds may depend on volume, tokens, compacts, tests and stencils. If a final object exists on \((\mathcal{C}, \sigma, S)\) and these positional limits hold, then \(\text{Ord}_{(\text{id}, \sigma^{\text{v}})} (\mathcal{C}, S)\) holds.

Status: theorems. Lean: Campaign.realUV, FullVolume.Final.from_spec.

Under (D3) and simplicity, regularity holds with \(b_{\ast} = 2 b_0\). Every-phase reconstruction implies (R1)–(R15); separated OPE implies (O1)–(O2); under simplicity, the seven headline trace clauses imply (T1)–(T4). Full contact resolution implies (D2).

2. What it uses

  • I.1, II.1–II.2: gauge groups, families and running law; III.2, III.12–III.15: funded runs and ultraviolet estimates (Independent.constructionRun); IV.1: continuum seeds.

  • V.1–V.3: phases and reconstruction; VI.1–VI.2: field algebra and stress tensor; VII.1–VII.5: Maxwell comparison, trace, OPE, contacts and asymptotic freedom (EveryPhase.all_characters, Solstice.provided).

3. Strategy

Fix the run before extraction. Refine first for coupling limits, then for the continuum table and final object; attach all later structures on that refinement. Fixed-volume convergence and uniqueness of limits remove the mesh subsequence. Public translation then gives the stated hierarchy.

4. Steps

  1. Choose the funded run for \((G, b)\) using the run-selection theorem with the fixed dynamics, local estimates and component prescription. constructionRun.

  2. Recover smooth full-mesh limits from dense-grid Cauchy convergence, equicontinuity and discrete Taylor estimates; smear by dominated convergence. realUV.

  3. Extract coupling limits by countable-product compactness, then a final object on a further refinement. Running.extraction, Pert.withFinal.

  4. Attach reconstruction, trace, OPE and contacts; subsequence stability retains earlier limits. fromFunded, contacts_on.

  5. For Wick exclusion, realize compactly supported relative-momentum tests as Fourier products. Dominated convergence gives equality of Laplace transforms under a hypothetical Wick identity, contradicting the trace asymptotic. polynomial_excludes

  6. Translate the detailed theorem and set \(b = 1\), \(\sigma = (\text{id}, p)\), \(q = \tau^{\text{v}}\). constructed, jointExistence.

5. Where the difficulty lies

Every conclusion must concern the same previously chosen family. The Wilson limit is mixed-state; the trace test is selected-phase. The polynomial low-energy and heat bounds do not establish a mass gap. Volume-subsequence independence and agreement between penalties are not asserted.

6. Notation

\(\sigma = (\sigma^{\text{m}}, \sigma^{\text{v}})\): increasing mesh/volume maps; refinement is componentwise composition. \(\text{Ord}\): mesh-then-volume smeared limit. \(\mathcal{C}\): internal family; \(\mathfrak{F}\): trajectory witnesses, continuum seed, field algebra, phase selection and realizing continuations; \(Q\): OPE reader; \(S\): correlation table; \(\Theta\): centered stress trace. \(\text{NoHit}_p\): configurations distinct modulo \(L_p \mathbb{Z}^4\). \(\mathcal{F}_+\): unnormalized positive-sign Fourier transform. \(b_0 = 11 C_{\mathfrak{g}} / (48 \pi^2)\).

Exact funding inequalities and renormalization comparison estimates remain in III.12–III.15; every-phase analytic arguments in V.3; normalized trace-scheme conditions in VII.3.
