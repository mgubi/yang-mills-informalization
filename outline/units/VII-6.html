1. What this unit proves

Setting. Let \(G\) be a compact matrix group with simple Lie algebra, \(C\) a Wilson family with its construction record, and \(F\) an extraction order with ordered-tail filter \(\Phi_F\) on cutoff pairs \((N, p)\). Let \(S\) be its limit table, with ordered smeared and generic point limits and Euclidean hierarchy \(H\); the free Maxwell reference has hierarchy \(H_0\). Write \(\mathcal{F}^S_d = \text{Label}_d / \text{Null}_d (S)\), projection \(\pi_d\) and unit \(\boldsymbol{1}_d\), and similarly \(\mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_d\). Fix the RG-history binding, Haar budget and chart ratio of III.11. Its one-step cost assumption is (S), and \(c_d = 2 (w_d + 1)\), where \(w_d\) is the degree-\(d\) label-frame polynomial degree plus \(4\).

A scaling consists of positive radii \(r_k\) strictly decreasing to zero, positive constants \(\xi_k\), and complex-linear isomorphisms \(U_k (d) : \mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_d \to \mathcal{F}^S_d\). It is approaching when, for every arity and free fields \(v_i \in \mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_{d_i}\), the smooth kernels

\(\displaystyle K_k (x) = \langle U_k v_1 (r_k x_1) \cdots U_k v_n (r_k x_n) \rangle^S - \langle v_1 (x_1) \cdots v_n (x_n) \rangle^{\text{free}}\)

satisfy: for every compact \(K \subset \text{Conf}_n\) and \(M, \varepsilon > 0\), eventually \(| \int tK_k | \leqslant \varepsilon\) for every smooth \(t\) supported in \(K\) with \(\|t\|_{\infty} \leqslant M\).

Proposition VII.6.1 (prescribed null sequences). Assume (S), real \(z_d \geqslant c_i\) for every \(i \leqslant d\), the uniform-band property \(\text{Fire}_d (z_d)\) for every \(d\), and \(\dim \mathcal{F}^S_d \leqslant \dim \mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_d\) for every \(d\). Let \(\mathcal{L}\) be an ultrafilter on \(\mathbb{N}^2\) finer than \(\Phi_F\). For every positive null sequence \(s_i\), there exist strictly increasing \(f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) and an approaching scaling with \(r_k = s_{f (k)}\) and \(\xi_k = 1\). For each \(d\), eventually \(U_k (d)\) is real, covariant under signed coordinate permutations between \(H_0, H\), and unit-preserving. There are depth-\(k\) vanes \(V_k\), checked at level \(k\), whose stages tend to infinity and whose radii equal \(r_k\); for each \(d\), eventually their full lift tuples through degree \(k\) converge along \(\mathcal{L}\) to \(Y_k\), with \(U_k (d) = \pi_d \circ Y_{k, d}\).

Status: theorem conditional on the stated inputs. III.2 supplies the uniform-band property; VI.2 supplies the dimension cap from IV.1's corrected rank widths in the construction used by VII.2. Lean: pays_sky.

Proposition VII.6.2 (checked vanes). Assume (S), \(z_d \geqslant c_i\) for \(i \leqslant d\), and \(\text{Fire}_d (z_d)\) for every \(d\). Retain the ordered and generic point limits, smoothness of \(S\), and \(H_0\); the full hierarchy \(H\) is unnecessary. For every positive null sequence \(s\), there exist strictly increasing \(p : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\), with \(s \circ p\) strictly decreasing, and depth-\(d\) vanes \(V_d\) of stage \(\geqslant d\), checked at level \(d\), with radius \(s_{p (d)}\).

Status: theorem. Lean: wakesSky.

Proposition VII.6.3 (moving-band chessboard bound). Assume (S). Fix \(d \in \mathbb{N}\), \(z \geqslant c_i\) for \(i \leqslant d\), Taylor order \(\Delta \in \mathbb{N}\), real exponent \(e\), real band ceiling \(u_0\), and degree-\(\leqslant d\) stage packages \(P_k\) with order \(\Delta\), exponent \(e\) and bands \(b_k \leqslant u_0\). Fix \(n, m \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(n \geqslant 2\), \(m + 1 \leqslant n\), labels \(g_i \in \text{Label}_{d_i}\) with \(d_i \leqslant d\) for \(0 \leqslant i \leqslant m\), and compact \(K \subset \text{Conf}_{m + 1}\). For all sufficiently large \(k\), take any:

  • stage-\(k\) cost record for \(P_k\) with exponent \(- 3\), mark exponent \(z\) and arity bound \(n\);

  • cutoff \(N > k\), period \(p\), and compatible stage-\(k\) RG representative along the same history;

  • configuration whose stage-\(k\) rounding, in units of \(\ell (b_k, k)\), lies in \(K\) and fits the stopping stage;

  • order-\(\Delta\) polynomial lifts \(T_i\) for that representative and labels \(f_i \in \text{Label}_{d_i}\) whose images under \(T_i\) and the coupling dilation equal \(g_i\) weighted by \(\ell (b_k, k) / a_k\).

For every nonempty \(s \subset \{0, \ldots, m\}\),

\(\displaystyle \rho_{\mathcal{T}} (\Phi_s) \leqslant \mu_k^{- 1 / (16 n)}, \qquad \Phi_s = [\eta_s] \left( e^{- D} \prod_{i = 0}^m (1 + \eta_i O_i) \right) .\)

Here the commuting markers satisfy \(\eta_i^2 = 0\), \(\eta_s = \prod_{i \in s} \eta_i\), \(O_i\) is the probe labelled \(f_i\), and \(D\) is the free cumulant polynomial of \(g_i\) at the rounded rescaled points. The exponential is finite. The root uses coupling \(\beta_{N, p}\) and width-stage cells. The threshold depends on \((d, z, \Delta, e, u_0, g, K, n)\), with band dependence only through \(u_0\).

Status: theorem. Lean: earningsSky.

Proposition VII.6.4 (fixed-field supply). Let \(Y\) be a final record for \((C, F, S)\), \(\varphi\) a filter finer than \(\Phi_F\), \(d \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(d \leqslant 3\), and \(x \in \text{Label}_d\). There is \(p \in \mathbb{N}\) such that, for all large \(k\), there are \(y \in \text{Label}_d\) and cutoff labels \(q\) with \(\pi_d y = \pi_d x\), \(q \to y\) along \(\varphi\), and \(\text{quota}_k^{N, p'} (q_{N, p'}) \leqslant \hat{\beta}_k^p\) eventually along \(\varphi\). One can take \(p = 1\) and \(q\) constant.

Status: theorem. Lean: inletFirst.

Proposition VII.6.5 (degrees below four). For every \(d \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(d < 4\), \(\dim \mathcal{F}^{\text{free}}_d = 1\). If \(Y\) is a final record for \((C, F, S)\), every \(u \in \mathcal{F}^S_d\) equals \(t\boldsymbol{1}_d\) for some \(t \in \mathbb{C}\).

Status: theorem. Lean: dim_before, first_grade.

3. Strategy

Varying the reading band over a unit interval makes successive reading intervals overlap, so they cover every sufficiently small radius. Uniform stage estimates across one block-factor change then permit exact prescribed radii. Chessboard comparison and two auxiliary bands control coincidence strips and Riemann sums; diagonal selection and ultrafilter limits produce approaching maps. Eventual injectivity and the dimension cap make them isomorphisms. Low-degree supply follows separately from the comparator and unit coordinates.

4. Steps

  1. Dynamic stopping windows allow two neighbouring price indices. Under the block-factor bound supplied by poised stages, continuity gives overlapping reading intervals and exact radii. Reading intervals.

  2. Uniform probe and lift bounds propagate source expansions to the width stage; affordability there gives VII.6.3. Root estimate.

  3. Two auxiliary bands make the strip and interpolation errors vanish; station admissibility follows. Balanced estimates.

  4. Diagonal choice gives checked vanes; bounded lifts converge along the common ultrafilter. Projection preserves symmetry and the unit; eventual injectivity plus the dimension cap gives bijectivity. Lift limits, isomorphisms.

  5. Below degree four, the comparator transfers one-dimensionality. Constant unit representatives have quota \(|t| a_k^d \leqslant \hat{\beta}_k\) eventually. Unit supply.

5. Where the difficulty lies

The stage threshold must be uniform over the entire band interval. Two neighbouring price indices replace constant-factor plateaus. The dimension cap supplies surjectivity; weak convergence alone does not give the downstream trace argument. Scaling compatibility across degrees and stress-tensor normalization are not asserted.

6. Notation

\(a_k \downarrow 0\) is the stage spacing, with block factors \(a_k / a_{k + 1} \geqslant 2\); \(\hat{\beta}_k = \bar{\beta} + c_G \log a_k^{- 1}\), \(c_G > 0\); \(\mu_k = \exp ((\log \log \hat{\beta}_k)^2)\); \(\ell (b, k) = a_k \mu_k^b\). Quota means stage-coordinate coefficient size; \(\text{Conf}_n\) is the configuration space of distinct points.

The coupling dilation multiplies a frame vector with \(q\) curvature factors by \(s_{k, N, p}^q\), where \(s_{k, N, p} = \beta_{N, p ; k}^{- 1 / 2} / (\hat{\beta}_k^{- 1 / 2} \mu_k)\) and \(\beta_{N, p ; k}\) is the actual stage coupling. Position weighting multiplies an engineering-degree-\(h\) frame vector by the corresponding scale to power \(h\).

A degree-\(D\) package has band \(b \geqslant D + 6\), Taylor order \(\theta \geqslant D\), exponent \(e\) and price-index cost parameters with credit at least \(bD + D + 2\). Poised stages have reached the reference stages, both neighbouring block factors are at most \(\mu_k\), and their tables preserve the unit, reality and signed covariance, with relative coordinate errors at most \(1 / 2\) and \(\mu_k^{- 1 / 2}\). Each package has a cost record with exponent \(- 3\), the specified mark and arity bounds, and compatible representatives and polynomial lifts realizing those tables for every \(N > k, p\).

The uniform-band property \(\text{Fire}_d (z)\) requires three package families with bands \(b_i + s\), \(s \in [0, 1]\), fixed Taylor orders and exponents, and \(\kappa < 0\) satisfying

\(\displaystyle b_1 + 6 < b_0, \quad b_2 + 5 < b_0 + \kappa, \quad (b_0 - b_1) d (d + 2) + \kappa < 0, \quad (b_0 - b_2) d (d + 2) - b_0 - \kappa < 0.\)

One threshold makes every triple poised for every \(s \in [0, 1]\), with arity bound \(d + 2\) and mark exponent \(z\).

A depth-\(D\) vane records stage \(k\), band \(b\), strip exponent and complex-linear cutoff tables on labels through degree \(D\). Checked at level \(j\) requires \(5 < b \leqslant \log \log \hat{\beta}_k\), reference stages reached through degree \(D\), and the table bounds above for every \(N > k, p\). All generic rescaled lattice pairs of norm at most \(100\) fit; every degree-\(\leqslant D\) free basis pair has recentred chessboard roots at most \(1 / (j + 1)\) on the fitted radius-\(100\) window. Its station is admissible through arity \(j\), at window radius \(j + 1\) and tolerance \(1 / (j + 1)\).
