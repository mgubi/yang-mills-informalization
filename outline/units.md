# The detailed map: the units of the proof

The Lean proof, restricted to the 413,000 lines that the main theorem uses, divided into units: mathematically coherent pieces, grouped by the eight parts of the proof. Each unit has its own page with its Lean source (linked), the units it builds on, and its key statements in plain language. The outline of each unit (its propositions, strategy and steps) is written by one AI model, checked against the Lean and rewritten by a second, and edited by a third; it is published on the unit's page as soon as it has been through all three rounds. For the logic of the whole proof, see the [overview](README.md).
