# Changes

These notes record publications and corrections that affect readers' understanding of the result. The [unit index](outline/units.md) records current outline statuses. All Lean references concern source commit [`3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464`](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/tree/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464).

## 2026-11-12

- **Second draft of the manuscript (265 pages).** Revised after two expert human reviews, six reviewer reports and a comparison with a collaborator's draft. Part I is rebuilt: the result in plain words, the definitions needed to state the theorem, **Theorem B as the main theorem** (p. 14), a strategy section (why the ultraviolet induction closes, the order of limits, how rotations are recovered, the trace law, the operator product expansion), then the principal statements with proof ideas. The literal formal statement (Theorem A) and the formal-interface conventions are in Section 8, at the end of Part I. Part II gained worked examples (the curvature square; two-field OPE; nested collisions), extracted lemmas for the decisive steps, and figures; the proof of the one-mark step (Lemma 14.5 of the first draft) was corrected (wrapping terms stay in the regular remainder, as in the Lean). Every changed statement was checked against the Lean; the [correspondence table](manuscript/correspondence.tsv) is published.

## 2026-11-11

- **Manuscript revised after an independent review.** Three referees read the whole first draft; every suggestion was adjudicated against the text and the Lean before being applied, and the changes were checked (in the ultraviolet sections this filled gaps from the Lean: a single-valued step map, convergence of the free forms, the leading map of the marked expansion, the block-to-fine comparison, the torus localization). Three rounds of cross-section checks matched every citation between sections with the statement it cites; a typographic pass covered every page. 241 pages.
- **First draft of the manuscript.** The [manuscript](manuscript/) now has a complete first draft ([PDF](manuscript/main.pdf), 226 pages): Part I (theorem, principal statements, deduction) and Part II (all proofs, Sections 10–19), each section through its review cycle. Revisions continue.

Part I of the manuscript has completed its review cycle; the first two Part II sections (C and F) are drafted and under re-check.
- **The manuscript joins this repository.** It is now in [manuscript/](manuscript/), as level 5, the full proof (under construction); the separate repository is archived.
- **Outlines condensed.** Each unit page now shows an outline of three to ten pages, condensed from the unit's detailed notes and checked against them sentence by sentence; the full notes are on a separate page per unit and in their own [PDF](pdf/yang-mills-detailed-notes.pdf).
- **All unit outlines edited; one PDF.** All thirty-two outlines have been through the three review rounds. The summary, the architecture and the outlines are available as one [PDF](pdf/yang-mills-outline.pdf).
- **Where the ideas come from.** A [provenance analysis](provenance.md): which parts of the proof follow Balaban's programme, which follow OpenAI's 2026 papers on two-dimensional sigma models, which are standard, and which appear new.
- **Reading aids.** A [FAQ](faq.md); a guide in the [statement](statement.md) from each clause of the theorem to the units that explain it; [notes on the data tables](data/README.md); a short entrance and navigation on every unit page, with code dependencies labelled as such.

It distinguishes the assessment document's date, 10 November, from publication with the source on 11 November.
